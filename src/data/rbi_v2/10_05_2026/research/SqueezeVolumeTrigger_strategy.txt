STRATEGY_NAME: SqueezeVolumeTrigger

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept**
This strategy captures explosive directional moves that follow periods of low volatility. When Bollinger Band width contracts to a 20-day low, it signals a coiled market (the "squeeze"). A volume spike above the 50-day average acts as the ignition trigger, confirming that a breakout is underway. Direction is determined by price breaking out of the band envelope.

**Key Strategy Components**

1. **Volatility Contraction Detection**
   - Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA with 2 standard deviations
   - BB Width = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band
   - Squeeze condition: Current BB Width ≤ lowest BB Width over the prior 20 days

2. **Volume Confirmation**
   - Volume MA: 50-period simple moving average of volume
   - Trigger condition: Current volume > 50-day average volume (ideally >1.5x for stronger signal)

3. **Directional Bias**
   - Long bias: Price closes above the upper Bollinger Band during the squeeze trigger
   - Short bias: Price closes below the lower Bollinger Band during the squeeze trigger

**Entry Rules**

*Long Entry:*
- BB Width hits 20-day low (squeeze present)
- Current volume > 50-day volume average
- Close > Upper Bollinger Band
- Enter at next bar open or on close confirmation

*Short Entry:*
- BB Width hits 20-day low (squeeze present)
- Current volume > 50-day volume average
- Close < Lower Bollinger Band
- Enter at next bar open or on close confirmation

**Exit Rules**

1. **Primary Exit — Channel Expansion**
   - Exit when BB Width expands back above its 20-day average OR
   - Exit when BB Width exceeds a predefined expansion threshold (e.g., 1.5x the squeeze low)

2. **Stop Loss — 2x ATR**
   - Initial stop: 2 × ATR(14) from entry price
   - Long stop: Entry − (2 × ATR)
   - Short stop: Entry + (2 × ATR)
   - Trailing option: Trail stop at 2 × ATR from highest high (long) or lowest low (short)

3. **Optional Profit Target**
   - Take partial profits at 1.5R, let remainder run with trailing ATR stop

**Risk Management**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade
  - Position size = (Account Risk $) / (2 × ATR)
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** Limit to 2–3 to avoid correlated volatility events
- **Avoid Choppy Regimes:** Skip signals if BB Width is already above its 50-day average (no true squeeze)
- **Time Stop:** Exit if no expansion occurs within 5–10 bars (squeeze failed)
- **Slippage Buffer:** Use limit orders on entry where possible; breakouts can gap

**Required Indicators**

| Indicator | Parameters | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Bollinger Bands | 20-period, 2 SD | Detect squeeze & breakout |
| BB Width | (Upper−Lower)/Middle | Measure contraction/expansion |
| Volume MA | 50-period SMA | Confirm breakout participation |
| ATR | 14-period | Stop placement & sizing |
| Volume | Raw | Spike detection |

**Trade Logic Flow**
1. Scan for BB Width at 20-day low → squeeze flag ON
2. Check volume > 50-day average → confirmation
3. Check close vs. bands → determine long/short
4. Enter with 2×ATR stop
5. Exit on width expansion or stop hit
6. Re-arm scan after position closes

**Best Market Conditions**
- Works best on liquid instruments (large-cap equities, index futures, FX majors)
- Avoid during low-liquidity sessions (overnight, holidays)
- Strongest performance after prolonged consolidation phases
- Less effective in strongly trending markets with no contraction phases
