STRATEGY_NAME: VortexConvergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy fuses two complementary intraday tools: VWAP slope behavior and the Vortex Indicator (VI). The core thesis is that when VWAP slope and Vortex crossover signals converge, they confirm a genuine intraday breakout; when they diverge, they warn of a false move. Entries are taken only on confluence, while exits are governed by opposing Vortex crossovers or price reverting beyond dynamic VWAP bands.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS

A. VWAP Slope Analysis
- Compute session VWAP (anchored to market open, reset daily).
- Calculate slope over a short lookback (e.g., 5–10 bars) using linear regression or simple delta of VWAP.
- Classify slope as:
  - Steep Up: slope > +threshold (e.g., +0.02% per bar)
  - Steep Down: slope < -threshold
  - Flat: within threshold band
- Slope convergence = VWAP slope direction agrees with Vortex crossover direction.
- Slope divergence = VWAP slope opposes Vortex crossover direction.

B. Vortex Indicator
- VI+ = |High - Prior Low| summed over N periods
- VI- = |Low - Prior High| summed over N periods
- Standard N = 14 (intraday: 5–14 depending on timeframe).
- Bullish crossover: VI+ crosses above VI-
- Bearish crossover: VI- crosses above VI+

C. Dynamic VWAP Bands
- Upper Band = VWAP + (k × ATR) or VWAP + (k × rolling std dev of price-VWAP)
- Lower Band = VWAP - (k × same measure)
- k typically 1.5–2.5
- Bands expand/contract with volatility, making them "dynamic."

D. Confluence Filter
- Long setup: Bullish Vortex crossover AND VWAP slope Steep Up (convergence)
- Short setup: Bearish Vortex crossover AND VWAP slope Steep Down (convergence)
- If crossover occurs but VWAP slope is Flat or opposing (divergence), skip the trade.

2. ENTRY RULES

Long Entry:
- VI+ crosses above VI- on the current bar
- VWAP slope is Steep Up over the lookback
- Price is above VWAP (confirms bullish positioning)
- Optional: price breaks above recent consolidation high or upper VWAP band
- Enter at close of signal bar or on next bar open

Short Entry:
- VI- crosses above VI+ on the current bar
- VWAP slope is Steep Down
- Price is below VWAP
- Optional: price breaks below recent consolidation low or lower VWAP band
- Enter at close of signal bar or next bar open

No-Trade Conditions:
- Vortex crossover with Flat or opposing VWAP slope (divergence)
- Price already extended beyond outer VWAP band (avoid chasing)
- Low-volume periods (e.g., lunch hour) unless strategy is explicitly volume-filtered

3. EXIT RULES

Primary Exit (Vortex Reversal):
- Long exit: VI- crosses above VI+ (opposing Vortex signal)
- Short exit: VI+ crosses above VI-

Secondary Exit (VWAP Band Reversion):
- Long exit: price closes below VWAP (mean reversion) or below lower band
- Short exit: price closes above VWAP or above upper band
- This acts as a "reverting beyond dynamic VWAP bands" stop/target

Tertiary Exit (Time/Profit):
- End-of-day flat (intraday only)
- Optional profit target at 1.5R–2R
- Optional trailing stop using VWAP as trail line

4. RISK MANAGEMENT

- Position sizing: risk 0.5%–1% of account per trade
- Stop-loss for longs: below signal bar low, below VWAP, or below lower band (whichever is tighter but logical)
- Stop-loss for shorts: above signal bar high, above VWAP, or above upper band
- Max daily loss limit: 2%–3% of account; stop trading after hit
- Max concurrent trades: 1–2 to avoid correlated exposure
- Avoid trading during major news releases unless volatility filter is satisfied
- Use ATR-based stops to adapt to intraday volatility regimes

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS

- VWAP (session-anchored, reset daily)
- VWAP slope (linear regression or delta over N bars)
- Vortex Indicator (VI+, VI-, period 5–14)
- ATR or rolling standard deviation (for dynamic band width)
- Optional: volume filter, time-of-day filter, consolidation range detector

6. EXECUTION NOTES

- Best timeframes: 1-min, 5-min, or 15-min charts
- Best instruments: liquid intraday markets (index futures, large-cap equities, major FX pairs)
- Convergence trades tend to have higher win rate; divergence signals are best used as a filter to avoid false breakouts
- Backtest across trending vs. ranging sessions — this strategy performs best in trending intraday regimes where VWAP slope is persistent
- Consider logging convergence vs. divergence outcomes to refine slope threshold and Vortex period
