STRATEGY_NAME: SqueezeFlow Reversal

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines volatility compression detection (Bollinger Bandwidth at 20-day lows) with money flow confirmation (Chaikin Money Flow crossing above zero) to identify high-probability long entries following periods of consolidation. The core thesis is that a volatility squeeze preceded by positive money flow signals institutional accumulation before an explosive breakout.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Volatility Squeeze Detection (Primary Filter)**
   - Bollinger Bandwidth = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band
   - Calculate Bandwidth using standard Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations)
   - Entry condition requires current Bandwidth ≤ lowest Bandwidth value of the prior 20 days
   - This identifies periods of extreme consolidation where a breakout is statistically imminent

2. **Money Flow Confirmation (Trigger)**
   - Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) over 20 periods
   - CMF = Σ[(Close − Low) − (High − Close)] / (High − Low) × Volume, summed over 20 periods, divided by 20-period volume sum
   - Entry trigger: CMF crosses above zero (from negative or flat territory)
   - Positive CMF during a squeeze indicates buying pressure accumulating despite low volatility

**Entry Rules:**
- **Long entry** when BOTH conditions are met on the same bar or within a 2–3 bar window:
  - Bollinger Bandwidth is at or below its 20-day low
  - CMF crosses above the zero line
- Enter at next bar open or on close of the signal bar
- Optional: require CMF to have been negative in the prior 1–3 bars to ensure a genuine "cross"

**Exit Rules:**
- **Trailing stop:** 5% below the highest close (or highest high) since entry
- **Volatility expansion exit:** Close the position when Bandwidth exceeds its 10-day simple moving average
- Whichever exit triggers first — the trailing stop or the Bandwidth expansion — closes the trade
- Consider a hard time-stop (e.g., 20–30 bars) if neither exit triggers

**Risk Management:**
- Position sizing based on the 5% trailing stop distance — risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade
- Avoid entries during earnings releases or major scheduled news events
- Skip signals if Bandwidth is at a 20-day low but the broader market/trend filter (e.g., price below 200-period SMA) is bearish
- Optional trend filter: only take longs if price is above the 200-period SMA to align with higher-timeframe direction

**Required Indicators:**
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) — for Bandwidth calculation
- Bollinger Bandwidth (derived from above)
- 20-day lowest Bandwidth value (rolling minimum)
- 10-day SMA of Bandwidth (for exit)
- Chaikin Money Flow (20-period)
- Optional: 200-period SMA (trend filter)
- Trailing stop tracker (5% from peak)

**Rationale:**
The squeeze represents equilibrium — buyers and sellers in balance with declining volatility. A CMF cross above zero during this compression reveals that accumulation is quietly occurring. When the squeeze inevitably resolves, this latent demand typically produces a sharp upside breakout. The Bandwidth-expansion exit captures the bulk of the volatility move, while the 5% trailing stop protects against false breakouts that reverse sharply.
