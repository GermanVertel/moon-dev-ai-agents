STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityRangeBreaker

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy captures momentum-driven price expansion when the market breaks out of a defined consolidation range, using volatility-adjusted filters to avoid false breakouts. Below is a complete breakdown.

---

## 1. Core Concept

The strategy identifies periods of low volatility (range-bound consolidation) and trades the subsequent expansion. The key innovation is that the breakout levels are **dynamically recalculated** based on recent volatility, rather than fixed or purely time-based levels. This adapts to changing market conditions and filters out noise during choppy markets.

---

## 2. Key Strategy Components

### A. Range Detection
- Use a lookback period (e.g., 20 bars) to identify a consolidation phase.
- Define the range high and low as the highest high and lowest low over the lookback window.
- Confirm consolidation when the range width (high − low) is below a volatility threshold (e.g., ATR-based or percentile-based).

### B. Dynamic Breakout Levels
- Instead of fixed highs/lows, compute breakout triggers using:
  - **Upper Trigger** = Range High + (k × ATR) — a buffer to avoid wick noise.
  - **Lower Trigger** = Range Low − (k × ATR).
- `k` is a tunable multiplier (commonly 0.25–0.5).
- ATR period typically 14; range lookback 20.

### C. Volatility Filter
- Only take breakouts when ATR is expanding (current ATR > ATR moving average) or when Bollinger Band width is at a low percentile (squeeze).
- This ensures you're trading expansion after contraction, not random noise.

### D. Trend Confirmation (Optional but recommended)
- Use a higher-timeframe EMA (e.g., 50-period) to bias direction:
  - Long breakouts only if price > EMA50.
  - Short breakouts only if price < EMA50.

---

## 3. Entry Rules

**Long Entry:**
1. Price closes above Upper Trigger.
2. ATR is expanding or BB width is in the lowest 20th percentile of the last 100 bars.
3. (Optional) Price above EMA50 on higher timeframe.
4. Enter at close of breakout bar or on next bar open.

**Short Entry:**
1. Price closes below Lower Trigger.
2. Same volatility and trend filters apply (inverse).
3. Enter at close or next bar open.

---

## 4. Exit Rules

**Profit Target:**
- Take profit at 1.5×–2× the ATR at entry, or at the next major resistance/support level.

**Stop Loss:**
- Place stop at the opposite side of the range (Range Low for longs, Range High for shorts) minus/plus a small ATR buffer.
- Alternatively, use a fixed ATR-based stop (e.g., 1.5× ATR).

**Trailing Stop (optional):**
- Trail using a Chandelier Exit or ATR trailing stop to capture extended trends.

**Time-Based Exit:**
- Exit if price re-enters the range and closes inside for 2 consecutive bars (failed breakout).

---

## 5. Risk Management

- **Position Sizing:** Risk 0.5%–1% of account equity per trade. Size = (Account × Risk%) / (Entry − Stop).
- **Max Concurrent Trades:** Limit to 1–2 to avoid correlated exposure.
- **Daily Loss Limit:** Stop trading after 2× the per-trade risk is lost in a day.
- **Avoid News Events:** Skip trades within 15 minutes of high-impact economic releases.
- **False Breakout Guard:** Require a close beyond the trigger, not just an intrabar wick.

---

## 6. Required Indicators

| Indicator | Purpose | Typical Settings |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| ATR | Volatility measurement & breakout buffer | 14 |
| Highest High / Lowest Low | Range boundaries | 20 bars |
| Bollinger Bands | Squeeze detection | 20, 2.0 |
| EMA | Trend bias filter | 50 (higher TF) |
| Volume (optional) | Confirm breakout strength | 20-period avg |

---

## 7. Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths:**
- Adapts to changing volatility regimes.
- Filters false breakouts via ATR buffer and volatility condition.
- Clear, rule-based entries/exits.

**Weaknesses:**
- Can miss fast breakouts if buffer is too wide.
- Underperforms in strongly trending markets without pullbacks.
- Requires parameter tuning across instruments/timeframes.

---

## 8. Recommended Markets & Timeframes

- **Markets:** Forex majors, index futures (ES, NQ), liquid crypto (BTC, ETH).
- **Timeframes:** 15m, 1H, 4H for swing/intraday.
- **Avoid:** Illiquid instruments and low-volume sessions.

---

**Summary:** VolatilityRangeBreaker combines range detection, dynamic ATR-based breakout triggers, and volatility expansion filters to trade high-probability breakouts while minimizing whipsaws. Proper risk management and trend bias confirmation are essential for consistent performance.
