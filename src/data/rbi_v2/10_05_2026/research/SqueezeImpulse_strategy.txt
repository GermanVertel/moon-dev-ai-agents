STRATEGY_NAME: SqueezeImpulse

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines volatility compression detection with trend-strength confirmation to trade high-probability breakouts. The core premise is that a Bollinger Band squeeze (low volatility) followed by an ADX surge above 30 signals a genuine trend ignition rather than a false breakout. The 161.8% Fibonacci extension provides a mathematically derived profit target based on the contraction range.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Volatility Compression Filter: Bollinger Band Width (BBW) must contract below 2% of current price, indicating a squeeze.
- Trend Strength Confirmation: ADX must cross above 30, confirming a strong directional move is developing.
- Directional Bias: Determined by price position relative to the middle Bollinger Band (20-period SMA) and/or DI+ vs DI- crossover.
- Momentum Trigger: Breakout above upper band (long) or below lower band (short) after squeeze release.
- Fibonacci Target: 161.8% extension of the contraction range measured from the breakout point.

2. ENTRY RULES
Long Entry:
- BBW < 2% of price (squeeze condition met).
- ADX crosses above 30 (from below).
- DI+ > DI- (bullish directional bias).
- Price closes above the upper Bollinger Band.
- Enter at next candle open or on breakout candle close.

Short Entry:
- BBW < 2% of price (squeeze condition met).
- ADX crosses above 30 (from below).
- DI- > DI+ (bearish directional bias).
- Price closes below the lower Bollinger Band.
- Enter at next candle open or on breakout candle close.

3. EXIT RULES
- Primary Target: 161.8% Fibonacci extension of the contraction range.
  - Range = (Squeeze High - Squeeze Low) during the BBW < 2% period.
  - Long target = Breakout Price + (Range × 1.618).
  - Short target = Breakout Price - (Range × 1.618).
- Trailing Stop: Use the middle Bollinger Band (20 SMA) as a trailing stop once price moves 1× the range in favor.
- Time Stop: Exit if ADX falls back below 25 or BBW expands beyond 6% of price without hitting target.
- Hard Stop: Opposite Bollinger Band or 1× ATR(14) from entry, whichever is tighter.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position Sizing: Risk no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade.
- Stop Loss: Place initial stop at the opposite Bollinger Band or 1.5× ATR(14), whichever is closer to entry.
- Risk-Reward: Minimum 1:2 R:R required before entry; skip trades where the 161.8% target is less than 2× the stop distance.
- Max Concurrent Trades: Limit to 2-3 open positions to avoid correlated exposure.
- Avoid trading during major news events (FOMC, NFP, CPI) where slippage risk is elevated.
- Trail stops to breakeven once price reaches 1× the contraction range in profit.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 standard deviations) — for squeeze detection and band breakouts.
- Bollinger Band Width (BBW) — (Upper Band - Lower Band) / Middle Band × 100, must be < 2.
- ADX (14-period) with DI+ and DI- lines — trend strength and directional bias.
- ATR (14-period) — for stop-loss placement and volatility normalization.
- Fibonacci Extension Tool — for calculating 161.8% target from the contraction range.
- Optional: Volume indicator to confirm breakout strength (volume > 1.5× 20-period average).

6. TIMEFRAME & MARKET CONDITIONS
- Best suited for 15-minute to 4-hour charts on liquid instruments (FX majors, index futures, large-cap equities).
- Works optimally in trending markets; avoid ranging/choppy conditions where ADX oscillates around 20-25.
- Squeeze duration should be at least 10-15 candles for a meaningful contraction range.

This squeeze-then-impulse approach filters out low-conviction breakouts by requiring both volatility compression and trend-strength confirmation, while the Fibonacci extension provides a disciplined, objective profit target.
