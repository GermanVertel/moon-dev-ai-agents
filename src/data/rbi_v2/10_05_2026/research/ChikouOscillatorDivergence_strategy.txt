STRATEGY_NAME: Chikou Oscillator Divergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines three indicators into a single custom oscillator to trade trend continuations and reversals with divergence confirmation.

**Core Components:**

1. **Custom Oscillator Construction:**
   - Chikou Span component: Compare current close to price N periods ago (typically 26), normalized to a percentage deviation
   - MACD histogram component: Standard MACD (12, 26, 9) histogram value, normalized by ATR or price
   - Stochastic component: %K and %D (14, 3, 3), with %K used as the oscillator input
   - Weighted blend: e.g., 40% Chikou deviation + 30% MACD histogram + 30% Stochastic (adjust based on backtest)
   - Oscillator ranges roughly -100 to +100 after normalization

2. **Entry Rules (Long):**
   - Price is in an established uptrend (price above 50 EMA, or Chikou Span above price 26 periods back)
   - Custom oscillator crosses above zero OR bullish divergence forms (price makes lower low, oscillator makes higher low)
   - MACD histogram confirms by turning positive or increasing
   - Stochastic %K crosses above %D below the 30 level
   - Enter on candle close confirming all three conditions

3. **Entry Rules (Short):**
   - Price in established downtrend (price below 50 EMA, Chikou below price 26 periods back)
   - Custom oscillator crosses below zero OR bearish divergence (price higher high, oscillator lower high)
   - MACD histogram confirms by turning negative or decreasing
   - Stochastic %K crosses below %D above the 70 level
   - Enter on candle close confirming all three conditions

4. **Exit Rules:**
   - **Take-Profit:** 2-3% above entry (long) or below entry (short)
   - **Stop-Loss:** 2-3% below entry (long) or above entry (short)
   - **Signal Exit:** Close position when oscillator diverges from price in the opposite direction of the trade (e.g., for longs, price makes higher high but oscillator makes lower high = bearish divergence → exit)
   - **Confirmation Exit:** Close when MACD histogram flips against position AND Stochastic crosses opposite direction

5. **Risk Management:**
   - Risk per trade: 1-2% of account equity
   - Position sizing: (Account Risk $) / (Entry - Stop Distance)
   - Trailing stop optional: move stop to breakeven once +1.5% profit reached
   - Max concurrent positions: 2-3 to limit correlated exposure
   - Avoid trading during major news events (NFP, FOMC) due to oscillator whipsaw

6. **Required Indicators:**
   - Chikou Span (Ichimoku component, 26-period shift)
   - MACD (12, 26, 9) histogram
   - Stochastic Oscillator (14, 3, 3)
   - 50 EMA for trend filter
   - ATR (14) for normalization and volatility-based sizing

7. **Timeframe & Markets:**
   - Best on 1H, 4H, or Daily charts
   - Works on trending instruments: FX majors, index futures, large-cap crypto
   - Avoid ranging/choppy markets — use ADX > 20 as an additional filter

8. **Divergence Detection Logic:**
   - Scan last 20-30 bars for swing highs/lows in price
   - Compare corresponding oscillator values at those swings
   - Regular divergence = reversal signal; Hidden divergence = continuation signal
   - Require at least 2 swing points to confirm divergence validity

**Key Edge:** The triple-confirmation (Chikou trend context + MACD momentum + Stochastic timing) reduces false signals from any single indicator, while the divergence exit captures trend exhaustion before the fixed take-profit is hit.
