STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityBand Reversal

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy trades VIX futures by identifying extreme volatility pricing dislocations through the VIX/VVIX ratio, confirmed by SPX options volume. It targets volatility regime reversals — fading stretched conditions when the ratio breaks its statistical envelope.

1. CORE STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Primary Signal: VIX/VVIX ratio (VIX = 30-day implied vol of SPX; VVIX = implied vol of VIX options). This ratio measures how "expensive" volatility-of-volatility is relative to spot volatility.
- Statistical Envelope: 30-day Bollinger Bands on the VIX/VVIX ratio (20-period SMA ± 2 standard deviations). Bands adapt to recent regime, so breakouts flag genuine dislocations rather than static thresholds.
- Confirmation Filter: SPX options activity (volume/OI skew, put/call volume ratio, or total SPX options volume vs. 20-day average) to validate that the ratio move reflects real positioning, not a thin/liquidity artifact.
- Instrument: VIX futures (front or second month), sized for roll and contango/backwardation effects.

2. ENTRY RULES
- SHORT VOLATILITY ENTRY (fade a vol spike): VIX/VVIX ratio closes ABOVE the upper Bollinger Band (ratio stretched high — VIX rich vs. VVIX) AND SPX options volume confirms elevated hedging/panic (e.g., SPX put volume > 1.5x 20-day average). Enter SHORT VIX futures, betting the ratio mean-reverts lower.
- LONG VOLATILITY ENTRY (fade complacency): VIX/VVIX ratio closes BELOW the lower Bollinger Band (ratio compressed — VIX cheap vs. VVIX) AND SPX options volume is subdued (e.g., total volume < 0.75x 20-day average), signaling complacency. Enter LONG VIX futures.
- Wait for the daily close to confirm the band break — avoid intraday whipsaws.

3. EXIT RULES
- Profit target: ratio reverts to the 20-period SMA (middle band).
- Stop loss: ratio moves 1.5x the band width beyond entry, or a fixed % adverse move in the VIX futures price.
- Time stop: exit after N days (e.g., 5–10) if neither target nor stop is hit, to avoid decay/roll drag.
- Optional trailing stop once the ratio crosses back inside the band.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position sizing: risk fixed % of equity per trade (e.g., 1–2%); VIX futures are volatile, so size conservatively.
- Respect futures term structure: avoid holding long VIX futures deep into contango (roll cost); prefer shorter holding periods.
- Cap concurrent exposure; do not stack correlated vol trades.
- Account for VIX futures contract multiplier ($1000) and margin requirements.
- Consider a volatility filter: skip signals when VVIX itself is at an extreme (regime too unstable).

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS / DATA
- VIX index level (daily close).
- VVIX index level (daily close) — compute VIX/VVIX ratio.
- 20-period SMA and 2x standard deviation bands (30-day lookback) on the ratio.
- SPX options volume (total and/or put/call) with 20-day average for confirmation.
- VIX futures front/second-month prices and term structure (contango/backwardation).

6. NOTES / EDGE
- The VIX/VVIX ratio is a mean-reverting relative-value measure; Bollinger breakouts isolate statistically rare extremes.
- SPX options volume confirmation reduces false signals from illiquid VVIX prints.
- Best suited to short holding periods given futures roll dynamics; backtest across high- and low-vol regimes before live deployment.
