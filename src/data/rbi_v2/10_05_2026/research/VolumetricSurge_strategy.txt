STRATEGY_NAME: VolumetricSurge

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Bollinger Band breakouts with volume confirmation to capture high-momentum upside moves, using RSI as a trailing exit signal.

**Core Concept:**
A close above the upper Bollinger Band signals price expansion beyond normal statistical range. However, breakouts without volume confirmation often fail. This strategy requires a simultaneous volume spike (2x the 20-day average) to validate genuine institutional participation before entering long.

**Indicators Required:**
1. Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations) — upper band, middle band, lower band
2. Volume with 20-day Simple Moving Average (SMA) of volume
3. RSI (14-period, standard setting)

**Entry Rules (Long Only):**
- Price closes ABOVE the upper Bollinger Band (not just wicks — use closing price)
- Current bar's volume ≥ 2.0 × 20-day average volume
- Both conditions must occur on the SAME bar (simultaneous confirmation)
- Optional filter: RSI < 70 at entry to avoid buying into already-overbought conditions

**Exit Rules:**
- Primary exit: RSI (14) crosses BELOW 70 (from above), signaling momentum exhaustion
- Alternative/backup exit: Price closes back below the middle Bollinger Band (20-SMA) — use this if RSI never reaches 70
- Hard stop-loss: Close below entry bar's low, or 2× ATR(14) below entry price

**Risk Management:**
- Position size: Risk no more than 1–2% of equity per trade
- Stop-loss placement: Below the breakout candle's low OR 2× ATR(14), whichever is tighter
- Trailing stop: Once RSI exceeds 80, trail stop at middle Bollinger Band
- Max holding period: 10–15 bars to avoid stagnation trades
- Avoid entries within 30 minutes of market open/close (for intraday) to reduce false volume spikes

**Trade Management Notes:**
- Volume spikes on breakouts often precede continuation runs but can also mark climax tops — the RSI exit protects against the latter
- If volume spike is >4x average, consider partial profit-taking (50%) at +2× ATR to lock gains
- Skip trades during low-liquidity sessions (holidays, pre-market) where volume averages are distorted
- Filter out earnings-gap days unless the strategy is intentionally event-driven

**Best Market Conditions:**
- Trending or momentum-driven markets (works poorly in choppy ranges)
- Liquid large-cap stocks, ETFs, or index futures where volume data is reliable
- Avoid during low-volatility consolidation regimes where Bollinger Bands are compressed

**Edge Case Handling:**
- If price gaps above upper band at open with no volume confirmation, wait for a subsequent bar to confirm
- If RSI is already below 70 at entry (rare), use middle-band close as primary exit instead
- Re-entry allowed only after RSI resets below 50 and a new breakout setup forms
