STRATEGY_NAME: CompressionFlip

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
The CompressionFlip strategy is a volatility-based mean-reversion/reversal system that exploits periods of extreme market compression (low volatility) to capture explosive directional moves when a trend filter confirms a flip. It combines Bollinger Band width percentile analysis with the Supertrend indicator for precise entry and exit timing.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Volatility Compression Detection: Bollinger Band Width (BBW) below its historical 10th percentile signals a "squeeze" — a low-volatility regime that often precedes a strong breakout/reversal.
- Directional Confirmation: Supertrend flip acts as the trigger, ensuring entry only when momentum aligns with the breakout direction.
- Dual Exit Framework: Opposing Supertrend crossover (trend-based exit) OR a 2x ATR trailing stop (volatility-based exit), whichever triggers first.

2. INDICATORS REQUIRED
- Bollinger Bands: Period = 20, Multiplier = 2 (standard). Compute BBW = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band.
- BBW Percentile: Lookback window = 100–252 bars. Compute the 10th percentile of BBW over the lookback. Condition: current BBW < 10th percentile.
- Supertrend: Period = 10, Multiplier = 3 (default). Provides trend direction and flip signals.
- ATR: Period = 14. Used for the 2x ATR trailing stop.

3. ENTRY RULES
Long Entry:
- Condition A: BBW is below the 10th percentile of its historical lookback (squeeze active).
- Condition B: Supertrend flips from bearish to bullish (close crosses above Supertrend line).
- Both conditions must be true within the same or adjacent bars (allow a 1–3 bar window for the squeeze to still be "active").
Short Entry:
- Condition A: BBW is below the 10th percentile (squeeze active).
- Condition B: Supertrend flips from bullish to bearish (close crosses below Supertrend line).

4. EXIT RULES
- Primary Exit (Trend): Exit long when Supertrend flips bearish; exit short when Supertrend flips bullish.
- Secondary Exit (Volatility): Apply a 2x ATR trailing stop from the highest high (long) or lowest low (short) since entry. Exit when price retraces by 2 × ATR.
- Whichever exit triggers first is executed. This protects against whipsaws during sudden volatility expansions.

5. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position Sizing: Risk 0.5–1% of equity per trade. Size = (Account Risk) / (Entry Price − Initial Stop).
- Initial Stop: Place at the 2x ATR trailing stop level from entry.
- Max Concurrent Positions: 1–2 to avoid overexposure during correlated squeezes.
- Squeeze Timeout: If no Supertrend flip occurs within 20 bars of the squeeze being detected, invalidate the setup.
- Volatility Filter: Avoid entries within 15 minutes of major news events (earnings, FOMC, CPI), as squeezes can resolve violently and unpredictably.

6. EXECUTION NOTES
- Best Timeframes: 1H, 4H, and Daily for cleaner Supertrend signals; 15m for intraday scalping with tighter ATR stops.
- Best Markets: Liquid instruments (major FX pairs, index futures, large-cap equities, BTC/ETH) where BBW percentiles are statistically meaningful.
- Confirmation Bias: Consider requiring RSI divergence or volume expansion on the breakout bar to filter false flips.
- Backtest Caution: The 10th percentile threshold should be walk-forward optimized; a fixed 10% may overfit to specific regimes.
