STRATEGY_NAME: VortexForce

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
The VortexForce strategy is a multi-indicator breakout system that combines trend-shift detection, volume momentum confirmation, and oscillator-based exit timing. It is designed to capture momentum-driven breakouts while avoiding false signals through layered confirmation.

---

**1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS**

- **Vortex Indicator (VI+ / VI−):** Primary trend-shift detector. A bullish shift occurs when VI+ crosses above VI−, and a bearish shift when VI− crosses above VI+.
- **Elder's Volume Force (EVF):** Volume-weighted momentum confirmation. Calculated as: `EVF = (Close − Close[1]) × Volume`, then typically smoothed with an EMA (e.g., 13-period) to filter noise.
- **KST Momentum Oscillator (3-day EMA):** Exit timing tool. The 3-day EMA of the KST (Know Sure Thing) oscillator serves as a fast reversal trigger.

---

**2. ENTRY RULES**

**Long Entry (Breakout Buy):**
- Vortex Indicator produces a bullish crossover: VI+ crosses above VI−.
- Elder's Volume Force (smoothed) is positive AND rising, confirming genuine volume-backed momentum.
- Price breaks above a recent swing high or consolidation range (breakout confirmation).
- Optional filter: Price above 20-period EMA to align with broader trend.

**Short Entry (Breakout Sell):**
- Vortex Indicator produces a bearish crossover: VI− crosses above VI+.
- Elder's Volume Force (smoothed) is negative AND falling, confirming selling pressure.
- Price breaks below a recent swing low or consolidation range.
- Optional filter: Price below 20-period EMA.

---

**3. EXIT RULES**

**Long Exit:**
- The 3-day EMA of the KST oscillator reverses downward (bearish cross of its signal or slope flip).
- Alternatively, exit on a Vortex bearish crossover as a hard stop.

**Short Exit:**
- The 3-day EMA of the KST oscillator reverses upward (bullish cross of its signal or slope flip).
- Alternatively, exit on a Vortex bullish crossover as a hard stop.

---

**4. RISK MANAGEMENT**

- **Stop Loss:** Place stop below the breakout candle's low (long) or above the breakout candle's high (short), or use an ATR-based stop (e.g., 1.5× ATR).
- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
- **Trailing Stop:** Trail using the 3-day KST EMA or a chandelier exit once the trade is in profit.
- **Time Stop:** If no favorable move occurs within N bars (e.g., 5–10), exit to avoid dead trades.
- **Avoid Choppy Markets:** Skip signals when Vortex lines are tightly intertwined or when EVF is near zero.

---

**5. REQUIRED INDICATORS**

| Indicator | Purpose | Typical Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Vortex Indicator | Trend-shift detection | 14-period |
| Elder's Volume Force | Volume momentum confirmation | 13-period EMA smoothing |
| KST Momentum Oscillator | Exit trigger | 3-day EMA of KST |
| ATR (optional) | Stop-loss sizing | 14-period |
| EMA (optional) | Trend filter | 20-period |

---

**6. LOGIC SUMMARY**

1. Wait for Vortex crossover (trend shift).
2. Confirm with rising/falling Elder's Volume Force (volume-backed momentum).
3. Enter on breakout of recent range.
4. Manage risk with ATR stop and position sizing.
5. Exit when the 3-day EMA of KST reverses direction.

This layered approach reduces whipsaws by requiring trend, volume, and momentum to align before entry, while using the fast KST EMA to lock in profits before full reversals occur.
