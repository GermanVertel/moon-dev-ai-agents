STRATEGY_NAME: FundingSqueeze

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy exploits mean-reversion in perpetual futures funding rates by shorting volatility when funding is elevated and price action compresses into a Bollinger Band squeeze, then exiting at liquidity clusters defined by volume-weighted price extremes.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Regime Filter: Only trade when the perpetual funding rate is in a "high" regime (e.g., annualized funding > 30-50%, or funding rate in the top 20th percentile of its rolling 30-day distribution). High positive funding signals crowded longs paying to stay long, which often precedes volatility compression and eventual mean-reversion.
- Volatility Trigger: Bollinger Band Squeeze — BandWidth (=(Upper-Lower)/Middle) drops to a multi-period low (e.g., lowest 10% of the last 50-100 bars). This signals a coiled market about to expand.
- Directional Bias: SHORT volatility, not necessarily short price. Implemented via short straddle/strangle, short gamma, or shorting the underlying when squeeze resolves downward.
- Exit Target: Liquidity clusters = recent volume-weighted price extremes (VWAP-anchored swing highs/lows, high-volume nodes on the volume profile, or volume-weighted pivot levels). Exit as price approaches these clusters.

2. ENTRY RULES
- Confirm funding regime: funding_rate >= threshold (e.g., 0.03% per 8h ≈ 33% annualized) for N consecutive periods.
- Confirm squeeze: Bollinger BandWidth at or below its 10th percentile over the lookback (default 100 bars), with band period 20 and 2 std devs.
- Optional confirmation: ADX < 20 (no trend), or price inside the bands for K bars.
- Trigger entry when either:
  a) Price closes back inside the bands after a brief poke outside (squeeze false-break), OR
  b) BandWidth begins expanding again after the squeeze low (expansion confirmation).
- Position: Short volatility (short straddle/strangle) OR directional short if squeeze breaks downward with volume.

3. EXIT RULES
- Primary exit: Price reaches a volume-weighted liquidity cluster (recent swing high/low weighted by volume, or high-volume node on the volume profile).
- Secondary exit: Funding rate normalizes (drops back into neutral regime).
- Time stop: Exit if neither target nor stop hit within X bars (e.g., 2x the average squeeze duration).
- Volatility exit: Close if implied/realized vol expands beyond a threshold (e.g., BandWidth > 90th percentile), signaling the squeeze has fully resolved.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position sizing scaled inversely to realized volatility and to the magnitude of the funding extreme.
- Hard stop: Price breaks beyond the recent volume-weighted extreme by a buffer (e.g., 1.5x ATR) — this invalidates the squeeze thesis.
- Max loss per trade capped (e.g., 1-2% of equity) because short-vol strategies have asymmetric payoff.
- Avoid holding through scheduled events (CPI, FOMC, earnings) that can cause gap risk.
- Cap concurrent short-vol exposure across correlated assets to avoid systemic squeeze risk.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS / DATA
- Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 std dev) + BandWidth percentile rank.
- Perpetual funding rate feed (8h or 1h granularity) with rolling percentile.
- Volume Profile / VWAP-anchored swing highs and lows (volume-weighted price extremes).
- ATR (14) for stop placement and sizing.
- ADX (14) optional for trend filter.
- Realized volatility (e.g., 20-period) for regime and sizing.
- Optional: Open Interest delta to confirm crowded positioning.
