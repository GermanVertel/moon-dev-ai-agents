STRATEGY_NAME: VolumeCascade

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines dual moving average crossover signals with volume confirmation and ATR-based exits to capture sustained trends while filtering out weak, low-conviction moves.

**Core Components:**

1. **Trend Identification (Dual MA Crossover)**
   - Fast MA: 10-period Simple Moving Average (SMA) of closing prices
   - Slow MA: 50-period Simple Moving Average (SMA) of closing prices
   - The 10/50 spread creates a responsive yet stable trend signal that reduces whipsaws common in single-MA systems.

2. **Volume Confirmation Filter**
   - A crossover signal is only valid if the current bar's volume exceeds its 20-period volume SMA (or an alternative threshold, e.g., 1.5x average volume).
   - This filters out crossovers driven by low-liquidity noise, ensuring institutional participation backs the move.

**Entry Rules:**

- **Long Entry:** 10-period MA crosses ABOVE the 50-period MA AND current volume > 20-period average volume.
- **Short Entry:** 10-period MA crosses BELOW the 50-period MA AND current volume > 20-period average volume.
- Enter at the close of the confirmation bar (or next bar open to avoid lookahead bias).

**Exit Rules (ATR-Based):**

- Compute ATR using a 14-period lookback.
- **Initial Stop-Loss:** Entry price ± (2.0 × ATR) — 2× ATR below entry for longs, 2× ATR above for shorts.
- **Trailing Stop:** As price moves favorably, trail the stop at (2.0 × ATR) from the highest high (longs) or lowest low (shorts) since entry.
- **Profit Target (optional):** Take partial profits at 3× ATR, letting the remainder run with the trailing stop.
- **Signal Exit:** Also exit if the 10-period MA crosses back through the 50-period MA in the opposite direction.

**Risk Management:**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk a fixed percentage of equity (e.g., 1–2%) per trade. Position size = (Account Risk $) / (2 × ATR). This normalizes risk across varying volatility regimes.
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** Limit to 3–5 uncorrelated instruments to avoid overexposure.
- **Volatility Guard:** Skip entries when ATR is in the top 5% of its 100-period range (extreme volatility) or bottom 10% (dead markets).
- **Time Stop:** Exit if the trade hasn't reached 1× ATR profit within 10 bars — signals a failed breakout.

**Required Indicators:**

| Indicator | Period | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| SMA (Fast) | 10 | Trend signal |
| SMA (Slow) | 50 | Trend signal |
| Volume SMA | 20 | Volume confirmation |
| ATR | 14 | Stop-loss, trailing, sizing |

**Strengths:**
- Volume filter significantly reduces false crossovers in choppy markets.
- ATR-based stops adapt to changing volatility, avoiding premature exits in volatile trends and overly wide stops in calm markets.

**Weaknesses & Mitigations:**
- Whipsaws in ranging markets → mitigated by volume filter and time stop.
- Lagging entries → acceptable trade-off for higher win-rate on sustained trends.
- Best suited for trending instruments (indices, commodities, large-cap equities) on 1H–Daily timeframes.

**Recommended Timeframes:** 1-hour, 4-hour, or daily charts. Avoid sub-15-minute timeframes where volume noise dominates.
