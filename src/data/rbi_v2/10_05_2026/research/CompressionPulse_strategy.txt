STRATEGY_NAME: CompressionPulse

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept**
This strategy identifies periods of abnormally low volatility (a "squeeze") and waits for a confirmed volatility expansion via momentum surge and volume confirmation. The edge comes from the statistical tendency of low-volatility regimes to precede directional breakout moves.

**Required Indicators**
1. **Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 standard deviations)** — used both for bandwidth calculation and as the exit target (upper band).
2. **Bollinger Band Width (BBW)** = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band, or absolute width. Compare current BBW to its own 20-period simple average.
3. **Rate of Change (ROC, 3-period)** — momentum trigger on price.
4. **Volume Median (20-day)** — baseline for detecting volume spikes.

**Entry Conditions (all must be true simultaneously)**
1. **Squeeze condition:** Current Bollinger Band Width is below its 20-period average (contraction regime — the market is coiling).
2. **Momentum flip:** 3-period ROC crosses from ≤ 0 to > 0 (fresh upward momentum ignition).
3. **Volume confirmation:** Current bar volume ≥ 1.5 × 20-day median volume (institutional participation / breakout validity).
4. **Directional bias (optional filter):** Price is at or above the middle band, confirming the breakout favors the upside.

**Entry Execution**
- Enter long on the close of the signal bar, or on a stop-entry just above the signal bar's high for confirmation.
- Only one position at a time; no pyramiding.

**Exit Rules**
- **Primary exit:** Price touches or closes above the upper Bollinger Band (mean-extension target).
- **Stop loss:** Placed below the lower Bollinger Band, or below the recent swing low / signal bar low, whichever is tighter (typically 1.5–2× ATR).
- **Time stop:** Exit if the trade has not reached the upper band within N bars (e.g., 10) and momentum (ROC) rolls back below zero.

**Risk Management**
- **Position sizing:** Risk a fixed percentage of equity (e.g., 1%) per trade based on distance from entry to stop.
- **Volatility-adjusted sizing:** Since squeeze regimes have small stops, cap position size to avoid over-leverage when bands are narrow.
- **Max concurrent exposure:** One open trade; avoid re-entry until a new squeeze forms.
- **Slippage buffer:** Because breakouts occur on volume spikes, assume wider fills; require the volume filter to be met on the signal bar (not just intraday).

**Rationale / Edge**
- BBW contraction below its average signals a statistical volatility trough — historically mean-reverting to expansion.
- ROC crossover confirms the direction of that expansion rather than guessing it.
- The 50% volume spike filter removes false breakouts that lack participation, improving the win rate of squeeze resolutions.
- Exiting at the upper band captures the bulk of the expansion move without giving back gains to mean reversion.

**Best Market Regime**
Trending or breakout-friendly markets (equities, futures, crypto) after consolidation phases. Avoid ranging, low-liquidity instruments where volume medians are unstable.

**Failure Modes to Watch**
- False squeezes in choppy markets producing whipsaws.
- Volume spikes from news/earnings that reverse intraday.
- Bandwidth already expanding before entry (missed the compression window).
