STRATEGY_NAME: Ichimoku Volume Reversion

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview**
This is a mean-reversion strategy built on the Ichimoku Kinko Hyo system, using the Chikou Span (lagging line) as the primary trigger. It combines price-vs-average deviation with volume divergence confirmation and volatility-adaptive trailing exits. The strategy is contrarian in nature—it fades extended moves rather than following them.

**Core Components**

1. **Chikou Span (Lagging Span)**
   - Chikou Span = current close plotted 26 periods back.
   - Compared against a 50-period Simple Moving Average (SMA) of price.
   - Cross above the 50-SMA signals a potential bearish reversal setup (price is overextended above its mean when projected backward).
   - Cross below the 50-SMA signals a potential bullish reversal setup.

2. **Volume Divergence Filter**
   - A "divergent volume spike" means volume expands sharply at the crossover but price does not confirm with a matching directional move (i.e., spike on a wick or exhaustion candle).
   - Compare current volume against a rolling average (e.g., 20-period volume SMA). A spike = volume > 1.5x–2x average.
   - Divergence condition: volume spike occurs while the Chikou/50-SMA cross happens against the prevailing short-term trend.
   - This filters out genuine breakouts from exhaustion moves.

3. **50-period SMA (Mean Reference)**
   - Acts as the mean reversion anchor and the Chikou comparison baseline.
   - Also used as a soft target for the reversion move.

**Entry Rules**

- **Long Entry (bullish reversion):**
  - Chikou Span crosses below the 50-SMA.
  - Volume spike (≥1.5x 20-period average) with divergence (price making a lower low but volume not confirming continuation).
  - Entry on the close of the confirmation candle or on a retest of the 50-SMA from below.

- **Short Entry (bearish reversion):**
  - Chikou Span crosses above the 50-SMA.
  - Volume spike with divergence (price making a higher high but momentum/volume fading).
  - Entry on close or retest of the 50-SMA from above.

**Exit Rules**

1. **Trailing Stop (Volatility-Adjusted)**
   - Use ATR (Average True Range, 14-period) to set trailing stop distance.
   - Multiplier: 1.5x–2.5x ATR, dynamically widened when expected volatility swings are high (e.g., during high-ATR regimes or ahead of news).
   - Trail the stop in the direction of the trade as price reverts toward the 50-SMA.

2. **Mean Target**
   - Primary profit target = 50-SMA itself (the mean).
   - Partial exit at 50% of the distance to the mean, remainder trails.

3. **Hold in Opposite Trend Direction**
   - If the broader trend (e.g., 200-SMA slope or Ichimoku cloud direction) is opposite to the reversion trade, hold the position longer—do not exit at the mean.
   - This exploits the fact that counter-trend reversions can extend when the dominant trend is exhausted.
   - In this case, the trailing stop becomes the sole exit mechanism.

**Risk Management**

- **Position sizing:** Risk per trade ≤1% of equity, sized off the ATR-based stop distance.
- **Stop loss:** Initial hard stop = entry ± 2x ATR (opposite side of trade).
- **Volatility regime filter:** Avoid entries when ATR is in the bottom 20th percentile (dead market) or top 95th percentile (chaotic).
- **Max holding period:** Time-stop of N bars (e.g., 30) if neither target nor stop is hit.
- **Correlation guard:** Limit simultaneous reversion trades in correlated instruments.

**Required Indicators**

| Indicator | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Chikou Span (Close shifted -26) | Primary crossover trigger |
| 50-period SMA | Mean reference / target |
| 200-period SMA or Ichimoku Cloud | Trend direction filter for hold logic |
| Volume + 20-period Volume SMA | Divergence spike detection |
| ATR (14) | Trailing stop and stop-loss sizing |
| Ichimoku Tenkan/Kijun (optional) | Additional confluence |

**Edge Case Notes**

- Chikou Span is a lagging construct—ensure your platform plots it correctly (close displaced 26 bars back) or compute manually.
- Volume divergence is subjective; define it numerically (e.g., volume > 1.5x avg AND close change < 0.5x ATR) to avoid discretionary ambiguity.
- The "hold in opposite trend" rule requires a clear trend definition—use 200-SMA slope or cloud position, not subjective reading.
