STRATEGY_NAME: ReversalIgnition

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a **pullback-continuation long setup** that combines candlestick breakout structure, RSI regime shift, and volume expansion. The core idea is that price breaks to a new local high on a bullish candle, RSI cools off from overbought territory (a "bearish turn" that reflects a healthy pullback rather than a trend reversal), and rising volume confirms renewed buying interest. The trade is taken long on the confluence of these three signals.

---

### 1. Key Strategy Components

**A. Bullish Candlestick Breakout**
- Price must close above a defined recent high (e.g., highest high of the last N bars, default N = 20).
- The breakout candle must be bullish (close > open) and ideally close in the upper third of its range.
- Optional quality filter: body size > 50% of the candle's total range (avoids wicky/indecisive breakouts).

**B. 14-Day RSI "Bearish Turn" After Being Bullish**
- RSI(14) was previously in bullish/overbought territory (above 60, or above 70 for a stricter version).
- RSI has now turned down (current RSI < previous RSI) and/or crossed below the bullish threshold.
- Interpretation: momentum is cooling, giving a better entry price on the breakout rather than chasing an extended move.
- The turn must be **recent** (within the last 1–3 bars) so the setup stays timely.

**C. Volume Expansion**
- Current bar volume > average volume of the prior 20 bars (e.g., volume > 1.2x–1.5x SMA(volume, 20)).
- Volume must be rising relative to the prior 1–2 bars (volume > volume[1]).
- Confirms that the breakout and RSI reset are backed by real participation.

---

### 2. Entry Rules

**Long Entry Trigger (all must be true on the same bar or within a 1–2 bar window):**
1. Close > highest high of the prior 20 bars (breakout).
2. Candle is bullish (close > open).
3. RSI(14) < RSI(14)[1] (turning down) AND RSI(14)[1] was ≥ 60 (was bullish).
4. Volume > 1.2 × SMA(volume, 20) AND volume > volume[1].

**Entry execution:**
- Enter long at the close of the signal bar, or on a stop order at the signal bar's high + 1 tick for confirmation.
- Avoid entering if price is more than X% above the 20-period EMA (default 3%) to prevent chasing parabolic moves.

---

### 3. Exit Rules

**Profit target:**
- Primary: 2R (two times the initial risk).
- Optional trailing: exit when price closes below the 10-period EMA, or when RSI re-enters overbought (>70) and turns down again.

**Stop loss:**
- Place stop below the low of the breakout candle, or below the recent swing low (whichever is tighter but still gives ≥ 1 ATR of room).
- Alternative: 1.5 × ATR(14) below entry.

**Time stop:**
- If neither target nor stop is hit within 10 bars, exit at market to avoid dead trades.

---

### 4. Risk Management

- **Position sizing:** Risk no more than 1% of account equity per trade. Size = (Account × 0.01) / (Entry − Stop).
- **Max concurrent positions:** 1–3 depending on correlation; avoid stacking longs in the same sector.
- **Volatility filter:** Skip signals when ATR(14) is in the bottom 10% of its 100-bar range (dead market) or top 5% (chaotic market).
- **Trend filter (optional):** Only take longs when price is above the 200-period EMA to align with the higher-timeframe trend.
- **No averaging down:** One entry per signal; if stopped out, wait for a fresh setup.

---

### 5. Required Indicators

| Indicator | Settings | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| RSI | 14-period | Detect bullish→bearish momentum turn |
| Volume SMA | 20-period | Baseline for volume expansion |
| Highest High | 20-period | Breakout level |
| ATR | 14-period | Stop placement & volatility filter |
| EMA | 200-period (optional) | Higher-timeframe trend filter |
| EMA | 10-period (optional) | Trailing exit |

---

### 6. Why This Works

The combination targets a specific market behavior: **a breakout that isn't overextended.** Pure breakout systems often buy into exhaustion (RSI pegged high, volume climaxing). By requiring RSI to *turn down* from a bullish state while price still makes a new high, the strategy waits for a brief momentum reset—often the pause before continuation. Rising volume on that bar confirms buyers are stepping back in, filtering out false breakouts on low participation.

**Best markets:** Trending equities, indices, and liquid futures on the daily or 4H timeframe. Less effective in choppy, range-bound conditions—hence the trend filter and ATR gate.
