STRATEGY_NAME: CompressionBurst

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy captures explosive directional moves that follow periods of unusually low volatility. It combines Bollinger Band width analysis with ATR-based squeeze detection, then uses a dynamic trailing stop derived from the breakout candle's own range to let winners run while locking in gains.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Volatility Squeeze Detection: Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations) are monitored for contraction. A squeeze is confirmed when the BandWidth (upper band − lower band) falls below a 20-period ATR threshold (e.g., BandWidth < 1.0 × ATR(20), or a configurable multiplier).
- Breakout Trigger: Once the squeeze condition is active, a long entry is taken when price closes above the upper Bollinger Band on the same or subsequent bar.
- Dynamic Trailing Exit: The stop is anchored to the breakout candle's range (High − Low of the trigger candle). The trailing stop trails price by a fixed percentage of that range (e.g., 50–75%), moving up only and never down.

2. ENTRY RULES (LONG ONLY)
- Condition A (Squeeze): Bollinger BandWidth < ATR(20) × squeeze_multiplier (default 1.0), evaluated on the prior bar or current bar.
- Condition B (Breakout): Close > Upper Bollinger Band.
- Condition C (Optional filter): Volume above its 20-period average, or price above a higher-timeframe trend filter (e.g., 50/200 EMA) to avoid false breakouts.
- Entry: Enter long at the close of the breakout candle (or next bar open).
- Record the breakout candle's range: R = High − Low.

3. EXIT RULES
- Initial Stop: Entry price − (R × trail_pct), where trail_pct defaults to 0.5–0.75.
- Trailing Logic: Each subsequent bar, recompute the stop as (Highest High since entry) − (R × trail_pct). The stop only ratchets upward.
- Hard Exit: Close position when price touches the trailing stop.
- Optional Profit Target: Exit if price reaches entry + (R × target_multiple), e.g., 2×R, for a fixed R-multiple take-profit.
- Time Stop (optional): Exit after N bars if neither stop nor target is hit, to avoid dead trades.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position sizing: Risk a fixed % of equity per trade (e.g., 1%). Size = (Equity × Risk%) / (Entry − Initial Stop).
- Since the initial stop is range-based, position size auto-adjusts to the breakout candle's volatility.
- Max concurrent positions and max daily loss limits recommended.
- Avoid entries near major news events or illiquid sessions.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA, 2.0 standard deviations (upper, middle, lower).
- ATR: 20-period Average True Range.
- BandWidth: (Upper − Lower) / Middle, or raw (Upper − Lower).
- Optional: Volume SMA(20), EMA(50/200) trend filter.

6. PSEUDOCODE
```
bb_upper, bb_mid, bb_lower = BollingerBands(close, 20, 2)
atr = ATR(high, low, close, 20)
bandwidth = bb_upper - bb_lower
squeeze = bandwidth < atr * squeeze_multiplier

if flat and squeeze and close > bb_upper:
    entry = close
    R = high - low
    stop = entry - R * trail_pct
    trail_high = high

if long:
    trail_high = max(trail_high, high)
    stop = max(stop, trail_high - R * trail_pct)
    if low <= stop:
        exit at stop
```

This CompressionBurst approach is distinct because it gates entries on a quantified volatility contraction (ATR-referenced BandWidth) and manages exits using the breakout candle's intrinsic range rather than fixed points or ATR multiples alone — making it adaptive to the specific energy released at the moment of breakout.
