STRATEGY_NAME: CompressionBreakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy identifies periods of abnormally low volatility (a "squeeze") using Bollinger Band width contraction, then trades the subsequent expansion breakout with an ATR-based trailing stop.

KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
1. Volatility Squeeze Detection
   - Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA with 2 standard deviations.
   - Band Width = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band (normalized to be price-independent).
   - Squeeze condition: current Band Width is the NARROWEST over the trailing 20 days (i.e., Band Width == rolling 20-day minimum). This is a stricter, cleaner definition than the common "width < threshold" approach.
   - Optionally require the squeeze to persist for 1–3 bars to filter noise.

2. Breakout Trigger (Long)
   - Price must CLOSE above the Upper Bollinger Band on the same bar the squeeze condition was true (or within 1–2 bars after).
   - Confirmation filters (recommended):
     * Close > prior bar's high (momentum confirmation).
     * Volume > 20-period average volume (avoids false breakouts on thin tape).
   - Entry: next bar open, or market/limit on the breakout close.

ENTRY RULES
- Long only (strategy as written; can be mirrored for shorts).
- Conditions on bar t:
  1. BBWidth[t] == min(BBWidth, 20)
  2. Close[t] > UpperBand[t]
  3. (Optional) Volume[t] > SMA(Volume, 20)
  4. (Optional) Close[t] > High[t-1]
- Enter long at bar t+1 open.

EXIT RULES
- Primary exit: 2× ATR trailing stop.
  * Initial stop = Entry Price − (2 × ATR(14))
  * Each bar: NewStop = max(PreviousStop, Close − (2 × ATR(14)))
  * Stop only ratchets up; never loosens.
  * Exit when price closes below (or touches, per preference) the trailing stop.
- Optional secondary exit: close below the Middle Band (20-SMA) as a momentum-failure signal.
- No fixed profit target — let the trend run until the trailing stop is hit.

RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position sizing: risk a fixed % of equity per trade (e.g., 0.5–1%). Shares = (Equity × Risk%) / (Entry − InitialStop).
- Max concurrent positions: 1–3 to limit correlated exposure.
- Time stop: exit if no new high is made within X bars (e.g., 15) and price stalls — optional.
- Avoid trading during earnings or major news if using single stocks.

REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0) — Upper, Middle, Lower
- Bollinger Band Width (derived)
- 20-day rolling minimum of Band Width
- ATR (14) for the trailing stop
- 20-period SMA of Volume (optional confirmation)
- Prior bar high (optional confirmation)

NOTES / EDGE CASES
- Squeezes often resolve in either direction; the close-above-upper-band filter ensures we only take upside resolution.
- In choppy markets, false breakouts are common — the volume and prior-high filters materially improve the win rate.
- The 2× ATR trailing stop naturally adapts: wider during volatile expansions, tighter as the move matures.
- Backtest across multiple timeframes (daily and 4H are typical) and asset classes (equities, FX, crypto) since squeeze dynamics vary.
