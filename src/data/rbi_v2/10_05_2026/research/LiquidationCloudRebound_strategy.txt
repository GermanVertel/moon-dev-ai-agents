STRATEGY_NAME: LiquidationCloudRebound

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines liquidation-driven capitulation signals with Ichimoku Cloud trend confirmation and orderbook microstructure for entries, exits, and risk placement. It is designed for long-only Bitcoin trades on intraday timeframes (1H execution with 24H liquidation context).

1. CORE STRATEGY COMPONENTS

A. Liquidation Shock Filter (Regime Trigger)
- Data source: Aggregate 24h liquidation volume across major derivatives exchanges (Binance, Bybit, OKX, etc.), split by longs vs shorts if available.
- Metric: 24h total liquidation notional.
- Condition: Current 24h liquidation value must exceed the 90th percentile of its rolling distribution (recommend 30–90 day lookback, e.g., 60 days).
- Interpretation: Extreme liquidation events signal forced selling exhaustion / capitulation, creating asymmetric long opportunities.
- Optional refinement: Weight short liquidations more heavily, since cascading short liquidations often precede sharp upside moves.

B. Ichimoku Cloud Rebound Confirmation (Trend + Timing)
- Indicators: Tenkan-sen (9), Kijun-sen (26), Senkou Span A/B (52), Chikou Span (26) on the 1H chart.
- Entry trigger requires BOTH:
  1. Price was recently below or inside the Cloud (within last N bars, e.g., 5–10).
  2. Price closes ABOVE the Cloud (above both Senkou Span A and B).
  3. Tenkan-sen crosses above Kijun-sen (or is already above and rising).
  4. Chikou Span is above price from 26 periods ago (bullish confirmation).
- This filters out false rebounds during sustained downtrends.

C. Orderbook Imbalance Zones (Risk Placement)
- Data source: Level 2 orderbook depth (bid/ask) on 1H snapshots or aggregated depth heatmaps.
- Imbalance zone definition: Price levels where bid depth significantly exceeds ask depth (e.g., bid/ask ratio > 1.5x within ±0.5% bands).
- These zones act as dynamic support. The stop loss is placed BELOW the nearest significant bid-heavy imbalance zone (with a small buffer, e.g., 0.2–0.3%).

2. ENTRY RULES (LONG)

All conditions must be TRUE on the 1H close:
1. 24h liquidation volume > 90th percentile (rolling 60-day window).
2. Price closes above the Ichimoku Cloud.
3. Tenkan-sen > Kijun-sen (bullish TK cross or sustained).
4. Chikou Span confirms bullish (above price 26 bars ago).
5. Optional confluence: RSI(14) on 1H rising from oversold (<40) or reclaiming 50.
6. Entry: Market or limit order at the close of the confirmation candle.

3. EXIT RULES

A. Primary Target — Cloud Resistance
- Exit at the upper boundary of the Ichimoku Cloud (Senkou Span A or B, whichever is higher) IF price approaches it and shows rejection (e.g., bearish candle, wick rejection, or failure to close above).
- Alternative: Use projected Cloud resistance 26 periods ahead (Senkou Span plotted forward) as a dynamic target.
- Scale-out option: Take 50% profit at first Cloud resistance touch, trail the rest.

B. Trailing Exit
- Trail stop below each new 1H swing low OR below the Kijun-sen once price is 2x ATR above entry.
- Exit fully if price closes back INSIDE the Cloud (trend invalidation).

C. Time Stop
- If the trade does not reach Cloud resistance within 48–72 hours, reassess and consider closing at breakeven or small profit.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT

- Stop Loss: Placed below the nearest hourly orderbook bid-heavy imbalance zone, with a 0.2–0.3% buffer. If no clear imbalance zone exists, default to 1.5x ATR(14) below entry.
- Position Sizing: Risk max 1–2% of account equity per trade. Size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop).
- Max Leverage: 2–3x on derivatives; spot preferred for liquidation-event trades due to volatility.
- Correlation Guard: Do not open multiple longs if already exposed to BTC beta via alts.
- Event Filter: Avoid entering within 30 minutes of major macro events (CPI, FOMC) unless liquidation trigger is extreme (>95th percentile).

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS & DATA

- Ichimoku Cloud (9, 26, 52) — 1H chart.
- ATR(14) — 1H, for stop sizing fallback.
- RSI(14) — optional confluence.
- 24h Liquidation Volume Feed — Coinglass, Coinalyze, or exchange APIs; must support percentile calculation over rolling 60 days.
- Level 2 Orderbook Depth — exchange WebSocket feeds (Binance/Bybit) aggregated into 0.5% bands.
- Timeframe: 1H execution, 24H liquidation context, 4H optional trend filter.

6. STRATEGY LOGIC SUMMARY (PSEUDOCODE)

IF 24h_liquidation > percentile(24h_liquidation, 90, 60d)
   AND close > max(SenkouA, SenkouB)
   AND Tenkan > Kijun
   AND Chikou > close[26]
THEN
   entry = close
   stop = nearest_bid_imbalance_zone * (1 - 0.003)
   target = upper_cloud_boundary (current or projected)
   size = (equity * 0.015) / (entry - stop)
   EXIT when price hits target OR close < Cloud OR time_stop > 72h

7. EDGE CASES & NOTES

- Liquidation percentiles can be skewed by outlier days; use log-scale or winsorize the distribution.
- Cloud resistance may be thin in strong trends — allow trailing stop to capture extended moves beyond the Cloud.
- Orderbook imbalance zones can vanish rapidly; re-fetch depth every 15–30 minutes while in trade.
- Backtest across 2020–2024 to validate percentile threshold (test 85th, 90th, 95th) and Cloud exit efficiency.
- Consider a short-side mirror version (LiquidationCloudRejection) for symmetry, but only after long-side validation.
