STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityTrapDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a mean-reversion/overextension fade setup that exploits the lag between price momentum (Bollinger Band touch) and momentum confirmation (MACD crossover). The core thesis is that when price pierces the upper 2-standard deviation Bollinger Band but MACD has NOT yet crossed above its signal line, the move lacks genuine momentum confirmation — a classic "trap" condition that often precedes a reversal.

═══════════════════════════════════════
1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════

• Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations)
  - Upper band acts as the overextension trigger
  - Band width also used as a volatility filter (avoid entering when bands are compressed)

• MACD (12, 26, 9)
  - MACD line vs. Signal line relationship is the confirmation gate
  - The key edge: enter ONLY when MACD line is still BELOW signal line at the moment of the upper-band touch

• ATR (14-period) — used for volatility checks and exit/stop sizing

• Divergence detection on MACD histogram / price swing highs

═══════════════════════════════════════
2. ENTRY RULES (SHORT / FADE SETUP)
═══════════════════════════════════════

Primary entry condition (all must be true):
1. Price high touches or exceeds the upper Bollinger Band (2σ).
2. MACD line is currently BELOW its signal line (no bullish crossover has occurred).
3. MACD histogram is flat or declining (negative slope) — confirms fading momentum.
4. ATR is above a minimum threshold (e.g., ATR > 20-period ATR average) to ensure tradable volatility.
5. Optional filter: Bollinger Band width is NOT in a squeeze (avoid low-volatility whipsaws).

Entry trigger:
- Enter short on the close of the candle that touches the upper band, OR
- Enter short on the first candle that closes back INSIDE the upper band (confirmation of rejection).

═══════════════════════════════════════
3. EXIT RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════

Exit Type A — MACD Divergence Exit:
- Monitor for bullish divergence on MACD (price making lower lows while MACD histogram makes higher lows).
- When divergence is confirmed, close the short position — this signals the momentum is genuinely turning and the mean-reversion move is exhausting.

Exit Type B — ATR Volatility Exit:
- If ATR spikes beyond a defined multiple (e.g., ATR > 1.5× its 20-period average) against the position, exit immediately to avoid runaway volatility.
- If ATR collapses below a floor (e.g., ATR < 0.5× average), exit because the move has stalled and risk/reward deteriorates.

Exit Type C — Target/Time Exit:
- Take profit at the middle Bollinger Band (20-period SMA) as the primary mean-reversion target.
- Optional runner to the lower Bollinger Band if divergence confirms strongly.
- Time stop: exit if neither target nor stop is hit within N bars (e.g., 10–15 bars).

═══════════════════════════════════════
4. RISK MANAGEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════

• Stop-loss: Place initial stop at 1.5× ATR above the entry high (or above the upper band touch high).
• Position sizing: Risk a fixed % of equity per trade (e.g., 1%), sized using ATR-based stop distance.
• Volatility guard: Skip trades when ATR is below the minimum threshold (dead markets cause false band touches).
• Correlation guard: Avoid stacking multiple shorts on correlated instruments simultaneously.
• Max concurrent exposure cap and daily loss limit.

═══════════════════════════════════════
5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
═══════════════════════════════════════

1. Bollinger Bands — 20-period SMA, 2.0 standard deviations (upper, middle, lower)
2. MACD — 12/26/9 (MACD line, signal line, histogram)
3. ATR — 14-period (plus a 20-period ATR average for volatility filtering)
4. Optional: Bollinger Band Width indicator for squeeze detection
5. Optional: Swing high/low detector for divergence confirmation

═══════════════════════════════════════
CORE EDGE SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════
The strategy profits from the statistical tendency of price to revert after tagging the 2σ upper band WITHOUT momentum confirmation. The MACD-not-yet-crossed condition filters out genuine breakouts (where MACD would already be above signal), isolating overextended "traps." Exits are dynamic — driven by MACD divergence (momentum-based) and ATR volatility (risk-based) rather than fixed targets alone, adapting the trade to evolving market conditions.
