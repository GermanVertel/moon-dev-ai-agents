STRATEGY_NAME: SqueezeLiquidation

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy targets explosive breakout moves that occur when price consolidates tightly (Bollinger Band squeeze) and is then catalyzed by forced liquidations in the futures market, with spot-futures basis divergence acting as a directional confirmation filter.

CORE LOGIC:
- Consolidation is identified when Bollinger Bands narrow (low volatility / squeeze), signaling stored energy.
- A breakout is only considered valid when a surge in futures liquidations occurs, indicating forced positioning unwind that fuels momentum.
- Spot-futures basis divergence confirms whether the move is driven by genuine spot demand (healthy) versus leveraged futures speculation (fragile), filtering out false breakouts.

COMPONENTS & INDICATORS:
1. Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 std dev)
   - Bandwidth = (Upper - Lower) / Middle
   - Squeeze condition: Bandwidth in lowest percentile (e.g., below 20th percentile of last 100 bars) or Bandwidth < threshold.
2. Futures Liquidation Data
   - Track long liquidations and short liquidations (aggregated across major exchanges).
   - Liquidation surge = liquidation volume > N times its rolling average (e.g., 3x the 50-bar average) within a short window.
   - Direction: Long liquidation surge = downward pressure; short liquidation surge = upward pressure.
3. Spot-Futures Basis
   - Basis = (Futures Price - Spot Price) / Spot Price
   - Basis divergence: Basis moves opposite to price direction, or basis spikes abnormally.
   - Confirmation: For a long breakout, basis should be positive and expanding (futures leading with healthy premium), OR basis divergence resolves in favor of breakout direction. For a short breakout, basis should be negative or compressing.

ENTRY RULES:
LONG ENTRY:
- Bollinger Band squeeze detected (bandwidth in low percentile).
- Price closes above the upper Bollinger Band (breakout).
- Short liquidation surge detected (shorts being squeezed).
- Spot-futures basis confirms: basis is positive and widening, or basis divergence shows futures premium expanding.
- Volume confirmation: breakout candle volume > 1.5x average.

SHORT ENTRY:
- Bollinger Band squeeze detected.
- Price closes below the lower Bollinger Band.
- Long liquidation surge detected (longs being liquidated).
- Spot-futures basis confirms: basis is negative and widening (futures discount deepening), or basis divergence shows futures discount expanding.
- Volume confirmation: breakout candle volume > 1.5x average.

EXIT RULES:
- Take Profit: 
  - Long: Exit at 2x the Bollinger Band width beyond entry, or when RSI > 75 and momentum stalls.
  - Short: Exit at 2x the Bollinger Band width beyond entry, or when RSI < 25 and momentum stalls.
- Stop Loss:
  - Long: Below the middle Bollinger Band or the breakout candle low, whichever is tighter.
  - Short: Above the middle Bollinger Band or the breakout candle high, whichever is tighter.
- Trailing Stop: Use ATR-based trailing stop (e.g., 1.5x ATR) once price moves 1x ATR in favor.
- Time Stop: Exit if breakout fails to extend within 3–5 bars (price re-enters Bollinger Bands).

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Position sizing: Risk no more than 1–2% of equity per trade.
- Leverage: Use moderate leverage (2–3x) given futures liquidation volatility.
- Avoid trading during low-liquidity hours or major news events unless liquidation surge is exceptionally strong.
- Confirm liquidation data is not stale (use real-time or near real-time feeds).
- Maximum 2–3 concurrent positions to avoid correlated exposure.
- If basis divergence contradicts breakout direction, skip the trade (no confirmation = no entry).

REQUIRED DATA & TOOLS:
- OHLCV price data (1m, 5m, or 15m timeframe).
- Bollinger Bands indicator (20, 2).
- Futures liquidation feed (aggregated long/short liquidations).
- Spot and futures price feeds for basis calculation.
- Volume moving average.
- ATR for trailing stops.
- RSI for overbought/oversold exit confirmation.

MARKET CONDITIONS:
- Best suited for ranging-to-breakout transitions in liquid markets (BTC, ETH, major altcoins).
- Avoid choppy, low-volume conditions where squeezes produce false breakouts.
- Works well during high-leverage periods when liquidation cascades are likely.

SUMMARY:
SqueezeLiquidation combines volatility compression (Bollinger squeeze) with market microstructure shocks (liquidation surges) and derivatives sentiment (spot-futures basis divergence) to trade high-probability breakouts. The triple confirmation reduces false signals and aligns entries with forced positioning unwinds that often precede sustained moves.
