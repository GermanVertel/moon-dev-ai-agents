STRATEGY_NAME: FiboPulseTrail

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy combines Fibonacci retracement levels with a trend-following oscillator to enter trades in the direction of the dominant trend while timing entries at high-probability pullback zones.

**Core Concept:**
Price in a strong trend tends to pull back to key Fibonacci levels (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) before resuming. The oscillator confirms whether the pullback is a healthy retracement (continuation) or a reversal (trend exhaustion).

**Required Indicators:**
1. **Fibonacci Retracement Tool** — anchored to the most recent significant swing high and swing low (lookback of 50–100 bars).
2. **Trend-Following Oscillator** — options include:
   - MACD (12, 26, 9) for momentum confirmation
   - Stochastic RSI (14, 14, 3, 3) for overbought/oversold in trend context
   - ADX (14) with +DI/-DI to confirm trend strength (>25)
3. **Moving Average Filter** — 50 EMA and 200 EMA to define trend direction.
4. **ATR (14)** — for volatility-based stop placement.

**Entry Rules (Long):**
1. Price is above the 200 EMA and 50 EMA is rising (uptrend confirmed).
2. ADX > 25 confirming trend strength.
3. Price pulls back and touches the 38.2%, 50%, or 61.8% Fibonacci retracement level.
4. Oscillator shows bullish divergence or crosses above its signal line (e.g., MACD histogram turning positive, or Stoch RSI crossing up from below 30).
5. Enter long on the candle close that confirms the oscillator signal at/near the Fibonacci level.

**Entry Rules (Short):**
- Mirror the above: price below 200 EMA, 50 EMA falling, ADX > 25.
- Price rallies to 38.2%, 50%, or 61.8% Fibonacci level.
- Oscillator shows bearish divergence or crosses below signal line.
- Enter short on confirming candle close.

**Exit Rules:**
- **Take Profit:** Target the previous swing high (for longs) or swing low (for shorts). Alternatively, scale out at 50% and 100% of measured move.
- **Stop Loss:** Place below the 61.8% Fibonacci level (for longs) or above it (for shorts), with a buffer of 0.5× ATR.
- **Trailing Stop:** Once price moves 1× ATR in profit, trail using the 50 EMA or a 2× ATR chandelier stop.
- **Oscillator Exit:** Close the trade if the oscillator shows opposite divergence or crosses its signal line against the position.

**Risk Management:**
- Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
- Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop Loss distance).
- Avoid trading when ADX < 20 (ranging market) — Fibonacci levels lose reliability.
- Skip trades if price is consolidating between the 38.2% and 61.8% levels for more than 10 bars without oscillator confirmation.
- Maximum 2 concurrent trades to limit correlated exposure.

**Confluence Checklist (must have at least 3 of 4):**
1. Trend alignment (EMA + ADX)
2. Fibonacci level touch
3. Oscillator signal/divergence
4. Candle confirmation (engulfing, pin bar, or momentum close)

**Timeframes:** Works best on 1H, 4H, and Daily charts. Avoid lower timeframes due to noise.

**Market Conditions:** Best in trending markets (forex majors, indices, crypto in directional phases). Poor performance in choppy/ranging markets — filter out with ADX.
