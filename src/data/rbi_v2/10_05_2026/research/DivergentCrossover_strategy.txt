STRATEGY_NAME: DivergentCrossover

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines trend-following (EMA crossover) with momentum confirmation (RSI + divergence) and a volume filter to reduce false signals. It is designed for long-only entries in an environment where momentum is supportive but not overextended.

**1. Key Strategy Components**
- **Trend Confirmation:** 20-period EMA crossing above the 50-period EMA signals a bullish trend shift.
- **Momentum Filter:** 14-period RSI must be below 70 (i.e., not overbought) to avoid chasing extended moves.
- **Divergence Signal:** A momentum indicator based on RSI divergence — typically, bullish RSI divergence (price makes a lower low while RSI makes a higher low) — must be present or recently confirmed to add conviction.
- **Volume Confirmation:** Current volume must be within ±20% of the 20-day average volume to ensure the move is backed by normal, healthy participation (not a low-liquidity spike or a volume anomaly).

**2. Entry Rules**
- **Primary Trigger:** 20 EMA crosses above 50 EMA (golden cross).
- **RSI Condition:** 14-period RSI < 70 at the time of the crossover (or on the signal bar).
- **Divergence Condition:** A bullish RSI divergence must be identified on the recent swing low (e.g., over the last 10–20 bars). The divergence can be confirmed via a higher RSI low against a lower price low.
- **Volume Condition:** Volume on the signal bar (or average of last 3 bars) must be between 80% and 120% of the 20-period average volume.
- **Execution:** Enter long at the close of the signal bar or on the next bar open if all conditions are met.

**3. Exit Rules**
- **Profit Target:** Optional — use a risk-reward ratio (e.g., 2:1 or 3:1) or trail with a shorter EMA (e.g., 10-period EMA).
- **Stop Loss:** Place below the most recent swing low or a fixed percentage (e.g., 2% below entry).
- **Trend Reversal Exit:** Exit if the 20 EMA crosses back below the 50 EMA.
- **RSI Overbought Exit:** Consider partial or full exit if RSI exceeds 75–80 (overbought) while price stalls.
- **Volume Anomaly Exit:** If volume spikes >200% of average on a down bar, consider tightening stops.

**4. Risk Management**
- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
- **Stop Loss:** Hard stop at swing low or 2% below entry, whichever is tighter.
- **Trailing Stop:** Optional — trail using 10-period EMA or ATR-based trailing stop (e.g., 2× ATR).
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** Limit to 3–5 to avoid overexposure.
- **Avoid News Events:** Skip entries around major earnings or economic releases unless volume filter confirms normal participation.

**5. Required Indicators**
- 20-period EMA (on close)
- 50-period EMA (on close)
- 14-period RSI (on close)
- RSI Divergence detection (bullish: price lower low, RSI higher low over lookback of 10–20 bars)
- 20-period average volume
- Optional: ATR (14) for volatility-based stops

**6. Best Timeframes & Markets**
- **Timeframes:** 1H, 4H, or Daily charts for swing trading.
- **Markets:** Liquid equities, ETFs, or major forex pairs with reliable volume data (avoid low-liquidity crypto pairs where volume is unreliable).

**7. Signal Confirmation Logic (Pseudo-code)**
```
if EMA20 > EMA50 and EMA20[1] <= EMA50[1]:  # crossover
    if RSI14 < 70:
        if bullish_RSI_divergence(lookback=15):
            if 0.8 * avg_volume20 <= volume <= 1.2 * avg_volume20:
                enter_long()
```

**8. Strengths & Weaknesses**
- **Strengths:** Combines trend, momentum, divergence, and volume — reduces false breakouts and overbought entries.
- **Weaknesses:** Divergence can be subjective; volume filter may delay entries in fast markets; EMA crossovers lag.

**9. Optimization Notes**
- Test divergence lookback (10–25 bars).
- Test volume tolerance (10%–30%).
- Test RSI upper bound (65–75).
- Walk-forward test to avoid curve fitting.
