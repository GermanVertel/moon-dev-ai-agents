STRATEGY_NAME: GoldenPulse Breakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines trend confirmation via EMA crossover, momentum strength via ADX, and volatility-adjusted risk management via ATR, with RSI acting as an early exit trigger. It is designed to capture sustained directional moves after a confirmed trend shift, while avoiding choppy or weak-trend environments.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Trend Confirmation: 50-period EMA crossing the 200-period EMA (Golden Cross for longs, Death Cross for shorts).
- Momentum Filter: ADX must rise above 25 to confirm that the trend has sufficient strength.
- Volatility-Based Risk: 2x ATR used for stop-loss placement.
- Overbought/Oversold Exit: 5-period RSI used to signal exhaustion and trigger exits.
- Timeframe: Best suited for intraday to swing timeframes (15m, 1H, 4H) where EMA crossovers and ADX signals are meaningful.

2. ENTRY RULES
Long Entry:
- 50 EMA crosses above 200 EMA (bullish crossover).
- ADX rises above 25 (trend strength confirmed).
- Enter long on the close of the candle that satisfies both conditions, or on a pullback to the 50 EMA for better risk/reward.

Short Entry:
- 50 EMA crosses below 200 EMA (bearish crossover).
- ADX rises above 25.
- Enter short on the close of the candle that satisfies both conditions, or on a pullback to the 50 EMA.

Optional Confirmation:
- Price should be above the 200 EMA for longs and below for shorts at entry.
- Avoid entries if ADX is falling or below 25, as this indicates weak trend.

3. EXIT RULES
Stop-Loss:
- Long: Entry price minus 2x ATR (14-period).
- Short: Entry price plus 2x ATR (14-period).
- Use ATR at the time of entry and keep it fixed, or trail it as the trade progresses.

Take-Profit / Exit Signal:
- Long: Exit when 5-period RSI rises above 70 (overbought).
- Short: Exit when 5-period RSI falls below 30 (oversold).
- Alternatively, exit on the opposite EMA crossover (50 crossing back over 200) or if ADX falls below 20, indicating trend weakening.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position Sizing: Risk no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade.
- Stop-Loss: Always use the 2x ATR stop; never trade without a stop.
- Risk-Reward: Aim for at least 1:2 risk-reward; the RSI exit often provides this if the trend runs.
- Trade Filter: Only take trades when ADX > 25 and rising; skip ranging markets.
- Max Concurrent Trades: Limit to 1-2 positions to avoid overexposure to correlated pairs.
- Trailing Stop: Optional—trail stop by 2x ATR once price moves 1x ATR in favor.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- 50-period EMA
- 200-period EMA
- ADX (14-period) with +DI and -DI
- ATR (14-period)
- RSI (5-period)

6. ADDITIONAL NOTES
- Best pairs: Major forex pairs, indices, or high-liquidity crypto (BTC/ETH).
- Avoid news events that can cause whipsaws around the EMA crossover.
- Backtest across different market regimes to ensure robustness.
- Consider using a higher timeframe (e.g., 4H) for trend confirmation and a lower timeframe (e.g., 1H) for entry timing.
