STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityVWAPBreakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This is a volatility-adjusted breakout system that combines volume-weighted price context with dynamic volatility bands to capture directional moves, using a retracement-based exit for profit protection.

CORE COMPONENTS:
1. **VWAP Baseline (20-period)**: Acts as the "fair value" anchor. Use a rolling 20-period VWAP (not session VWAP) to keep the band responsive to recent volume-weighted price. Reset behavior should be consistent with your timeframe (intraday or swing).

2. **ATR Bands (2x multiplier)**: 
   - Upper Band = VWAP_20 + (2 × ATR)
   - Lower Band = VWAP_20 − (2 × ATR)
   - ATR period: 14 (standard) on the same timeframe as the VWAP.
   - Bands expand/contract with volatility, so breakouts are volatility-normalized.

3. **Breakout Trigger**:
   - **Long Entry**: Price closes ABOVE the Upper Band.
   - **Short Entry**: Price closes BELOW the Lower Band.
   - Require a CLOSE beyond the band (not just a wick) to reduce false signals.

ENTRY RULES:
- Long: Close > (VWAP_20 + 2×ATR_14)
- Short: Close < (VWAP_20 − 2×ATR_14)
- Optional filter: Confirm with rising volume on breakout bar, or require price to hold above/below band for 1 additional bar.

EXIT RULES (50% Retracement of Breakout Move):
- Define the breakout move as the distance from the band level (entry reference) to the highest high (for longs) or lowest low (for shorts) reached after entry.
- **Long Exit**: Price retraces 50% from the post-entry high back toward the entry band level.
  - Exit Level = Entry_Band_Level + 0.5 × (Post_Entry_High − Entry_Band_Level)
- **Short Exit**: Price retraces 50% from the post-entry low back toward the entry band level.
  - Exit Level = Entry_Band_Level − 0.5 × (Entry_Band_Level − Post_Entry_Low)
- This is a trailing-style exit that locks in gains as the move extends.

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- **Initial Stop-Loss**: Place at the opposite band or at VWAP_20 (whichever is tighter/more logical). A common choice: stop at VWAP_20 for longs, since a return to fair value invalidates the breakout thesis.
- **Position Sizing**: Size inversely to ATR so risk per trade is constant across volatility regimes.
- **Max Holding Period**: Optional time-stop (e.g., N bars) to avoid dead trades if neither target nor stop hits.
- **Re-entry**: Allow re-entry only if a fresh close beyond the band occurs after a stop-out.

REQUIRED INDICATORS:
- Rolling VWAP (20-period)
- ATR (14-period)
- Price close and high/low for retracement tracking
- Optional: Volume (for confirmation filter)

TIMEFRAME & MARKET SUITABILITY:
- Best on liquid instruments with clear volume data (futures, major FX, large-cap equities, crypto majors).
- Works on intraday (5m–1h) for momentum bursts and on daily for swing breakouts.
- Avoid low-liquidity or ranging markets where VWAP bands compress and produce chop.

STRENGTHS:
- Volatility-normalized entries reduce whipsaw in quiet periods.
- VWAP anchor gives institutional-relevant reference.
- 50% retracement exit dynamically adapts to the size of each move rather than using fixed targets.

WEAKNESSES:
- Can give back ~half of unrealized gains by design.
- Prone to false breakouts in choppy, low-ATR conditions.
- Rolling VWAP (vs. session VWAP) may behave differently across timeframes—test carefully.
