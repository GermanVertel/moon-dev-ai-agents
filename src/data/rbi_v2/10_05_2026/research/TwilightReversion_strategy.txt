STRATEGY_NAME: TwilightReversion

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview:**
TwilightReversion is a mean-reversion strategy that capitalizes on end-of-day overextensions. It targets breaches of the Bollinger Band Upper Band during the late-day session (typically the final 1-2 hours of regular trading), betting that prices stretched too far above their statistical mean will revert downward before or shortly after the close.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Bollinger Bands Setup:**
   - Period: 20 (standard)
   - Standard Deviation multiplier: 2.0
   - Applied to a chosen timeframe (intraday, e.g., 5-min or 15-min bars)
   - Bands recalculate on each bar close

2. **Late-Day Session Filter:**
   - Only evaluate signals during a defined late-day window (e.g., 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM ET for US equities)
   - This exploits documented late-session liquidity and volatility patterns where overextensions are more prone to reversal

3. **Entry Logic (Short / Mean-Reversion):**
   - Price closes ABOVE the Upper Bollinger Band during the late-day window
   - Enter short (or exit longs) on the next bar open
   - Optional confirmation: RSI > 70 or volume spike to filter weak breaches

4. **Exit Logic:**
   - Primary exit: Price closes BELOW the Upper Bollinger Band (reversion to band)
   - Secondary exit: Price closes BELOW the Lower Bollinger Band (strong downside continuation) — treated as an aggressive take-profit or stop signal
   - Time-based exit: Flatten position at or before market close

**Entry/Exit Rules Summary:**
- **Short Entry:** Late-day bar closes above Upper Band → short next bar
- **Take Profit / Exit:** Bar closes below Upper Band → close short
- **Stop / Hard Exit:** Bar closes below Lower Band → exit (protect against trend continuation), or fixed % stop
- **EOD Flatten:** All positions closed by session end

**Risk Management:**
- Position sizing based on ATR or fixed fractional risk per trade (e.g., 1% of equity)
- Hard stop-loss placed above the breach bar's high (or a fixed % / ATR multiple)
- Maximum one signal per instrument per session to avoid overtrading
- Avoid entries in the final minutes where fills may be poor
- Consider avoiding earnings days or high-impact news events

**Required Indicators:**
- Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2σ) — Upper and Lower
- Optional: RSI (14) for overbought confirmation
- Optional: Volume moving average for breach validation
- Session/time filter (late-day window)

**Best Suited For:**
- Liquid instruments (large-cap equities, index ETFs, futures) with reliable intraday mean reversion
- Ranging or mildly trending markets where band breaches fade rather than extend
- Traders who can monitor the final trading hours and close positions before session end
