STRATEGY_NAME: Volumetric Squeeze-Breakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Bollinger Band volatility compression (the "squeeze") with volume delta confirmation to trade high-probability breakouts. It filters out false breakouts by requiring a 3x surge in volume delta, then uses a measured-move profit target and structural stop placement.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Volatility Compression Detection: Bollinger Bands must be in a "squeeze" state, meaning band width (Upper Band − Lower Band) contracts to a multi-period low. This signals energy buildup before expansion.
- Volume Delta Confirmation: A breakout is only valid if accompanied by a 3x surge in volume delta (net buying vs. selling pressure) relative to its recent average. This distinguishes genuine institutional participation from noise.
- Directional Breakout: Price must close outside the Bollinger Band (above Upper for longs, below Lower for shorts).
- Engulfing Candle Context: The breakout candle should engulf the prior candle's range, providing a structural reference for stop placement.

2. ENTRY RULES
Long Entry:
- Bollinger Band width is at or near its N-period low (squeeze condition).
- Price closes above the Upper Bollinger Band.
- Volume delta ≥ 3x the average volume delta of the last N bars.
- Entry candle is bullish and engulfs the prior candle's body/range.
- Enter at close of the breakout candle or on a small pullback retest.

Short Entry:
- Bollinger Band width at N-period low (squeeze condition).
- Price closes below the Lower Bollinger Band.
- Volume delta ≥ 3x the average (net selling surge).
- Entry candle is bearish and engulfs the prior candle's range.
- Enter at close or on retest.

3. EXIT RULES
- Profit Target: 1.5x the squeeze range, measured from the breakout point. Squeeze range = (Upper Band − Lower Band) at the moment of the tightest compression. Target = Breakout Price ± (1.5 × Squeeze Range).
- Stop Loss: Placed just beyond the low of the engulfing candle (for longs) or just above the high (for shorts), with a small buffer.
- Optional Trailing Stop: Trail below the engulfing candle low or use a moving average once price reaches 1x the squeeze range.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position sizing based on distance between entry and stop (engulfing candle low/high).
- Risk per trade capped at 1–2% of account equity.
- Skip trades where the squeeze range is abnormally large (poor R:R) or where volume delta surge is accompanied by a wick-heavy candle (rejection).
- Avoid trading during low-liquidity sessions or major news events that distort volume delta.
- Require minimum R:R of 1.5:1 before entry; if stop is too wide, reduce size or skip.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Bollinger Bands (typically 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations) — for squeeze detection and breakout levels.
- Bollinger Band Width / Bandwidth indicator — to quantify squeeze (N-period low).
- Volume Delta — net buy/sell volume (from order flow or tick data); needs a rolling average to compute the 3x surge.
- Candlestick pattern recognition — to identify engulfing candles.
- Optional: ATR for volatility context and trailing stop sizing.

6. EXECUTION NOTES
- Confirm squeeze on a closing basis, not intrabar, to avoid premature signals.
- Volume delta surge should be measured over the same timeframe as the breakout candle.
- Consider a secondary confirmation (e.g., RSI not overbought/oversold at breakout) to reduce chasing extended moves.
- Backtest across multiple timeframes; squeeze breakouts work best on 15m–4H charts in trending markets.
- Log squeeze range, volume delta multiple, and R:R for each trade to refine the 3x threshold.
