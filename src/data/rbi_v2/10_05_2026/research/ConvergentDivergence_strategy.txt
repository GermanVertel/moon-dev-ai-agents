STRATEGY_NAME: ConvergentDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a precision mean-reversion system that waits for three independent technical conditions to align before entering a counter-trend trade. The core thesis is that when momentum (MACD) diverges from price, volume dries up, and volatility compresses (Bollinger Bands narrowing), the market is coiling for a reversal back toward the mean.

**1. Key Strategy Components**

- **MACD Divergence (Signal Trigger):**
  - *Bullish divergence:* Price makes a lower low, but MACD line (and/or MACD histogram) makes a higher low. Signals weakening downside momentum.
  - *Bearish divergence:* Price makes a higher high, but MACD makes a lower high. Signals weakening upside momentum.
  - Use standard MACD (12, 26, 9) on the chosen timeframe.
  - Divergence must be confirmed across at least two consecutive swing points to filter noise.

- **Decreasing Volume (Confirmation Filter):**
  - Volume on the most recent swing leg must be lower than the volume on the prior swing leg.
  - This confirms that the current price extreme is being made on fading participation — a classic exhaustion signature.
  - Optionally use a 20-period volume moving average: current volume should be below its average during the divergence window.

- **Bollinger Bands Narrowing (Volatility Compression):**
  - Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations).
  - Band Width = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band.
  - The current Band Width must be in the lowest 20–25% of its recent range (e.g., 100-period lookback), or the bands must be visibly contracting over the last 5–10 bars.
  - Compression signals an impending expansion, and combined with divergence + fading volume, it biases the expansion toward the mean.

**2. Entry Rules**

- **Long Entry (Bullish Setup):**
  1. Bullish MACD divergence confirmed on the last two swing lows.
  2. Volume on the latest swing low < volume on the previous swing low.
  3. Bollinger Band Width is in the bottom quartile of its lookback range (compression active).
  4. Price is at or below the lower Bollinger Band (or within 0.5% of it).
  5. Enter long on the close of the confirmation candle (a bullish reversal candle such as hammer, engulfing, or a close back inside the lower band).

- **Short Entry (Bearish Setup):**
  1. Bearish MACD divergence confirmed on the last two swing highs.
  2. Volume on the latest swing high < volume on the previous swing high.
  3. Bollinger Band Width in the bottom quartile (compression active).
  4. Price is at or above the upper Bollinger Band (or within 0.5% of it).
  5. Enter short on the close of the confirmation candle (bearish reversal candle or close back inside the upper band).

**3. Exit Rules**

- **Take Profit:**
  - Primary target: the Bollinger Band middle band (20-period SMA) — the statistical mean.
  - Secondary target (optional runner): the opposite Bollinger Band if momentum strongly reverses.
  - Scale out 50% at the middle band, trail the remainder.

- **Stop Loss:**
  - Place stop beyond the most recent swing extreme that formed the divergence.
  - For longs: below the recent swing low, plus a small buffer (e.g., 0.25 × ATR).
  - For shorts: above the recent swing high, plus the same ATR buffer.
  - Alternative: stop at 1.5 × ATR from entry if swing structure is unclear.

- **Time-Based Exit:**
  - If the trade has not reached the middle band within N bars (e.g., 20), exit at market — the mean-reversion thesis has failed.

**4. Risk Management**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 0.5–1% of account equity per trade. Because divergence setups can fail during strong trends, keep size conservative.
- **Risk/Reward:** Aim for a minimum 1.5:1 R:R. If the distance to the middle band is less than 1.5× the stop distance, skip the trade.
- **Trade Filter:** Avoid taking counter-trend entries against a dominant higher-timeframe trend (e.g., don't short a strong daily uptrend) unless the higher timeframe also shows compression.
- **Max Concurrent Trades:** Limit to 1–2 correlated positions to avoid overexposure during a broad market move.
- **Volatility Guard:** Skip trades during major news events (earnings, FOMC) where volume and volatility become erratic and invalidate the compression premise.

**5. Required Indicators**

- MACD (12, 26, 9) — for divergence detection.
- Volume (raw) + 20-period Volume SMA — for fading-volume confirmation.
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) — for mean target and Band Width compression.
- ATR (14) — for stop placement and position sizing.
- Optional: Swing high/low detection logic (e.g., 5-bar fractal) to programmatically identify divergence pivots.

**Summary:** ConvergentDivergence only fires when momentum fades (MACD divergence), participation dries up (decreasing volume), and volatility coils (narrowing Bollinger Bands). The convergence of these three conditions produces a high-probability snap back to the mean, with the middle Bollinger Band as the natural profit target and swing structure defining risk.
