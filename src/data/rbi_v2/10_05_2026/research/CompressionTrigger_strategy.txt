STRATEGY_NAME: CompressionTrigger

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is designed to capture explosive directional moves that follow periods of unusually low volatility. It combines Bollinger Band width analysis (to detect squeezes) with a breakout confirmation and an ATR-based trailing stop for exit management.

---

**1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS**

- **Volatility Squeeze Detection:** Uses Bollinger Band Width (BBW) to identify when the bands have contracted to their narrowest point in the last 20 trading days. This signals a coiled market ready for expansion.
- **Breakout Confirmation:** A long entry is only triggered when price closes *above* the upper Bollinger Band, confirming upward momentum after the squeeze.
- **Trailing Exit:** Once in a trade, a trailing stop set at 2x the Average True Range (ATR) protects profits and allows the trade to run.

---

**2. INDICATORS REQUIRED**

- **Bollinger Bands (BB):**
  - Period: 20
  - Standard Deviations: 2.0
  - Components: Upper Band, Middle Band (SMA 20), Lower Band
- **Bollinger Band Width (BBW):**
  - Formula: (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band
  - Lookback for squeeze: 20 periods
- **Average True Range (ATR):**
  - Period: 14 (standard)
  - Multiplier for trailing stop: 2.0

---

**3. ENTRY RULES (LONG ONLY)**

1. Calculate BBW for the current bar.
2. Determine the minimum BBW value over the last 20 bars (including current).
3. **Condition A (Squeeze):** Current BBW ≤ minimum BBW of the last 20 bars (i.e., narrowest width in 20 days).
4. **Condition B (Breakout):** Current close > Upper Bollinger Band.
5. **Entry:** If both Condition A and Condition B are true on the same bar (or Condition A was true within the last N bars and Condition B triggers), enter a long position at the close of that bar (or next bar open for realism).

*Optional refinement:* Allow a small window (e.g., 3 bars) after the squeeze for the breakout to occur, since price may not break immediately on the squeeze bar.

---

**4. EXIT RULES**

- **Initial Stop:** Set at entry price − (2 × ATR at entry).
- **Trailing Stop:** After entry, the stop is trailed upward as follows:
  - New Stop = Highest Close Since Entry − (2 × Current ATR)
  - The stop only moves up, never down.
- **Exit Trigger:** Close the position when price closes below the trailing stop level (or if using intrabar stops, when price touches the stop).
- **Optional Time Stop:** Exit if the trade has not moved favorably within X bars (e.g., 20 bars) to avoid dead trades.

---

**5. RISK MANAGEMENT**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk a fixed percentage of account equity per trade (e.g., 1–2%). Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry Price − Initial Stop Price).
- **ATR-Based Stop:** Ensures the stop adapts to current market volatility — wider in volatile markets, tighter in calm ones.
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** Limit to 1–3 open trades to control exposure.
- **Slippage & Commission:** Account for realistic fills, especially on breakout entries.
- **Avoid Choppy Markets:** The squeeze filter naturally avoids low-volatility chop, but consider a trend filter (e.g., price above 200 SMA) to only take longs in bullish regimes.

---

**6. ADDITIONAL NOTES / EDGE CASES**

- **False Breakouts:** Squeezes can produce fake breakouts. The ATR trailing stop helps mitigate this by exiting quickly if momentum fails.
- **Parameter Sensitivity:** The 20-day squeeze lookback and 2x ATR multiplier should be backtested and optimized per instrument/timeframe.
- **Works Best On:** Liquid instruments with clear volatility cycles (e.g., indices, forex majors, large-cap stocks) on daily or 4H charts.
- **Confirmation Enhancement:** Consider requiring volume expansion on the breakout bar to filter weak signals.

---

**SUMMARY:**  
CompressionTrigger is a volatility-regime strategy that waits for the market to coil (narrowest BBW in 20 days), then rides the expansion via a breakout above the upper band, using a 2x ATR trailing stop to lock in gains while letting winners run.
