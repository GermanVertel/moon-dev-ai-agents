STRATEGY_NAME: VolumetricEngulf

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

## Core Concept
This strategy identifies high-probability breakout opportunities by combining **candlestick engulfing patterns** with **volume expansion confirmation**. The premise is that when a large candle engulfs the previous candle's range AND does so on significantly elevated volume, it signals institutional participation and a likely directional breakout. Volatility is used as a filter to ensure the market has enough range to produce meaningful moves.

## Key Strategy Components

1. **Engulfing Pattern Detection** — A bullish or bearish candle whose body/range completely engulfs the prior candle's body/range.
2. **Volume Spike Confirmation** — The engulfing candle's volume must exceed a rolling average (e.g., 1.5x–2x the 20-period volume SMA).
3. **Volatility Filter** — ATR or Bollinger Band width must be expanding, confirming a regime shift from consolidation to expansion.
4. **Breakout Trigger** — Price must close beyond a recent swing high/low or consolidation range to confirm directional intent.

## Entry Rules

**Long Entry:**
- Bullish engulfing candle forms (current close > open, body engulfs prior candle's body).
- Volume on engulfing candle ≥ 1.8 × 20-period volume SMA.
- Current ATR > 20-period ATR average (volatility expansion).
- Price closes above the highest high of the last 10 candles (breakout confirmation).
- Enter at next candle open or on a stop-buy above the engulfing candle's high.

**Short Entry:**
- Bearish engulfing candle forms (current close < open, body engulfs prior candle's body).
- Volume on engulfing candle ≥ 1.8 × 20-period volume SMA.
- Current ATR > 20-period ATR average.
- Price closes below the lowest low of the last 10 candles.
- Enter at next candle open or on a stop-sell below the engulfing candle's low.

## Exit Rules

- **Profit Target:** 2× the engulfing candle's range (measured move), or trail using a 1.5× ATR chandelier stop.
- **Stop Loss:** Placed at the opposite extreme of the engulfing candle (below its low for longs, above its high for shorts).
- **Time Exit:** Close position if no breakout follow-through within 5 candles.
- **Momentum Exit:** Exit if volume dries up below the 20-period SMA on the follow-through candle.

## Risk Management

- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1% of account equity per trade.
- **Stop Distance:** Use the engulfing candle's opposite extreme as the hard stop — this naturally sizes the position based on volatility.
- **R-Multiple Target:** Minimum 1.5R reward-to-risk; ideally 2R+.
- **Max Concurrent Trades:** Limit to 2–3 correlated positions to avoid cluster risk.
- **Avoid News Events:** Skip entries within 15 minutes of major economic releases to prevent slippage on false breakouts.

## Required Indicators

| Indicator | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Volume SMA (20) | Baseline for volume spike detection |
| ATR (14) | Volatility expansion filter & trailing stop |
| Swing High/Low (10) | Breakout confirmation level |
| Candlestick pattern recognition | Engulfing detection |
| Bollinger Band Width (optional) | Secondary volatility regime filter |

## Ideal Market Conditions
- Post-consolidation phases (tight ranges before expansion).
- Liquid instruments (large-cap equities, FX majors, BTC/ETH).
- Trending or breakout-friendly sessions (e.g., first 2 hours of market open).

## Edge & Rationale
The combination of **engulfing structure + volume expansion + volatility regime shift** filters out weak patterns that fail without participation. Volume confirms conviction, engulfing confirms intent, and volatility confirms the market is ready to move — stacking three independent confirmations dramatically improves breakout reliability.
