STRATEGY_NAME: CascadeVolatility

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
This strategy capitalizes on post-liquidation volatility expansions by detecting open interest (OI) cliffs (sudden drops indicating forced liquidations), then entering straddle-style positions when historical volatility (HVOL) bands break and funding rates flip polarity. Exits are guided by VIX term structure shifts.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Liquidation Cascade Detection (OI Cliffs):**
   - Monitor open interest across perpetual futures and options markets.
   - Identify "OI cliffs" — sharp, rapid declines (e.g., >5% drop within 15-60 min) signaling forced liquidations.
   - Confirm cascade with elevated volume spikes and wick-heavy candles on lower timeframes.
   - Track liquidation heatmaps to identify clustered liquidation levels.

2. **Volatility Expansion Confirmation (HVOL Bands):**
   - Calculate historical volatility (HVOL) using rolling windows (e.g., 20-period ATR-normalized or realized vol).
   - Define HVOL bands (e.g., ±1.5 to 2 standard deviations from mean HVOL).
   - Entry trigger: HVOL breaks above upper band post-cascade, signaling volatility regime shift.

3. **Funding Rate Polarity Flip:**
   - Monitor funding rates on perpetual swaps.
   - A polarity flip (positive → negative or vice versa) post-cascade confirms directional sentiment exhaustion.
   - Combine with OI cliff timing: funding flip within 1-3 funding intervals of the cascade strengthens signal.

4. **Straddle Entry Construction:**
   - Enter a long straddle (buy ATM call + buy ATM put) or synthetic straddle via perps/options.
   - Strike selection: nearest ATM to spot at cascade low/high.
   - Expiry: 1-3 days out to capture near-term vol expansion.
   - Alternative: delta-neutral perp position with dynamic hedging if options unavailable.

5. **VIX Term Structure Exit Logic:**
   - Track VIX futures term structure (front month vs. second month).
   - Exit when term structure flips from backwardation (fear) to contango (calm) — signals vol mean reversion.
   - Secondary exit: HVOL reverts below mid-band, or straddle P&L hits target (e.g., +50-100%).
   - Hard stop: time-based exit if no vol expansion within 24-48 hours.

**Entry Rules:**
- **Trigger Sequence (all must align):**
  1. OI cliff detected (≥5% drop in <1 hour).
  2. HVOL breaks above upper band.
  3. Funding rate flips polarity within 3 funding intervals.
- **Execution:** Enter straddle at market or limit near cascade extreme.
- **Sizing:** 1-2% risk per trade; reduce size if VIX already elevated (>25).

**Exit Rules:**
- **Primary:** VIX term structure flips to contango.
- **Secondary:** HVOL reverts to mean (mid-band).
- **Profit Target:** +75% on straddle premium.
- **Stop Loss:** -40% on straddle premium OR 48-hour time stop.
- **Emergency:** If OI recovers rapidly (>80% of pre-cascade level), exit immediately.

**Risk Management:**
- **Position Sizing:** Max 2% capital per cascade event; scale in tranches.
- **Correlation Risk:** Avoid multiple simultaneous straddles in correlated assets.
- **Liquidity Check:** Ensure options/perps have sufficient depth; avoid illiquid strikes.
- **Slippage Buffer:** Account for wide spreads during cascades (use limit orders).
- **Vol Crush Risk:** Exit if implied vol collapses without price movement.
- **Max Drawdown Guard:** Halt strategy if cumulative drawdown >10%.

**Required Indicators & Data:**
1. **Open Interest (OI)** — real-time perp/options OI feeds.
2. **Historical Volatility (HVOL)** — 20-period rolling realized vol with bands.
3. **Funding Rates** — 8-hour funding data across major exchanges.
4. **VIX Term Structure** — front and second-month VIX futures.
5. **Liquidation Heatmaps** — Coinglass/Glassnode-style data.
6. **Volume Profile** — to confirm cascade exhaustion.
7. **ATR** — for dynamic strike/stop placement.
8. **Options Chain** — for straddle construction and IV monitoring.

**Optimal Conditions:**
- High-leverage markets (crypto perps, index futures).
- Elevated baseline vol regimes (VIX 15-30).
- Events: macro releases, exchange outages, whale liquidations.

**Avoid When:**
- VIX already >35 (late-stage vol).
- OI cliffs are gradual (not cascades).
- Funding rates stable (no polarity flip).
- Low liquidity sessions (weekends, holidays).
