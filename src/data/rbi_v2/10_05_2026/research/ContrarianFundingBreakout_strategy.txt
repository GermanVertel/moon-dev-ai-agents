STRATEGY_NAME: ContrarianFundingBreakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines a sentiment-based contrarian signal (extreme funding rate) with a momentum confirmation (price breakout) and a dynamic exit mechanism (retracement + volatility stop). It aims to capture explosive upside moves that occur when bearish positioning (reflected in very low funding) is forced to unwind as price breaks to new highs.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Funding Rate Extreme: 24-hour funding rate reaching its lowest level in 30 days. This indicates that shorts are paying longs heavily (or longs are being paid very little), signaling crowded bearish positioning and a potential short squeeze.
- Price Breakout Confirmation: Price must breach the 10-day high. This confirms that the market is starting to move against the crowded short side, validating the contrarian thesis.
- Combined Trigger: Both conditions must be true simultaneously (or within a short window, e.g., same daily close) to enter long.
- Exit Logic: Two independent exit rules:
  a) 50% retracement from the peak price achieved since entry (trailing peak).
  b) Volatility-based stop: a stop placed at a distance equal to a multiple of recent volatility (e.g., 2x ATR) below the peak or entry.
- The exit is triggered by whichever occurs first.

2. ENTRY RULES
- Timeframe: Daily bars (or 24-hour periods for crypto). Funding rate is typically 8-hour or 24-hour; use the 24-hour funding rate as specified.
- Condition 1: 24h funding rate == lowest value over the past 30 days (30-day low). Use a rolling 30-day lookback.
- Condition 2: Price (close or intraday high) > highest high of the previous 10 days (10-day high breakout).
- Entry: Enter long at the next bar's open (or at the close of the bar that satisfies both conditions) with a market order.
- Position Sizing: Use a fixed fractional risk model (e.g., risk 1% of equity per trade). The stop distance (volatility-based) determines the position size.

3. EXIT RULES
- Track the highest price (peak) since entry.
- Exit Rule A (Retracement): If price retraces 50% from the peak to the entry price (i.e., peak - (peak - entry) * 0.5), exit at market. This locks in profits while allowing for some pullback.
- Exit Rule B (Volatility Stop): Place a stop-loss at peak - (ATR_multiplier * ATR). For example, 2 * ATR(14) below the peak. This stop trails the peak upward.
- Whichever exit is hit first triggers a full exit.
- Optional: If price makes a new low below the entry before reaching the peak, the volatility stop (if placed below entry) may trigger. Ensure the stop is not wider than the maximum acceptable risk.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Initial Stop: At entry, set the volatility stop at entry_price - (ATR_multiplier * ATR). This defines the initial risk (R).
- Position Size: Size = (Account Equity * Risk_Per_Trade) / (ATR_multiplier * ATR). For example, risk 1% of a $10,000 account = $100. If ATR = $500 and multiplier = 2, stop distance = $1000, so position size = 0.1 units (or equivalent).
- Trailing Stop: As price rises, move the volatility stop up to peak - (ATR_multiplier * ATR). Never move it down.
- Retracement Exit: The 50% retracement from peak is an additional profit-taking rule. It may trigger before the volatility stop if volatility is low.
- Max Risk per Trade: 1-2% of equity.
- No Pyramiding: One entry per signal. No adding to position.
- Re-entry: After exit, wait for a new signal (new 30-day funding low + new 10-day high breakout).

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- 24-hour Funding Rate (from exchange API or data provider).
- 30-day rolling minimum of the 24-hour funding rate.
- 10-day rolling maximum of price (high or close).
- ATR (Average True Range) with period 14 (or similar) for volatility measurement.
- Peak price tracker (highest high since entry).

6. ADDITIONAL NOTES
- Market Context: This strategy works best in trending or squeeze environments. Avoid low-liquidity periods.
- Data Frequency: Use daily closes for signals. Funding rate is typically updated every 8 hours; use the 24-hour aggregate (e.g., sum of 8-hour rates over 24h, or the 24h average).
- Backtesting: Ensure funding rate data is aligned with price data. Use conservative assumptions for slippage and fees.
- Parameter Optimization: The 30-day and 10-day lookbacks, ATR multiplier, and retracement percentage (50%) can be optimized, but avoid overfitting.
- Edge Case: If the 30-day funding low occurs on the same day as the 10-day high breakout, enter. If the funding low is followed by a breakout days later, the signal is still valid as long as the funding rate remains at or near the 30-day low (e.g., within the lowest 5% of the 30-day range).

This strategy is unique because it combines a contrarian sentiment signal (funding rate extreme) with a momentum breakout, and uses a dual-exit system (retracement + volatility stop) to manage profits and risk dynamically.
