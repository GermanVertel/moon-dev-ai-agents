STRATEGY_NAME: VolumeDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines MACD histogram divergence detection with volume-based trend confirmation to identify high-probability entry points during strong market movements. The dual confirmation approach filters out weak divergence signals that lack volume support.

**Core Components:**

1. **MACD Histogram Divergence Detection**
   - Calculate MACD (12, 26, 9) and extract the histogram (MACD line minus signal line)
   - Identify bullish divergence: price makes a lower low while MACD histogram makes a higher low
   - Identify bearish divergence: price makes a higher high while MACD histogram makes a lower high
   - Require divergence to form over a minimum of 5 bars to filter noise

2. **Volume Ratio Confirmation**
   - Calculate Volume Ratio = Current Volume / SMA(Volume, 20)
   - Uptrend confirmation: Volume Ratio > 1.2 on the divergence bar or the confirmation candle
   - This ensures institutional participation is present during the reversal setup
   - Optional: require Volume Ratio to be rising over 3 consecutive bars for stronger signal

**Entry Rules (Long):**
- Bullish MACD histogram divergence detected (price lower low, histogram higher low)
- Volume Ratio > 1.2 on the confirmation candle
- Confirmation candle closes above the prior candle's high
- Price above 200 EMA (trend filter) OR price reclaims 50 EMA (reversal filter)
- Enter on the close of the confirmation candle or on a break above its high

**Entry Rules (Short):**
- Bearish MACD histogram divergence detected (price higher high, histogram lower high)
- Volume Ratio > 1.2 on the confirmation candle
- Confirmation candle closes below the prior candle's low
- Price below 200 EMA OR price loses 50 EMA
- Enter on the close of the confirmation candle or on a break below its low

**Exit Rules:**
- **Stop Loss:** Entry price minus 1.5 × ATR(14) for longs; plus 1.5 × ATR(14) for shorts
- **Take Profit 1:** 2.0 × ATR(14) — scale out 50% of position
- **Take Profit 2:** 3.5 × ATR(14) — close remaining position
- **Trailing Stop:** After TP1 is hit, trail stop at 1.0 × ATR below the highest close (longs)
- **Reversal Exit:** Exit immediately if MACD histogram crosses zero in the opposite direction of the trade
- **Time Stop:** Exit if trade has not reached TP1 within 15 bars

**Risk Management:**
- Risk no more than 1% of account equity per trade
- Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry Price − Stop Loss Price)
- Maximum 3 concurrent positions
- Skip signals if ATR(14) is below 20-period average ATR (low volatility = unreliable divergence)
- Avoid trading within 30 minutes of major news releases

**Required Indicators:**
- MACD (12, 26, 9) — histogram focus
- Volume SMA (20) — for Volume Ratio
- ATR (14) — for stops and targets
- EMA 50 and EMA 200 — trend context filter
- Optional: RSI (14) to avoid entering overbought/oversold extremes

**Best Market Conditions:**
- Works best in trending markets with pullbacks (not choppy ranges)
- Higher timeframes (1H, 4H, Daily) produce more reliable divergence signals
- Volume Ratio filter is most effective in liquid markets (large-cap stocks, major FX pairs, BTC/ETH)
