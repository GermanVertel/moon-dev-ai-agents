STRATEGY_NAME: VortexSqueeze

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview:**
VortexSqueeze is a hybrid momentum-breakout strategy that fuses three independent market dimensions: directional trend confirmation (Vortex Indicator), participation intensity (volume surge), and volatility compression (Bollinger Band Width). The core thesis is that a genuine trend ignition occurs only when directional momentum aligns with an abnormal volume influx *while* the market is coiled in a low-volatility squeeze — filtering out false breakouts that occur during already-expanded volatility.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Vortex Indicator (VI+ / VI−):**
   - Period: 14 (standard)
   - VI+ = Σ|High − Prior Low| / Σ(True Range)
   - VI− = Σ|Low − Prior High| / Σ(True Range)
   - Bullish signal: VI+ crosses *above* VI− (note: the prompt's "VI+ crosses VI+" is interpreted as the standard VI+/VI− crossover, since VI+ cannot cross itself meaningfully).
   - This confirms the *directional* leg of the setup.

2. **Volume Surge Filter:**
   - Volume SMA: 20-period
   - Surge threshold: current volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-period volume SMA
   - Ensures the crossover is backed by real participation, not thin-market noise.

3. **Bollinger Band Width Compression:**
   - Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations
   - BBW = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band
   - Compression condition: current BBW is in the bottom 20th percentile of the last 100 bars (i.e., BBW ≤ 20th percentile of rolling BBW history)
   - Signals that volatility is coiled and a directional expansion is imminent.

**Entry Rules (Long):**
- VI+ crosses above VI− on the current bar (previous bar had VI+ ≤ VI−)
- Current volume ≥ 1.5 × 20-period volume SMA
- BBW ≤ 20th percentile of the last 100 BBW readings (squeeze active)
- Optional confirmation: close > 20-period SMA (bias alignment)
- Enter long at next bar open (or close of signal bar).

**Exit Rules:**
- Primary exit: price touches the *upper* Bollinger Band (opposing band touch) — take profit.
- Secondary exit: VI+ crosses back below VI− (trend invalidation).
- Time stop: exit after N bars (e.g., 20) if neither condition triggers.

**Risk Management:**
- Initial stop-loss: placed at the *lower* Bollinger Band at entry, or a fixed ATR-based stop (1.5 × ATR(14)), whichever is tighter.
- Position sizing: risk ≤ 1% of equity per trade; size = (Equity × 0.01) / (Entry − Stop).
- Trailing stop: once price reaches the middle band, move stop to breakeven; thereafter trail below the middle band.
- Avoid trades within 30 minutes of major news releases (volume surge may be event-driven, not trend-driven).
- Skip signals if BBW is already expanding (squeeze already released) to avoid chasing.

**Required Indicators:**
- Vortex Indicator (14) — VI+ and VI− lines
- Volume SMA (20)
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0) — Upper, Middle, Lower
- Bollinger Band Width (derived) — with 100-bar percentile ranking
- ATR (14) — for stop placement
- Optional: 20-period SMA for trend bias filter

**Rationale / Edge:**
Each component alone produces many false signals: Vortex crossovers whipsaw in chop, volume spikes occur in news noise, and squeezes often resolve without follow-through. By requiring all three simultaneously, VortexSqueeze isolates the rare confluence where *direction*, *conviction*, and *potential energy* align — the classic precondition for a sustained directional move. Exiting at the opposing Bollinger Band captures the volatility expansion leg while avoiding the mean-reversion that typically follows a band tag.
