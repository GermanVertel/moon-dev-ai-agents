STRATEGY_NAME: ContrarianDeviance

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy combines the Deviance Index (DI) with RSI analysis to identify high-probability contrarian entries in futures markets, specifically designed for 2x leveraged positions over a 3-6 month holding period.

## Core Strategy Components

**1. Deviance Index (DI) Signal**
- The DI measures the divergence between a futures contract's price movement and its volatility behavior
- A "contrarian" trend alignment occurs when price and volatility move in opposite directions relative to their historical relationship
- Specifically: when price declines but volatility is compressing (or price rises while volatility expands), the DI flags a contrarian setup
- This divergence often precedes mean-reversion moves as the market "corrects" the misalignment

**2. RSI Probability Filter**
- During "expanse periods" (periods of volatility expansion or range broadening), the strategy looks for a high statistical probability of low RSI readings (typically below 30-35)
- This is not a simple RSI oversold signal — instead, it's a probabilistic assessment that RSI is likely to reach or remain in low territory given current market conditions
- Low RSI + contrarian DI alignment = high-conviction long entry signal

**3. Position Sizing**
- Enter with 2x leveraged futures contracts (e.g., 2 micro or mini contracts, or 2x notional exposure)
- Leverage amplifies returns but also risk — strict stop management is essential

## Entry Rules

1. DI must signal contrarian trend alignment (price/volatility divergence confirmed)
2. Market must be in an "expanse period" (volatility expansion or range broadening detected)
3. RSI probability model must indicate high likelihood of low RSI values (below 35)
4. Actual RSI reading should confirm — enter long when RSI is in the low zone
5. All three conditions must align within a 5-10 day window for a valid setup

## Exit Rules

- **Primary exit:** DI reverts to neutral or confirms trend alignment (divergence resolved)
- **Secondary exit:** RSI reaches overbought territory (above 70) signaling exhaustion
- **Time-based exit:** Close position within 3-6 months regardless of P&L to avoid holding through regime changes
- **Profit target:** 15-25% gain on the underlying (30-50% on 2x leverage)
- **Stop loss:** 8-10% adverse move on underlying (16-20% on 2x leverage)

## Risk Management

- Maximum 2x leverage — never exceed this given the contrarian nature of entries
- Hard stop loss at 8-10% underlying move (tight given leverage)
- Position size: no more than 5-10% of total portfolio per trade
- Only take trades where all three signals (DI, expanse period, RSI probability) align — no partial setups
- Avoid holding through major economic events (FOMC, CPI, earnings for equity futures)
- Scale out: take 50% profit at first target, let remainder run with trailing stop

## Required Indicators

1. **Deviance Index (DI):** Custom indicator measuring price-volatility divergence
2. **RSI (14-period):** Standard Relative Strength Index
3. **Volatility measure:** ATR or realized volatility to detect expanse periods
4. **RSI probability model:** Statistical model (e.g., Monte Carlo or historical frequency) estimating likelihood of low RSI during current expanse conditions
5. **Trend alignment filter:** Moving averages or ADX to confirm the contrarian context

## Key Insight

The strategy's edge comes from the *convergence* of three independent signals: structural divergence (DI), volatility regime (expanse), and momentum probability (RSI). Each alone is weak, but together they identify high-probability contrarian reversals where the market is statistically likely to correct its price-volatility misalignment. The 3-6 month horizon allows mean-reversion to play out while avoiding long-term regime risk.
