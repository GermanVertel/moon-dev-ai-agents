STRATEGY_NAME: KineticRupture

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
The KineticRupture strategy is a volatility-driven breakout system that identifies and trades price ruptures from consolidation zones, using dynamic volatility thresholds to confirm genuine breakouts and filter out false signals. It combines volatility expansion measurements with price structure to capture explosive moves.

1. Key Strategy Components
- Volatility Regime Detection: Uses Average True Range (ATR) or Bollinger Band Width to identify periods of low volatility (consolidation) and high volatility (expansion).
- Dynamic Breakout Levels: Breakout thresholds are set relative to current volatility, not fixed price levels, allowing the strategy to adapt to changing market conditions.
- Momentum Confirmation: Requires a surge in volume or momentum (e.g., RSI or MACD) to validate the breakout.
- Trend Filter: A higher-timeframe moving average (e.g., 200 EMA) ensures trades align with the dominant trend.

2. Entry Rules
- Long Entry: 
  - Price breaks above the upper Bollinger Band (or a defined resistance level) after a period of low volatility (squeeze).
  - ATR expands above its moving average, confirming volatility surge.
  - Volume spikes above its 20-period average.
  - Price is above the 200 EMA (trend filter).
- Short Entry:
  - Price breaks below the lower Bollinger Band (or support level) after a volatility squeeze.
  - ATR expansion and volume spike confirm the move.
  - Price is below the 200 EMA.

3. Exit Rules
- Take Profit: 
  - Fixed risk-reward ratio (e.g., 2:1 or 3:1) based on ATR-multiplied targets.
  - Or trail stop using a Chandelier Exit (ATR-based trailing stop).
- Stop Loss:
  - Placed at the opposite side of the breakout level or at a multiple of ATR (e.g., 1.5x ATR) from entry.
- Time-Based Exit: Close position if price re-enters the consolidation zone, indicating a failed breakout.

4. Risk Management
- Position Sizing: Risk a fixed percentage of account equity (e.g., 1-2%) per trade, calculated using the ATR-based stop distance.
- Volatility Filter: Avoid trading during extreme volatility (e.g., news events) unless confirmed by volume.
- Max Drawdown Control: Reduce position size after consecutive losses or during high-volatility regimes.
- Correlation Check: Avoid multiple positions in highly correlated assets to limit exposure.

5. Required Indicators
- Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 standard deviations) – for volatility squeeze and breakout levels.
- Average True Range (ATR, 14-period) – for volatility measurement and stop/target calculation.
- Volume Moving Average (20-period) – for breakout confirmation.
- 200-period EMA – for trend direction filter.
- RSI or MACD – optional momentum confirmation.

6. Best Market Conditions
- Works best in markets transitioning from low to high volatility (e.g., after earnings, news events, or range consolidation).
- Avoid choppy, low-volume markets where breakouts frequently fail.

This strategy is designed to capture explosive moves while managing risk through volatility-adjusted stops and trend alignment.
