STRATEGY_NAME: KineticDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview**
KineticDivergence is a hybrid mean-reversion/breakout strategy that fuses Keltner Channel structure with RSI momentum divergence to time entries at statistically extreme price levels. The core edge comes from requiring BOTH a channel break (volatility-adjusted extreme) AND an RSI divergence (momentum exhaustion) before entering — this dual-confirmation filter reduces false breakouts common in single-indicator systems.

**Key Components**

1. **Keltner Channel (volatility envelope)**
   - EMA Period: 20
   - ATR Period: 10
   - ATR Multiplier: 2.0
   - Upper Band = EMA(20) + (2.0 × ATR(10))
   - Lower Band = EMA(20) − (2.0 × ATR(10))

2. **RSI (momentum oscillator)**
   - Period: 14 (standard)
   - Overbought threshold: 70
   - Oversold threshold: 30
   - Divergence detection window: 5–15 bars

3. **ATR (for exits)**
   - ATR Period: 14
   - Exit multiplier: 1.5× ATR from entry (fixed)

**Entry Rules**

*Long Entry (bullish divergence + lower channel break):*
- Price closes BELOW the lower Keltner Band (oversold extension)
- RSI makes a HIGHER LOW while price makes a LOWER LOW (bullish divergence)
- RSI is below 30 at the divergence pivot
- Entry on the close of the confirmation candle (first bullish close after divergence forms)

*Short Entry (bearish divergence + upper channel break):*
- Price closes ABOVE the upper Keltner Band (overbought extension)
- RSI makes a LOWER HIGH while price makes a HIGHER HIGH (bearish divergence)
- RSI is above 70 at the divergence pivot
- Entry on the close of the confirmation candle (first bearish close after divergence forms)

**Exit Rules**

- **Take Profit:** Fixed 1.5× ATR(14) measured from entry price
- **Stop Loss:** Fixed 1.0× ATR(14) on the opposite side of entry
- **Time Stop:** Exit after 20 bars if neither target nor stop is hit
- **Trailing option:** Once price moves 1.0× ATR in favor, trail stop to breakeven

**Risk Management**

- Risk per trade: 1–2% of account equity
- Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop distance in $)
- Max concurrent positions: 2 (to avoid correlated exposure)
- Avoid trading during major news events / low-liquidity sessions
- Skip signals if ATR is below its 20-period average (dead volatility = unreliable breaks)

**Required Indicators / Data**

- EMA(20) — Keltner midline
- ATR(10) — Keltner width
- ATR(14) — exit sizing
- RSI(14) — divergence detection
- Pivot high/low detection (fractal or swing logic, 3-bar lookback)
- Bar close data (avoid intrabar signals)

**Best Markets & Timeframes**

- Works best on 1H–4H charts in FX, crypto, and index futures
- Ranges and post-extension reversions are the sweet spot
- Avoid strong trending regimes (channel breaks become continuations, not reversals)

**Strengths & Weaknesses**

- ✅ Dual confirmation drastically reduces false breakout entries
- ✅ Volatility-adaptive bands self-adjust to market conditions
- ✅ Fixed ATR exits give consistent R-multiples for backtesting
- ⚠️ Divergence detection is subjective — needs strict pivot rules
- ⚠️ Can miss strong trend continuations (by design)
- ⚠️ Requires patience; signals are infrequent
