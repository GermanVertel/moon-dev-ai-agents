STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityFibDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines volatility regime analysis, Fibonacci price targeting, and momentum divergence to time entries and exits around implied volatility (IV) breakouts.

**Core Concept:**
The strategy waits for implied volatility to breach a defined resistance level (signaling an expansion in volatility regime), then looks for a strong bullish divergence to confirm a directional entry. Fibonacci retracement levels are used as profit targets, while volatility bars govern exits.

**Key Components:**

1. **Volatility Regime Filter (IV Breakout)**
   - Track Implied Volatility (IV) via VIX, IV rank, or an IV proxy (e.g., ATR percentile).
   - Define IV resistance as a rolling high (e.g., 20-period high of IV) or a fixed percentile threshold (e.g., 75th percentile of IV rank).
   - Signal triggers only when IV closes above this resistance — confirming a volatility expansion regime.

2. **Divergence Detection (Entry Trigger)**
   - Use RSI or MACD on price.
   - Bullish divergence = price makes a lower low while RSI/MACD makes a higher low.
   - Require "strong" divergence: minimum 2 swing points, magnitude threshold (e.g., RSI delta > 5 points), and confirmation on candle close.
   - Entry only when divergence coincides with or immediately follows the IV breakout.

3. **Fibonacci Retracement Targets**
   - Anchor Fib levels to the most recent significant swing high/low.
   - Use 0.382, 0.5, 0.618 as intermediate targets; 1.0 or 1.618 extension as final target.
   - Targets serve as take-profit zones, not entries.

4. **Volatility Bar Exit**
   - Exit when a volatility bar (e.g., ATR-based bar, or a bar whose range exceeds 1.5× ATR) prints in the opposite direction of the trade.
   - Also exit if IV collapses back below its prior resistance level (regime invalidation).

**Entry Rules:**
- IV closes above resistance level.
- Bullish divergence confirmed on RSI/MACD at a swing low.
- Enter long on the close of the divergence confirmation candle (or next bar open).

**Exit Rules:**
- Scale out at Fib 0.382, 0.5, 0.618.
- Final exit at Fib 1.0/1.618 or on opposing volatility bar.
- Hard stop: below the divergence swing low or 1.5× ATR, whichever is tighter.
- Time stop: exit if no Fib target hit within N bars (e.g., 20).

**Risk Management:**
- Position size based on ATR (volatility-adjusted sizing).
- Max risk per trade: 1–2% of equity.
- Reduce size when IV is already extended (IV rank > 90) to avoid buying peak volatility.
- Avoid trades during low-liquidity sessions.

**Required Indicators:**
- Implied Volatility (VIX / IV rank / ATR percentile proxy)
- RSI or MACD (for divergence)
- ATR (for volatility bars and stops)
- Fibonacci retracement tool (swing-anchored)
- Volume (optional confirmation filter)
