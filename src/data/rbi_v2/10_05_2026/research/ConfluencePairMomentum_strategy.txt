STRATEGY_NAME: ConfluencePairMomentum

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a market-neutral pair trading system that uses the Confluence Index (CI) to measure whether price action is trending (conforming) or mean-reverting (deviating) across two correlated instruments. It exploits divergence between the two legs to enter long/short positions, and exits when the legs re-harmonize.

1. CORE CONCEPT — CONFLUENCE INDEX (CI)
- The CI is a normalized oscillator (0–100) that quantifies the probability that current price action aligns with an established trend.
- High CI (e.g., >70): price is trending/conforming — trend-following behavior is favored.
- Low CI (e.g., <30): price is deviating — mean-reversion behavior is favored.
- Compute CI for both legs of the pair (Asset A and Asset B) using a rolling window (e.g., 50–100 bars) that blends:
  - Trend strength (ADX or slope of an EMA)
  - Directional consistency (ratio of up-bars vs down-bars, or R² of a linear regression on price)
  - Volatility-adjusted momentum (z-scored returns)
- CI = weighted blend of these components, then smoothed with an EMA (e.g., 5-period) to reduce noise.

2. PAIR SELECTION
- Choose two highly correlated instruments (e.g., ES/NQ, BTC/ETH, XLE/USO) with a rolling correlation > 0.7 over a 60-day lookback.
- Compute the spread or ratio: Spread = log(A) − β·log(B), where β is the hedge ratio from a rolling OLS regression (e.g., 60-bar window).
- Z-score the spread over a 20–40 bar window.

3. ENTRY RULES (DIVERGENCE)
- Long A / Short B when:
  - CI_A is HIGH (conforming/uptrend) AND CI_B is LOW (deviating/downtrend), AND
  - Spread z-score < −1.5 (spread is stretched to the downside).
- Short A / Long B when:
  - CI_A is LOW AND CI_B is HIGH, AND
  - Spread z-score > +1.5.
- Require a minimum CI gap (e.g., |CI_A − CI_B| > 25) to confirm genuine divergence between legs.
- Optional confirmation: CI crossover (fast CI crosses slow CI on one leg) in the direction of the trade.

4. EXIT RULES (HARMONY)
- Exit when the two legs "harmonize":
  - |CI_A − CI_B| < 10 (both legs return to similar confluence regime), OR
  - Spread z-score reverts to ±0.5 (mean reversion complete), OR
  - CI of the trending leg drops below 50 (trend exhaustion).
- Hard stop: exit if spread z-score extends beyond ±3.0 (divergence failure).
- Time stop: exit after N bars (e.g., 20) if neither harmony nor stop is triggered.

5. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position sizing: risk 0.5–1% of equity per pair trade, sized by ATR of the spread.
- Beta-neutral sizing: hedge ratio β ensures dollar-neutrality between legs.
- Max concurrent pairs: 3–5 to limit correlation risk.
- Daily correlation check: if rolling correlation drops below 0.5, close the pair and re-select.
- Drawdown circuit breaker: pause trading if strategy drawdown exceeds 8%.

6. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Confluence Index (custom): ADX, EMA slope, R² of linear regression, z-scored returns.
- Rolling OLS hedge ratio (β).
- Spread z-score (mean and standard deviation of spread).
- ATR of the spread for sizing.
- Rolling correlation coefficient.
- Optional: fast/slow CI EMAs for crossover confirmation.

7. EXECUTION NOTES
- Rebalance hedge ratio weekly or when β drifts >20%.
- Trade during liquid sessions to minimize slippage on both legs.
- Use limit orders on the spread; avoid entering on wide bid-ask spreads.
- Backtest across regimes (trending, choppy, high-vol) to validate CI thresholds.
