STRATEGY_NAME: VolumetricBreakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Bollinger Band breakouts with volume confirmation and momentum-based exits, creating a filtered approach that avoids low-conviction breakouts.

**Core Components:**

1. **Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 std dev)**
   - Upper Band = SMA(20) + 2 × StdDev(20)
   - Middle Band = SMA(20)
   - Lower Band = SMA(20) - 2 × StdDev(20)
   - The upper band acts as the dynamic breakout threshold

2. **Volume Filter (20-day average)**
   - Volume Spike Condition: Current Volume > 2 × SMA(Volume, 20)
   - Acts as confirmation that the breakout has genuine participation

3. **RSI (14-period)**
   - Used exclusively for exit signals
   - Exit trigger when RSI crosses below 70 (momentum cooling off)

**Entry Rules (Long Only):**
- **Primary Trigger:** Price closes ABOVE the upper Bollinger Band
- **Confirmation:** Same-bar volume exceeds 2× the 20-day average volume
- Both conditions must occur on the SAME closing bar
- Enter at next bar's open (or market close of signal bar)
- Position size: standard risk unit (e.g., 1-2% of equity)

**Exit Rules:**
- **Momentum Exit:** Close long when RSI(14) crosses below 70
- This captures the transition from overbought/strong momentum to weakening momentum
- Exit at next bar's open after the RSI cross signal

**Risk Management:**
- **Initial Stop-Loss:** Place stop at the middle Bollinger Band (SMA 20) OR 1× ATR(14) below entry, whichever is tighter
- **Trailing Stop Option:** Trail stop along the middle band to lock in profits as trend extends
- **Max Position Risk:** 1-2% of account equity per trade
- **Time Stop:** If neither exit nor stop triggers within N bars (e.g., 20), consider closing to free capital
- **Avoid Chasing:** Skip signals where price is already >3% above the upper band (extended breakout)

**Required Indicators:**
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2)
- Volume SMA (20)
- RSI (14)
- ATR (14) — for stop sizing

**Key Assumptions & Notes:**
- Works best in trending or momentum-driven markets (equities, crypto, futures)
- The volume filter is critical — it eliminates "fake" breakouts on low liquidity
- RSI exit at 70 (not 80) is aggressive; it exits earlier but may cut winners short in strong trends
- Consider a hybrid exit: RSI < 70 OR price closes back below upper band for a tighter trailing mechanism
- Backtest across multiple timeframes (daily, 4H) to validate robustness
- Watch for whipsaws in ranging markets — consider adding an ADX > 20 filter to confirm trending regime
