STRATEGY_NAME: NocturnalEngulf

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept**
This strategy exploits a specific two-candle overnight pattern — two consecutive "hanging candles" (small-bodied candles with long lower wicks, indicating buyers defending a level) — where the second candle is at least 10% larger than the first. The pattern is treated as a bullish accumulation signal, but ONLY when the broader market is in a range-bound consolidation phase. Despite the "Dark Cloud" label in the source, the trade direction here is LONG, so the pattern is interpreted as a wick-based absorption/defense signal rather than a bearish reversal.

**Key Strategy Components**
1. **Session Filter (Overnight Only):** Pattern must form during overnight/off-hours candles (e.g., futures overnight session, crypto low-liquidity hours, or pre-market). Day-session candles are ignored for signal generation.
2. **Hanging Candle Definition:** A candle with a small real body (upper + lower body ≤ 30% of total range) and a lower wick ≥ 2× the body, with the close in the upper half of the candle's range.
3. **Consecutive Sequence:** Two such hanging candles must appear back-to-back with no intervening candle.
4. **Size Expansion Rule:** Candle #2's total range must be ≥ 110% of Candle #1's total range (the "10% larger" condition).
5. **Consolidation Regime Filter:** Market must be range-bound — measured by ADX < 20 (or similar low-trend metric) AND price contained within a horizontal range where the range width is ≤ 1.5× ATR over the lookback window.

**Entry Rules**
- Wait for the close of the second (larger) hanging candle during the overnight session.
- Confirm the consolidation filter is active (ADX < 20, price inside range).
- Enter LONG at the open of the next candle (or on a break above the second candle's high for confirmation).
- Optional confirmation: the second candle's high must exceed the first candle's high.

**Exit Rules**
- **Profit Target:** Prior range high, or a fixed multiple (e.g., 1.5–2× ATR).
- **Stop Loss:** Below the lower wick of the second candle (the defended level), or 1× ATR below entry.
- **Time Stop:** Exit if the trade has not reached target by the end of the following session, since the edge is tied to overnight dynamics.
- **Regime Exit:** Close if ADX rises above 25 (range breaks into trend) against the position.

**Risk Management**
- Risk no more than 1% of equity per trade.
- Position size = (Account Risk %) / (Entry − Stop distance).
- Avoid entries during major scheduled news (overnight illiquidity can cause slippage).
- Limit to one trade per consolidation range to avoid over-trading false repeats.
- Require minimum range width so the reward:risk is at least 1.5:1.

**Required Indicators**
- Candle anatomy calculator (body %, wick ratios, total range).
- ATR (for sizing and stop placement).
- ADX (for consolidation/range regime detection).
- Rolling range high/low (for targets and range-width filter).
- Session/time filter (to restrict signals to overnight candles).

**Notes / Caveats**
- The "hanging candle" pattern is classically bearish at highs, so this long-only interpretation depends heavily on the consolidation context and wick defense — backtest carefully.
- The 10% size expansion is a relative filter; ensure candle ranges are normalized for volatility so it isn't triggered by noise.
- Liquidity during overnight sessions is thin — use limit orders where possible and validate fills on your specific market.
