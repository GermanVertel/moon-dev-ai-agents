STRATEGY_NAME: DivergentVolumePulse

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines volume-weighted momentum confirmation with oscillator divergence and volatility-based exit timing. It is designed for swing traders operating on intraday-to-daily timeframes (e.g., 1H or 4H charts), seeking high-probability entries after hidden or classic divergence and exiting when volatility compresses into a consolidation.

1. CORE COMPONENTS

A. Volume-Weighted MACD (VW-MACD)
- Standard MACD = EMA(12) - EMA(26) of price, with a 9-period signal line.
- Volume-Weighted variant: multiply each EMA's input by volume weighting, or apply VWMA (Volume-Weighted Moving Average) in place of standard EMA for the MACD fast and slow lines.
- Zero-line cross is the primary directional trigger:
  - Bullish: VW-MACD crosses from below zero to above zero.
  - Bearish: VW-MACD crosses from above zero to below zero.
- Volume weighting ensures signals are backed by genuine participation, filtering out thin-market false crosses.

B. Chande Momentum Oscillator (CMO)
- Formula: CMO = 100 × (Sum of gains − Sum of losses) / (Sum of gains + Sum of losses) over N periods (default N = 14 or 20).
- Range: −100 to +100.
- Used to detect divergence against price:
  - Bullish divergence: price makes a lower low while CMO makes a higher low.
  - Bearish divergence: price makes a higher high while CMO makes a lower high.
- Divergence must be confirmed within the last 5–10 bars relative to the VW-MACD zero cross.

C. Bollinger Bandwidth (BBW)
- BBW = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band, using 20-period SMA and 2 standard deviations.
- Used as an exit filter: a 2-day (or 2-bar on higher timeframe) consolidation is identified when BBW contracts for two consecutive periods and price closes within the middle 50% of the bands.
- Signals that momentum has exhausted; used to close positions regardless of P&L direction.

2. ENTRY RULES

Long Entry (all conditions must align):
1. VW-MACD line crosses above zero on the current or previous bar.
2. CMO shows bullish divergence with price over the prior 5–10 bars (price lower low, CMO higher low).
3. CMO is rising on the entry bar (slope positive).
4. Optional confirmation: price above 50-period VWMA or 200-period EMA for trend alignment.

Short Entry (all conditions must align):
1. VW-MACD line crosses below zero on the current or previous bar.
2. CMO shows bearish divergence with price over the prior 5–10 bars (price higher high, CMO lower high).
3. CMO is falling on the entry bar (slope negative).
4. Optional confirmation: price below 50-period VWMA or 200-period EMA.

Entry execution: enter at the close of the signal bar or on the open of the next bar. Use limit orders near the signal bar close to reduce slippage.

3. EXIT RULES

Primary Exit — Bollinger Bandwidth Consolidation:
- Exit the position when BBW contracts for 2 consecutive periods AND price closes within the middle 50% of the bands (between 0.25 and 0.75 of band width from lower band).
- This indicates a volatility squeeze and loss of directional momentum; close the full position at market.

Secondary Exits:
- Stop-loss: place at the most recent swing low (long) or swing high (short), or a fixed ATR-based stop (1.5× ATR(14)).
- Take-profit: optional 2:1 or 3:1 risk-reward, or trail using a 2× ATR chandelier exit.
- Time stop: exit if neither target nor stop is hit within 10 bars, to avoid capital stagnation.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT

- Position sizing: risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade. Calculate size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop).
- Maximum concurrent positions: 3–5 to limit correlated exposure.
- Avoid trading during major news events (FOMC, CPI, NFP) unless already in a trade with a widened stop.
- Volatility filter: skip entries when BBW is already in the bottom 10th percentile of its 100-period range (pre-squeeze), as breakout potential is limited.
- Divergence validity: require at least a 3-bar gap between the two CMO pivots to avoid noise.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS / SETUP

- VW-MACD (12, 26, 9) with volume weighting on fast/slow EMAs.
- Chande Momentum Oscillator (14 or 20).
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) with Bandwidth calculation.
- ATR (14) for stop sizing.
- Optional: 50-period VWMA and 200-period EMA for trend filter.
- Chart timeframe: 1H, 4H, or Daily. Confirm signals on one timeframe higher for multi-timeframe alignment.

6. TRADE MANAGEMENT NOTES

- The strategy thrives in trending-then-exhausting markets. It performs best when volume confirms momentum shifts and CMO divergence warns of weakening price action.
- False signals occur in choppy ranges; the BBW exit filter helps avoid holding through dead zones.
- Backtest across at least 3 years of data and multiple assets (FX majors, index futures, large-cap equities) to validate edge.
- Consider scaling out 50% at 1.5R and letting the remainder ride until the BBW consolidation exit.
