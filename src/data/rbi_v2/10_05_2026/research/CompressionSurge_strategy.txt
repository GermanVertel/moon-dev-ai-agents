STRATEGY_NAME: CompressionSurge

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
The CompressionSurge strategy is a volatility-expansion breakout system that waits for the market to "compress" (low Bollinger Band Width) and then "surge" (volume spike + close above upper band). It only trades when both a squeeze condition and a volume confirmation align, filtering out low-conviction breakouts.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA with 2 standard deviations (upper, middle, lower).
- Bollinger Band Width (BBW): (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band. Tracked over a 20-day lookback.
- Squeeze Condition: Current BBW is at its lowest value in the last 20 days (a 20-day low), signaling volatility compression.
- Volume Confirmation: Current bar volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-day average volume (150% spike).
- Breakout Trigger: Price closes ABOVE the upper Bollinger Band on the same bar that satisfies both squeeze and volume conditions.
- ATR Trailing Stop: 14-period ATR, exit when price trails 2× ATR below the highest close (or highest high) since entry.

2. ENTRY RULES (Long-only by default; mirror for shorts if enabled)
- Precondition A: BBW == lowest BBW over the last 20 bars.
- Precondition B: Volume ≥ 1.5 × SMA(Volume, 20).
- Trigger: Close > Upper Bollinger Band.
- All three must occur on the SAME bar (or allow Precondition A/B within the last 1–2 bars for flexibility, configurable).
- Enter at next bar open (or market close of signal bar, per execution preference).

3. EXIT RULES
- Primary: 2× ATR(14) trailing stop.
  - Track the highest close (or highest high) since entry.
  - Stop level = Highest Close Since Entry − (2 × ATR at entry, or dynamic ATR).
  - Exit when price closes below the trailing stop level.
- Optional secondary exits:
  - Close below the middle Bollinger Band (mean reversion exit).
  - Time-based exit after N bars (e.g., 20) if neither stop nor target hit.
- No fixed profit target — let the trailing stop capture the volatility expansion move.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position sizing: Risk a fixed % of equity per trade (e.g., 1%), calculated as (Entry − Initial Stop) where Initial Stop = Entry − 2×ATR.
- Maximum risk per trade is defined by the 2×ATR distance; size accordingly.
- Avoid entering if ATR is unusually large relative to historical range (prevents oversized stops).
- Limit concurrent positions and correlated exposure (e.g., max 1 position per symbol).
- Consider a hard stop at 2×ATR below entry in case of gap-downs that bypass trailing logic.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) — Upper, Middle, Lower.
- Bollinger Band Width (derived from BB).
- 20-day SMA of Volume.
- ATR (14).
- Highest Close / Highest High tracker since entry (for trailing stop).

6. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Lookback for BBW low: 20 bars (configurable).
- Volume multiplier: 1.5× (configurable).
- ATR trailing multiple: 2.0 (configurable).
- Signal bar must satisfy squeeze + volume + close-above-upper simultaneously for highest quality; relax to a 2-bar window if trade frequency is too low.
- Best suited to liquid instruments with reliable volume data (equities, futures, major FX with tick volume).
- Backtest across varying volatility regimes; the squeeze filter naturally avoids choppy, low-volatility whipsaws but can miss slow-grinding trends.

SUMMARY: CompressionSurge exploits the classic "squeeze then expansion" dynamic — low BBW signals coiled energy, a 150% volume spike confirms conviction, and a close above the upper band triggers entry. The 2×ATR trailing stop lets winners run through the volatility expansion while capping downside risk.
