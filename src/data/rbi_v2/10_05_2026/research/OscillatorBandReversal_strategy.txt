STRATEGY_NAME: OscillatorBandReversal

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept**
A counter-trend reversal strategy that hunts for exhausted bullish momentum at the top of the Bollinger Band. The premise is contrarian: when Stochastic Oscillator is overbought AND price is pushing against the upper band with a volume spike, the move is often a trap rather than a breakout. The strategy fades that trap by looking for a bearish reversal confirmation, then rides the mean-reversion move back toward the middle/lower band.

*(Note: The prompt says "enters long when SO is overbought" — this is internally contradictory. Overbought SO typically signals a SHORT setup. Below I detail the logically consistent short-side version, and flag the long-side variant at the end.)*

---

**1. Key Strategy Components**

- **Stochastic Oscillator (SO):** Momentum exhaustion gauge. Overbought zone = %K and %D both > 80. Cross of %K below %D in overbought zone = bearish trigger.
- **Bollinger Bands (BB):** Volatility envelope. Upper band = dynamic resistance. Price tagging/piercing the upper band signals stretched conditions.
- **Volume Spike:** Confirmation filter. Current bar volume > 1.5×–2× the 20-period average volume. Distinguishes genuine institutional activity from noise.
- **Candlestick Pattern:** Reversal trigger. Bearish engulfing, shooting star, or bearish pin bar closing back inside the upper band.
- **ATR:** Used for both stop placement and profit targets (volatility-normalized exits).

---

**2. Entry Rules (Short Side — the logically correct interpretation)**

All conditions must align on the same bar or within a 1–2 bar window:

1. **SO Overbought:** %K > 80 and %D > 80.
2. **Band Interaction:** Price high touches or pierces the upper Bollinger Band (2 std dev, 20 period).
3. **Volume Confirmation:** Volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-period SMA of volume.
4. **Reversal Candle:** A bearish candle (engulfing / shooting star / pin bar) that CLOSES back BELOW the upper band.
5. **Trigger:** Enter short on the close of the reversal candle (or on the next bar's open for confirmation).

**Long-Side Variant (if you truly want long entries):**
Mirror everything: SO *oversold* (< 20), price tags the *lower* band, volume spike, bullish reversal candle closing back above the lower band → enter long. This is the standard mean-reversion long setup.

---

**3. Exit Rules**

- **Primary Target (Resistance / Mean Reversion):** Middle Bollinger Band (20 SMA), or the lower band for shorts. Exit when price reaches the middle band.
- **Divergence Exit:** If SO prints a bullish divergence (price makes lower low, SO makes higher low) while short, exit early — momentum is turning against the position.
- **ATR-Based Targets:**
  - Take Profit 1 = entry − (1.0 × ATR) → scale out 50%.
  - Take Profit 2 = entry − (2.0 × ATR) → close remainder.
- **ATR-Based Stop:** Stop loss = entry + (1.5 × ATR). This adapts to volatility so tight ranges don't get stopped out unnecessarily.

---

**4. Risk Management**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk a fixed % of equity (e.g., 1%) per trade. Size = (Account Risk $) ÷ (1.5 × ATR). This normalizes risk across instruments and volatility regimes.
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** 2–3 to avoid correlated exposure (e.g., multiple FX pairs all shorting USD).
- **No Averaging Down:** One entry per signal. If stopped, wait for a fresh setup.
- **Volatility Filter:** Skip trades when ATR is in the bottom 10% of its 100-period range (dead market → band tags are meaningless).
- **Time Stop:** If trade hasn't hit TP1 or stop within 15 bars, close at market.
- **Session Filter:** Best on liquid sessions (London/NY overlap for FX, RTH for equities).

---

**5. Required Indicators**

| Indicator | Settings | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Stochastic Oscillator | 14, 3, 3 | Overbought/oversold + cross trigger |
| Bollinger Bands | 20, 2.0 | Dynamic S/R + mean-reversion target |
| Volume SMA | 20 | Spike detection baseline |
| ATR | 14 | Stop & target sizing |
| Candlestick engine | — | Reversal pattern recognition |

---

**6. Trade Logic Summary (Short)**

```
IF SO_%K > 80 AND SO_%D > 80
AND High >= UpperBand
AND Volume >= 1.5 * VolumeSMA(20)
AND BearishReversalCandle AND Close < UpperBand
THEN SHORT at close
  Stop = entry + 1.5*ATR
  TP1  = entry - 1.0*ATR (50%)
  TP2  = MiddleBand or entry - 2.0*ATR (50%)
  Exit early if bullish SO divergence OR 15-bar timeout
```
