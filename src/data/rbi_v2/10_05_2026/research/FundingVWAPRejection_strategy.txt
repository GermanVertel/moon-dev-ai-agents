STRATEGY_NAME: FundingVWAPRejection

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
This strategy exploits the mean-reverting tendency of over-leveraged long positioning in perpetual futures. When funding rates spike above +0.1%, longs are paying heavily to stay in position — a sign of crowded sentiment. If price is simultaneously rejecting a weekly VWAP cluster, the setup suggests trapped longs vulnerable to a squeeze. Entry is short; exit occurs when funding normalizes (crowd unwinds) or price liquidity-grabs below the daily lower Bollinger Band (exhaustion/stop-hunt completion).

**Key Components:**

1. **Funding Rate Signal (Primary Trigger)**
   - Monitor perpetual funding rate (8-hour interval on most venues).
   - Threshold: funding > +0.1% per period (annualized ≈ +109%).
   - Sustained readings (>2 consecutive periods above threshold) increase conviction.
   - Rising funding slope = strengthening signal.

2. **Weekly VWAP Cluster (Location Filter)**
   - Calculate weekly VWAP (anchored Monday 00:00 UTC) plus prior 2–3 weekly VWAPs.
   - Cluster = zone where 2+ weekly VWAPs overlap within ~0.3% of price.
   - Price must show **rejection** at cluster: wick into zone + bearish close on 1H or 4H chart.
   - Rejection candle should show upper wick ≥ 50% of candle range.

3. **Daily Bollinger Band (Exit/Exhaustion Reference)**
   - 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations on daily chart.
   - Lower band acts as liquidity-grab target zone.

**Entry Rules (Short):**
- Funding rate > +0.1% (current or last completed period).
- Price is within or just below a weekly VWAP cluster (within 0.5%).
- Confirmed rejection: 1H bearish engulfing, shooting star, or bearish pin bar at cluster.
- Optional confluence: RSI(14) on 4H > 65 (overbought) or bearish divergence.
- Enter short on close of rejection candle or on break of its low.

**Exit Rules:**
- **Primary exit (profit):** Funding rate normalizes to ≤ +0.02% (crowd unwound) — close 100%.
- **Secondary exit (target):** Price sweeps below daily lower Bollinger Band (liquidity grab) — close 100% or scale out 75%.
- **Partial scale:** Close 50% at 1× ATR(14) profit; trail remainder.
- **Time stop:** If funding remains elevated but price stalls > 3 days without hitting target, reduce size.

**Stop Loss:**
- Hard stop: 1.5× ATR(14) above entry, OR
- Structural stop: close above the weekly VWAP cluster + 0.5% buffer.
- If funding spikes further (>+0.2%) while price rises, exit immediately — trend strength invalidates thesis.

**Risk Management:**
- Position size: 1–2% account risk per trade.
- Max 2 concurrent shorts across correlated assets.
- Reduce size by 50% if BTC funding is also > +0.1% (market-wide leverage = higher squeeze risk both ways).
- Avoid entries within 30 min of major macro events (CPI, FOMC).

**Required Indicators/Data:**
- Perpetual funding rate feed (exchange API, 8H intervals).
- Weekly anchored VWAP ×3 (current + prior 2 weeks).
- Daily Bollinger Bands (20, 2).
- ATR(14) on 4H for stops and targets.
- RSI(14) on 4H (optional confluence).
- 1H/4H candlestick pattern recognition.

**Best Suited For:**
- Liquid perpetuals (BTC, ETH, top-20 alts).
- Late-stage rallies / euphoric phases where funding is stretched.
- Ranging-to-distribution markets, not strong trending breakouts.
