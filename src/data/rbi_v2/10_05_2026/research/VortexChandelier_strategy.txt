STRATEGY_NAME: VortexChandelier

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines the Vortex Indicator (VI) crossover with declining volume confirmation to identify trend reversals, while using Chandelier Exit for volatility-adaptive stop-loss placement. The core idea is that a VI crossover alone can produce false signals, but when it coincides with declining volume, it suggests weakening conviction in the prior trend—making a reversal more likely. The Chandelier Exit then protects profits by trailing stops based on ATR.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Vortex Indicator (VI+ and VI-): Measures positive and negative directional movement. A bullish crossover (VI+ crosses above VI-) signals potential uptrend; a bearish crossover (VI- crosses above VI+) signals potential downtrend.
- Declining Volume Confirmation: Volume must be lower on the crossover bar (or over a short lookback) compared to the prior trend's peak volume. This filters out strong continuation moves and highlights exhaustion.
- Chandelier Exit: A trailing stop based on the highest high (for longs) or lowest low (for shorts) minus/plus a multiple of ATR. It dynamically adjusts to volatility.
- Trend Reversal Focus: The strategy is designed to catch reversals, not trend continuations. It works best in ranging or topping/bottoming markets.

2. ENTRY RULES
- Long Entry:
  - VI+ crosses above VI- (bullish crossover).
  - Volume on the crossover bar is lower than the average volume of the prior N bars (e.g., N=10) or lower than the volume at the prior swing high.
  - Optional: Price is above a longer-term moving average (e.g., 200 EMA) to avoid counter-trend trades in strong downtrends.
- Short Entry:
  - VI- crosses above VI+ (bearish crossover).
  - Volume on the crossover bar is lower than the average volume of the prior N bars or lower than the volume at the prior swing low.
  - Optional: Price is below a longer-term moving average (e.g., 200 EMA) to avoid counter-trend trades in strong uptrends.

3. EXIT RULES
- Chandelier Exit for Longs:
  - Initial stop = Highest High over lookback period (e.g., 22 bars) - (ATR multiplier * ATR). Common multiplier: 3.0.
  - As price rises, the stop trails upward but never moves down.
  - Exit when price closes below the Chandelier Exit line.
- Chandelier Exit for Shorts:
  - Initial stop = Lowest Low over lookback period (e.g., 22 bars) + (ATR multiplier * ATR). Common multiplier: 3.0.
  - As price falls, the stop trails downward but never moves up.
  - Exit when price closes above the Chandelier Exit line.
- Optional Profit Target: Use a fixed risk-reward ratio (e.g., 2:1) or exit on opposite VI crossover.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position Sizing: Risk a fixed percentage of account equity per trade (e.g., 1-2%). Calculate position size based on the distance between entry price and initial Chandelier Exit stop.
- ATR Multiplier: Adjust based on market volatility. Higher multiplier (e.g., 3.5-4.0) for volatile markets, lower (e.g., 2.0-2.5) for calmer markets.
- Lookback Period: Chandelier Exit lookback (e.g., 22) should be tuned to the timeframe. Shorter for intraday, longer for swing trading.
- Volume Threshold: Define "declining volume" precisely. Example: Volume < 0.8 * SMA(Volume, 10) on the crossover bar.
- Avoid Choppy Markets: Use an ADX filter (e.g., ADX < 20) to avoid low-conviction ranging markets where VI crossovers are frequent but unreliable.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Vortex Indicator (VI+ and VI-): Period 14 (default). Can be adjusted (e.g., 10-20).
- Volume: Raw volume or volume SMA for comparison.
- Average True Range (ATR): Period 14 (default) for Chandelier Exit.
- Highest High / Lowest Low: Lookback period 22 (default) for Chandelier Exit.
- Optional: 200 EMA for trend filter, ADX for market condition filter.

6. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Timeframe: Works on any timeframe, but 1H to Daily is recommended for clearer volume patterns.
- Backtesting: Test on multiple assets and market conditions. Pay attention to slippage and commissions, especially on lower timeframes.
- Parameter Optimization: Avoid overfitting. Use walk-forward analysis to validate parameters like VI period, ATR multiplier, and volume threshold.
- Signal Confirmation: Consider waiting for the close of the crossover bar to avoid intrabar false signals.
- Chandelier Exit Calculation: Ensure the trailing stop only moves in the favorable direction (up for longs, down for shorts).

This strategy aims to capture reversals by combining momentum shift (VI crossover) with weakening participation (declining volume), while managing risk dynamically through volatility-based stops (Chandelier Exit).
