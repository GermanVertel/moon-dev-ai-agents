STRATEGY_NAME: VolumeBandDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Bollinger Band mean-reversion with On-Balance Volume (OBV) divergence confirmation, filtering out false band touches by requiring volume flow to disagree with price extremes.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA with 2 standard deviations (upper, middle, lower).
- OBV: Cumulative volume indicator used as a divergence detector against price.
- Core thesis: Price touching an extreme band is only tradeable when OBV diverges — signaling exhaustion rather than genuine continuation.

2. INDICATORS REQUIRED
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) — provides lower, middle (SMA20), and upper bands.
- OBV — computed from close-to-close direction and volume.
- Swing lookback (e.g., 5–10 bars) — to detect price lows/highs and compare with OBV at those points.

3. ENTRY RULES
LONG ENTRY:
- Price touches or pierces the lower Bollinger Band.
- Positive OBV divergence: price makes a lower low (or equal low) while OBV makes a higher low over the same lookback window.
- Enter long on close of the signal bar (or next bar open) once divergence is confirmed.

SHORT ENTRY:
- Price touches or pierces the upper Bollinger Band.
- Negative OBV divergence: price makes a higher high while OBV makes a lower high over the same lookback window.
- Enter short on close of the signal bar (or next bar open).

4. EXIT RULES
- Long exit: price touches or crosses the middle band (SMA20) — take profit.
- Short exit: price touches or crosses the middle band (SMA20) — take profit.
- Optional time stop: exit after N bars if middle band is not reached.

5. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Stop-loss for longs: below the recent swing low (or a fixed ATR multiple, e.g., 1.5×ATR below entry).
- Stop-loss for shorts: above the recent swing high (or 1.5×ATR above entry).
- Position sizing: risk 1–2% of equity per trade based on stop distance.
- Avoid trading during low-liquidity sessions where OBV signals are noisy.
- Skip signals if bands are extremely wide (high volatility regime) unless ATR-based stops are widened proportionally.

6. CONFIRMATION & FILTERS
- Require OBV divergence to span at least 3–5 bars for validity.
- Optional RSI filter: for longs, RSI < 40; for shorts, RSI > 60 — strengthens mean-reversion bias.
- Optional trend filter: skip counter-trend longs if price is far below a long-term EMA (e.g., 200 EMA) unless divergence is very strong.

7. LOGIC SUMMARY
- Band touch alone = noise.
- Band touch + OBV divergence = exhaustion signal.
- Target is the statistical mean (middle band), making it a high-probability, tight-target mean-reversion play.
