STRATEGY_NAME: HarmonicFiltration

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy identifies and trades false breakouts by combining harmonic pattern recognition (Gartley, Bat, Butterfly, Crab, Shark) with price action confirmation at key support/resistance levels. The core premise is that false breakouts often terminate at harmonic reversal zones (PRZ - Potential Reversal Zones), where Fibonacci-derived ratios align with failed breakout attempts.

KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS:

1. Harmonic Pattern Detection
- Scan for valid XABCD structures using Fibonacci ratios:
  - Gartley: B=0.618, D=0.786 (XA retracement)
  - Bat: B=0.382-0.50, D=0.886
  - Butterfly: B=0.786, D=1.27-1.618
  - Crab: B=0.382-0.618, D=1.618
  - Shark: B=0.382-0.618, D=0.886-1.13
- Use zigzag or swing pivot detection (minimum 5-bar pivots) to define X, A, B, C, D points
- Validate BC projection and AB=CD symmetry before confirming

2. False Breakout Identification
- Detect when price breaks a recent swing high/low or range boundary
- Confirm failure when price closes back inside the prior range within 1-3 bars
- Require the breakout extreme to coincide with a harmonic PRZ (D-point zone ±0.5 ATR)
- Check for divergence: RSI/MACD divergence at the breakout extreme strengthens the signal

3. Confluence Filters
- Volume: false breakouts typically show declining volume on the breakout candle
- Wick analysis: long rejection wicks (>60% of candle range) at the breakout level
- Higher timeframe alignment: PRZ should sit at a HTF S/R level (daily/weekly)

ENTRY RULES:
- Aggressive: Enter on the close of the reversal candle back inside the range
- Conservative: Enter on the break of the reversal candle's high (for shorts, low for longs)
- Only take entries when harmonic PRZ + false breakout + rejection wick all align
- Skip trades during major news releases (NFP, FOMC, CPI)

EXIT RULES:
- Stop Loss: 1.5x ATR beyond the false breakout extreme
- Take Profit 1: 38.2% retracement of the CD leg
- Take Profit 2: C-point of the harmonic pattern
- Take Profit 3: A-point (full pattern completion target)
- Trail stop to breakeven once TP1 is hit

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Risk 0.5-1% per trade (harmonic patterns have ~60-65% win rate historically)
- Maximum 2 concurrent harmonic positions to avoid correlated risk
- Move to breakeven after 1R profit
- Cut position if price closes beyond the X-point (pattern invalidation)
- Skip setups with PRZ width >1.5 ATR (too imprecise)

REQUIRED INDICATORS:
- ZigZag or Swing High/Low indicator (for XABCD detection)
- Fibonacci retracement/extension tool (auto-plot for pattern ratios)
- ATR (14) for stop sizing and PRZ width validation
- RSI (14) and MACD (12,26,9) for divergence confirmation
- Volume profile or OBV for breakout volume analysis
- Optional: Harmonic pattern scanner (automated XABCD detection)

TIMEFRAMES:
- Best on 1H, 4H, and Daily charts
- Multi-timeframe confirmation: 4H pattern + 15M entry trigger

This strategy excels in ranging or corrective markets where false breakouts are frequent, and struggles in strong trending conditions where breakouts tend to hold.
