STRATEGY_NAME: LiquidationCluster

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines liquidation heatmap analysis with Keltner Channel volatility breakouts to identify high-probability mean reversion opportunities near liquidation clusters.

**Core Concept:**
Price tends to gravitate toward areas with high long liquidation density (liquidation magnets) due to forced selling pressure. When price approaches these clusters with a volatility expansion trigger, it creates a setup for a mean reversion trade back toward the VWAP.

**Required Indicators:**
1. Keltner Channel (20-period EMA, 2.0 ATR multiplier)
2. 20-period VWAP
3. Aggregated Liquidation Heatmap data (long liquidation levels)
4. ATR (14-period) for volatility measurement
5. Volume profile or open interest data (optional confirmation)

**Entry Rules (Short Setup):**
1. Identify high-density long liquidation clusters from aggregated exchange data (Binance, Bybit, OKX combined)
2. Wait for price to rally INTO or NEAR a significant long liquidation cluster (within 0.5% of cluster center)
3. Confirm volatility trigger: current ATR > 1.5x the 20-period average ATR
4. Confirm Keltner Channel breakout: price closes above the upper Keltner Band
5. Enter short on the first candle that closes back INSIDE the upper Keltner Band (failed breakout / rejection)
6. Optional confirmation: bearish divergence on RSI or decreasing volume on the breakout attempt

**Exit Rules:**
1. Primary target: 20-period VWAP (mean reversion target)
2. Take partial profits (50%) when price reaches VWAP
3. Trail remaining position using the middle Keltner Band (20 EMA)
4. Time-based exit: close position if not at VWAP within 8-12 candles
5. Hard stop: above the recent swing high or 1.5x ATR above entry

**Risk Management:**
- Position size: 1-2% risk per trade based on stop distance
- Stop loss: placed above the liquidation cluster high + 0.3% buffer
- Risk/Reward minimum: 1:2 (entry to stop vs entry to VWAP)
- Maximum concurrent positions: 2 (avoid correlated setups)
- Avoid trading during major news events (CPI, FOMC) where liquidation maps become unreliable
- Reduce size by 50% if liquidation cluster is less than 1% away from entry (tight stop, high whipsaw risk)

**Key Filters:**
- Only trade when funding rate is positive (longs paying shorts) — confirms crowded long positioning
- Skip setups where liquidation cluster is >5% away from current price (too far, momentum may continue)
- Require cluster density to be in top 20% of recent 7-day liquidation levels
- Avoid counter-trend trades against a strong daily trend (EMA 200 slope)

**Invalidation:**
- If price reclaims the upper Keltner Band with strong volume, exit immediately (breakout is real, not a fake-out)
- If a new, larger liquidation cluster forms below current price, reassess — cascade risk increases
