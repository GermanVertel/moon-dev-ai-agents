STRATEGY_NAME: FractalChannel

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines fractal geometry with channel breakout mechanics to capture sustained directional moves while filtering out market noise through multi-timeframe fractal confirmation.

**Core Concept:**
Fractal channels are dynamic price envelopes constructed from recent swing highs and swing lows identified via Bill Williams-style fractal patterns (5-bar formations). Unlike static channels, these adapt to market structure by re-anchoring to the most recent valid fractals, creating a "staircase" that trails price during trends.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Fractal Identification**
   - Up-fractal: A bar with a higher high than the 2 bars on each side (5-bar pattern)
   - Down-fractal: A bar with a lower low than the 2 bars on each side
   - Only confirmed fractals (after the 2 right-side bars close) are used to avoid repainting

2. **Channel Construction**
   - Upper channel = most recent confirmed up-fractal high
   - Lower channel = most recent confirmed down-fractal low
   - Channel width = distance between the two; used as volatility gauge
   - Optionally smooth with EMA(3) on fractal points to reduce whipsaw

3. **Entry Rules (Long)**
   - Price closes above the most recent upper fractal high
   - Prior structure shows higher-highs/higher-lows (trend filter via 50-EMA slope > 0)
   - Channel width expanding (confirming momentum, not chop)
   - Enter on the close of the breakout bar or on a retest of the broken fractal level

4. **Entry Rules (Short)**
   - Mirror of long: price closes below most recent lower fractal low
   - 50-EMA slope < 0
   - Channel width expanding

5. **Exit Rules**
   - **Trailing stop:** Move stop to the most recent opposite fractal (longs → trail to latest down-fractal low)
   - **Channel break:** Exit if price closes back inside the channel by more than 0.5×ATR
   - **Profit target (optional):** 2× channel width projected from entry, or trail with Chandelier Exit

6. **Risk Management**
   - Initial stop loss = most recent opposite fractal ± 0.25×ATR buffer
   - Position size = (Account Risk %) / (Entry − Stop) — typically 1–2% risk per trade
   - Skip trades when channel width < 0.5×ATR(14) (consolidation filter)
   - Max 2 concurrent positions; no new entries within 3 bars of a stop-out (cooldown)

**Required Indicators:**
- Fractal indicator (5-bar Bill Williams)
- ATR(14) for stop buffers and volatility filter
- EMA(50) for trend bias
- Optional: ADX(14) > 20 to confirm trending regime
- Optional: Volume MA(20) to confirm breakout participation

**Timeframes:** Best on 1H–Daily for swing trading; 5M–15M for intraday with tighter ATR multipliers.

**Strengths:** Adaptive to structure, avoids fixed-level lag, excellent risk-reward on trending instruments (FX majors, indices, crypto).

**Weaknesses:** Choppy markets generate false fractals; requires strict trend filter and consolidation skip rules to remain profitable.
