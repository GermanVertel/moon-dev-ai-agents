STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityCompression

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview:**
This strategy exploits periods of low volatility (compression) as a precursor to upward price expansion. It combines a volatility filter (ATR vs. its moving average) with a long-term trend filter (200-day SMA) to identify high-probability long entries, then applies dual protective exits to manage risk.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Volatility Filter (ATR Compression):**
   - Indicator: 10-day ATR (short-term volatility)
   - Benchmark: 20-day SMA of the ATR (longer-term volatility baseline)
   - Condition: 10-day ATR < 20-day SMA of ATR
   - Rationale: When short-term volatility falls below its recent average, the market is "coiling" — often preceding a directional breakout. This filters out choppy, high-volatility environments.

2. **Trend Filter (Long-Term Direction):**
   - Indicator: 200-day Simple Moving Average
   - Condition: Price closes above the 200-day SMA
   - Rationale: Ensures entries align with the dominant long-term uptrend, avoiding counter-trend traps during bearish regimes.

3. **Entry Rule:**
   - Enter a LONG position when BOTH conditions are met simultaneously on a daily close:
     - 10-day ATR < 20-day SMA(ATR)
     - Close > 200-day SMA

**Exit Rules:**

1. **ATR Stop-Loss (Volatility-Adjusted):**
   - Set stop at: Entry Price − (2 × ATR at entry)
   - Purpose: Adapts stop distance to current market volatility; wider stops in volatile conditions, tighter in calm ones.

2. **Trailing Stop (Percentage-Based):**
   - Set trailing stop at 5% below the highest price reached since entry
   - Purpose: Locks in profits during sustained trends while allowing normal pullbacks.

3. **Exit Trigger:** Whichever stop is hit first closes the position.

**Risk Management:**
- Dual exit mechanism ensures both volatility-aware and fixed-percentage protection.
- Position sizing should be calculated so that the 2×ATR stop equals a fixed % of account equity (e.g., 1–2% risk per trade).
- Avoid entering if ATR is at an extreme low that could indicate illiquidity or a pending news event.
- Consider re-entry only after a fresh compression signal forms.

**Required Indicators:**
- ATR (Average True Range), period = 10
- SMA of ATR, period = 20
- SMA of Close, period = 200
- Trailing stop tracker (5% from peak)

**Best Suited For:**
- Trending markets with periodic consolidation phases
- Daily timeframe (swing trading)
- Liquid instruments (equities, ETFs, major FX pairs, large-cap crypto)

**Potential Weaknesses:**
- Whipsaw risk if price briefly closes above 200-SMA then reverses
- ATR compression can persist for extended periods, delaying entries
- 5% trailing stop may be too tight for highly volatile assets — consider ATR-based trailing as an alternative
