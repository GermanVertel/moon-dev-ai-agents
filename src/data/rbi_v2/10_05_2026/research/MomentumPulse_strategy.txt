STRATEGY_NAME: MomentumPulse

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines short-term momentum confirmation with a trend-following exit filter, creating a dual-condition system that captures strong upward thrusts while protecting against fading momentum.

**Core Components:**
1. **Entry Trigger:** 3-5 day RSI crossing above 70 (overbought momentum confirmation)
2. **Exit Trigger:** 5-day EMA crossing below 10-day EMA, confirmed by declining volume
3. **Instrument:** Futures contracts (liquid, trending markets preferred)

**Entry Rules:**
- Calculate RSI over a 3-5 day lookback period (shorter than standard 14-day for sensitivity)
- Enter LONG when RSI crosses above 70
- This signals strong short-term buying pressure — momentum traders are aggressively accumulating
- Only enter on confirmed close above threshold, not intrabar
- Optional filter: require price above 20-day EMA to ensure alignment with broader trend

**Exit Rules:**
- Monitor 5-day EMA vs 10-day EMA relationship
- Exit LONG when 5-day EMA crosses BELOW 10-day EMA
- **Critical confirmation:** Volume must be DECREASING on the crossover bar (or over 2-3 bars)
- Rationale: A bearish EMA cross with declining volume suggests genuine momentum exhaustion rather than a volatile shakeout
- If EMA cross occurs with rising volume, hold position — could be a false signal or continuation setup

**Risk Management:**
- Position sizing: Risk 1-2% of capital per trade
- Hard stop-loss: Place below recent swing low or 2x ATR from entry
- Trailing stop: Activate after 1.5R profit, trail using 5-day EMA
- Max hold time: 10-15 trading days if neither exit nor stop triggered (momentum decay)
- Avoid entering within 2 days of major economic releases (FOMC, NFP)

**Required Indicators:**
- RSI (period 3-5)
- EMA (5-period)
- EMA (10-period)
- Volume (with simple moving average for comparison)
- ATR (14-period) for stop placement
- Optional: 20-day EMA for trend filter

**Market Conditions:**
- Best suited for trending futures markets (commodities, indices during momentum phases)
- Avoid choppy/ranging conditions where RSI >70 produces frequent false signals
- Works well in high-liquidity contracts (ES, NQ, CL, GC)

**Key Insight:**
The genius of this strategy is the asymmetric exit logic — a standard EMA crossover would exit too early during healthy pullbacks, but requiring declining volume filters out noise and lets winners run through normal consolidation.
