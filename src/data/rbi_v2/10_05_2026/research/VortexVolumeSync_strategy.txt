STRATEGY_NAME: VortexVolumeSync

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview**
VortexVolumeSync is a multi-confirmation trend-following strategy that layers three independent filters—directional trend strength (Vortex Indicator), momentum with volume weighting (Volume-Weighted MACD), and volatility regime (Keltner Channel width)—to enter trades only when all three align. The core thesis is that high-probability trend entries occur when directional conviction, volume-backed momentum, and low-volatility compression coincide.

---

**1. Key Strategy Components**

- **Vortex Indicator (VI)** — Trend confirmation via directional pressure
  - VI+ = |High_t − Low_{t−1}|
  - VI− = |Low_t − High_{t−1}|
  - VI+ = Σ(VI+_n) / Σ(TR_n), VI− = Σ(VI−_n) / Σ(TR_n)
  - Standard period: 14

- **Volume-Weighted MACD (VW-MACD)** — Momentum with volume participation
  - Compute MACD line (12, 26 EMA of price) and Signal line (9 EMA of MACD)
  - Multiply MACD histogram by normalized volume factor: `VolFactor = Volume / SMA(Volume, 20)`
  - VW-Histogram = (MACD − Signal) × VolFactor
  - Optional: Use VWMA (volume-weighted MA) instead of EMA for MACD construction

- **Keltner Channel Volatility Filter**
  - KC Upper = EMA(20) + (2 × ATR(20))
  - KC Lower = EMA(20) − (2 × ATR(20))
  - KC Width = Upper − Lower
  - Baseline = SMA(KC Width, 20)
  - Volatility is "compressed" when current KC Width < its 20-day SMA

---

**2. Entry Rules**

**Long Entry (all must be true simultaneously):**
1. VI+ > VI− (bullish directional bias)
2. VW-MACD line crosses above Signal line (bullish crossover) AND VW-Histogram > 0
3. KC Width < SMA(KC Width, 20) — volatility below 20-day average
4. Volume factor > 1.0 (confirming above-average participation on the crossover bar)

**Short Entry (all must be true simultaneously):**
1. VI− > VI+ (bearish directional bias)
2. VW-MACD line crosses below Signal line AND VW-Histogram < 0
3. KC Width < SMA(KC Width, 20)
4. Volume factor > 1.0

---

**3. Exit Rules**

- **Primary exit:** Opposite VW-MACD crossover (MACD crosses Signal in reverse direction)
- **Trend invalidation:** Vortex lines flip (VI+ crosses below VI− for longs, vice versa for shorts)
- **Volatility exit:** KC Width expands > 1.5× its 20-day average (regime shift / breakout exhaustion)
- **Profit target (optional):** 2× ATR from entry
- **Time stop:** Exit if no favorable move within 15 bars

---

**4. Risk Management**

- **Stop-loss:** 1.5 × ATR(20) below entry (longs) or above entry (shorts)
- **Position sizing:** Risk 1% of equity per trade; position size = (0.01 × Equity) / (1.5 × ATR)
- **Max concurrent positions:** 3 to avoid correlated trend exposure
- **Trailing stop:** Once profit reaches 1× ATR, trail stop at 1× ATR
- **Avoid trading:** First 15 minutes after market open and last 15 minutes before close (volume/volatility noise)

---

**5. Required Indicators**

| Indicator | Parameters | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Vortex Indicator | Period 14 | Trend direction confirmation |
| MACD | 12, 26, 9 | Momentum base |
| Volume SMA | 20 | Volume normalization |
| Keltner Channels | EMA 20, ATR 20, mult 2.0 | Volatility regime filter |
| ATR | 20 | Stops, targets, position sizing |

---

**6. Rationale & Edge**

- **Vortex** ensures directional conviction before committing capital.
- **Volume-weighted MACD** filters out low-conviction crossovers that lack participation.
- **Keltner compression** avoids entering during volatility spikes where whipsaws dominate, and positions the trader for breakout expansion.
- The triple-confirmation design reduces trade frequency but materially improves win-rate and expectancy on trending instruments (futures, FX majors, liquid equities).

**Best suited for:** Intraday to swing timeframes (15m–4H) on liquid, trending markets.
