STRATEGY_NAME: CompressedBand Ignition

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a classic "volatility contraction → expansion" play. It waits for the market to go quiet (low Bollinger Band width + weak trend strength per ADX), then trades the first decisive breakout when volatility returns. Below is a complete specification.

──────────────────────────────
1. CORE CONCEPT
──────────────────────────────
Markets alternate between low-volatility consolidation and high-volatility expansion phases. When Bollinger Band width hits a 20-day low, price is coiled. ADX < 20 confirms there is no dominant trend (i.e., the squeeze isn't just a pause inside an already-strong trend). The exit/entry trigger is the expansion: bands widen AND price closes beyond a band on above-average volume.

──────────────────────────────
2. REQUIRED INDICATORS
──────────────────────────────
- Bollinger Bands (BB): period = 20, std dev multiplier = 2.0
- Bollinger Band Width (BBW): (Upper − Lower) / Middle, or simply Upper − Lower
- ADX: period = 14 (with +DI / −DI for directional bias)
- Volume: 20-period simple moving average (Volume SMA)
- ATR (optional): period = 14 for stops and position sizing

──────────────────────────────
3. ENTRY CONDITIONS
──────────────────────────────
SETUP (squeeze detection — evaluated each bar):
- BBW(20) ≤ lowest BBW value of the prior 20 bars (20-day low), AND
- ADX(14) < 20

TRIGGER (expansion confirmation — must occur AFTER a valid setup, within N bars, e.g., 10):
- BBW is now expanding (BBW > BBW[1]), AND
- Close > Upper Band (long) OR Close < Lower Band (short), AND
- Volume > 1.5 × Volume SMA(20) (above-average volume), AND
- Directional filter (recommended): +DI > −DI for longs, −DI > +DI for shorts

ACTION:
- Enter at next bar open (or market close of the trigger bar) in the breakout direction.

──────────────────────────────
4. EXIT CONDITIONS
──────────────────────────────
Primary exits (any one triggers):
- Price closes back inside the Bollinger Bands (close crosses back through the band that was broken), OR
- ADX rises above 40 (trend maturity / exhaustion), OR
- Trailing stop hit.

Trailing stop:
- Long: stop = Close − (2 × ATR(14)), ratcheted upward only.
- Short: stop = Close + (2 × ATR(14)), ratcheted downward only.

Time stop:
- Exit if neither target nor stop is hit within 20 bars (optional, configurable).

Optional profit target:
- 2R to 3R measured move, or exit when BBW peaks and begins contracting.

──────────────────────────────
5. RISK MANAGEMENT
──────────────────────────────
- Position sizing: risk 0.5%–1% of equity per trade; size = (Equity × Risk%) / (ATR-based stop distance).
- Initial stop: 2 × ATR(14) from entry.
- Max concurrent positions: 1 per instrument (avoid stacking correlated squeezes).
- Avoid trading during known low-liquidity windows (e.g., first/last 15 min of session, major news releases).
- Filter: skip signals when ADX is rising strongly above 25 before the squeeze (means squeeze is inside an existing trend, not a fresh range).
- Slippage buffer: assume 1 tick + spread on entries/exits.

──────────────────────────────
6. PARAMETER SUMMARY
──────────────────────────────
- BB period: 20
- BB std dev: 2.0
- BBW lookback for low: 20
- ADX period: 14
- ADX squeeze threshold: 20
- ADX exhaustion threshold: 40
- Volume multiplier: 1.5× SMA(20)
- Setup validity window: 10 bars
- ATR period: 14
- Stop: 2 × ATR
- Risk per trade: 0.5%–1%

──────────────────────────────
7. LOGIC FLOW (PSEUDOCODE)
──────────────────────────────
```
if BBW <= min(BBW, 20) and ADX < 20:
    squeeze_active = True
    squeeze_bar = current_bar

if squeeze_active and (current_bar - squeeze_bar <= 10):
    long_trigger  = BBW > BBW[1] and Close > Upper and Volume > 1.5*VolSMA and +DI > -DI
    short_trigger = BBW > BBW[1] and Close < Lower and Volume > 1.5*VolSMA and -DI > +DI

    if long_trigger:  enter_long()
    if short_trigger: enter_short()

# Exits
if long and (Close < Upper or ADX > 40 or stop_hit): exit_long()
if short and (Close > Lower or ADX > 40 or stop_hit): exit_short()
```

──────────────────────────────
8. NOTES / EDGE CASES
──────────────────────────────
- The "20-day low" should use strictly prior bars to avoid look-ahead bias.
- Confirm BBW expansion on the trigger bar itself, not just the setup bar.
- In choppy regimes, false breakouts are common — the volume filter and DI filter are critical to reduce whipsaws.
- Backtest across multiple instruments/timeframes; squeeze strategies typically perform best on liquid futures, FX majors, and large-cap equities.
