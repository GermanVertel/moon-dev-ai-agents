STRATEGY_NAME: CumulativeBandMomentum

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a momentum-based trend-following system that uses the cumulative product of Bollinger Band lower bands as its core signal generator, combined with volatility-adjusted stop-loss placement. Below is the complete breakdown.

---

**1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS**

- **Core Signal — Cumulative Product of Lower Bands:**
  - Calculate 50-period Bollinger Bands (typically 20-period SMA ± 2 standard deviations, but here the lookback for the band computation may be tuned; the "50-period" refers to the rolling window over which the cumulative product is tracked).
  - Extract the **Lower Band** value for each bar: `LB_t = SMA_t - (k * σ_t)` where `k` = 2 (standard) and `σ_t` is the rolling standard deviation.
  - Compute the **cumulative product** of the lower band values over the rolling 50-period window:
    - `CP_t = Π (LB_{t-i})` for i = 0 to 49
  - Because lower band values are prices (positive numbers), the cumulative product is always positive in raw form. To make the signal meaningful, the strategy likely uses the **cumulative product of normalized/centered lower bands** (e.g., `LB_t - SMA_t`, or `LB_t / SMA_t - 1`), which can flip sign.
  - **Long condition:** `CP_t > 0` → bullish momentum regime.
  - **Short condition:** `CP_t < 0` → bearish momentum regime.

- **Momentum Regime Logic:**
  - A persistently positive cumulative product indicates the lower band is consistently above its reference (i.e., volatility is compressing upward / price is trending up with support rising).
  - A negative cumulative product signals the opposite — downward momentum with expanding downside volatility.

---

**2. ENTRY RULES**

- **Long Entry:**
  - When `CP_t > 0` (cumulative product of lower bands turns positive) AND no existing long position is open.
  - Enter at the close of the signal bar (or next bar open to avoid look-ahead bias).

- **Short Entry:**
  - When `CP_t < 0` (cumulative product turns negative) AND no existing short position is open.
  - Enter at the close of the signal bar (or next bar open).

- **Position Flipping:**
  - If currently long and `CP_t` flips negative → close long and open short.
  - If currently short and `CP_t` flips positive → close short and open long.
  - This makes the strategy **always-in-market** (reversal system) unless a stop-loss triggers a flat state.

---

**3. EXIT RULES**

- **Primary Exit — Signal Reversal:**
  - Exit long when `CP_t` crosses below zero.
  - Exit short when `CP_t` crosses above zero.

- **Stop-Loss Exit:**
  - Stop-loss is placed **2 standard deviations away from the mid-point (SMA)** of the Bollinger Bands.
  - For a **long position:** Stop = `SMA_t - (2 * σ_t)` = the Lower Band itself (since k=2).
  - For a **short position:** Stop = `SMA_t + (2 * σ_t)` = the Upper Band.
  - Note: If the standard deviation multiplier for the stop equals the band multiplier, the stop coincides with the band. If the strategy intends a wider stop, use a separate σ multiplier (e.g., 2σ from midpoint while bands use a different k).

- **Stop-Loss Behavior:**
  - Stops are typically **trailing** — recalculated each bar using the current SMA and σ, so they ratchet in the favorable direction.
  - If price touches the stop intra-bar, exit at the stop level (or next bar open for realism).

---

**4. RISK MANAGEMENT**

- **Volatility-Adaptive Stops:**
  - Because the stop distance is tied to 2σ, stops automatically widen in high-volatility regimes and tighten in low-volatility regimes. This prevents premature exits during volatile trends while protecting capital in calm markets.

- **Position Sizing:**
  - Recommended: risk a fixed percentage of equity per trade (e.g., 1–2%).
  - Position size = `(Account Risk %) / (Entry Price − Stop Price)`.
  - This normalizes risk across varying volatility environments.

- **Always-In-Market Caveat:**
  - Since the system flips on signal reversal, whipsaws in choppy markets can cause frequent reversals. Consider a **confirmation filter** (e.g., require `CP_t` to exceed a threshold, or require 2 consecutive bars of the same sign) to reduce false flips.

- **Maximum Drawdown Guard:**
  - Optional: pause trading if drawdown exceeds a predefined threshold (e.g., 15%) until `CP_t` re-establishes a clean trend.

---

**5. REQUIRED INDICATORS**

| Indicator | Purpose | Parameters |
|-----------|---------|------------|
| Bollinger Bands (Lower) | Signal source | 20-period SMA, 2σ (adjustable) |
| Bollinger Bands (Mid/SMA) | Stop-loss reference | Same period as bands |
| Standard Deviation (σ) | Stop distance & band width | Same period as bands |
| Cumulative Product (rolling) | Momentum regime signal | 50-period window |
| ATR (optional) | Alternative volatility filter | 14-period |

---

**6. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES**

- **Data Handling:** Ensure lower band values are never zero or negative before taking the product — if using raw prices, normalize first (e.g., subtract the SMA or take log) to allow sign flips.
- **Warm-Up Period:** Requires at least 50 + band-period bars before the first valid signal.
- **Backtesting:** Test on multiple timeframes (1H, 4H, Daily) and asset classes (FX, equities, crypto) to validate robustness.
- **Slippage & Fees:** Because the strategy can flip frequently, transaction costs materially affect net performance — model them carefully.

---

**SUMMARY:** CumulativeBandMomentum exploits the sign of a rolling cumulative product of Bollinger lower bands to define trend direction, entering long on positive momentum and short on negative momentum, while using a 2σ-from-midpoint volatility stop to manage risk. It is a reversal-style, always-in-market momentum system best suited to trending markets with clear volatility regimes.
