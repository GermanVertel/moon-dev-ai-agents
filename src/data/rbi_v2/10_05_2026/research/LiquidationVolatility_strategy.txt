STRATEGY_NAME: LiquidationVolatility

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy exploits volatility-driven liquidation cascades by entering long positions when price has been pushed down to extreme levels relative to recent highs, then riding the mean-reversion bounce back toward equilibrium.

**Core Concept:**
The premise is that sharp downward moves of 2x ATR from a 20-day high often trigger forced liquidations (long squeezes), creating temporary oversold conditions where price snaps back. The exit targets the opposite extreme—a modest recovery to 1x ATR above the 10-day low—capturing the reversion without overstaying.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Volatility Measurement (ATR):**
   - Use a 14-period ATR (Average True Range) on daily or 4H candles.
   - ATR serves as the dynamic "distance" unit for both entry and exit thresholds.

2. **Reference Levels:**
   - **20-day high:** Highest high over the last 20 periods (rolling).
   - **10-day low:** Lowest low over the last 10 periods (rolling).
   - These asymmetric windows (20 vs 10) bias the strategy toward catching deeper pullbacks while exiting quickly.

**Entry Rules (Long Only):**
- Trigger entry when: `Current Price ≤ (20-day High) − (2 × ATR)`
- Confirmation filters (recommended):
  - RSI(14) < 30 (oversold confirmation).
  - Volume spike on the down move (liquidation signature).
  - No entry if price has already closed below the 20-day high minus 3× ATR (avoid catching falling knives in a true crash).

**Exit Rules:**
- Primary target: `Exit when Price ≥ (10-day Low) + (1 × ATR)`
- Secondary exits:
  - Time stop: exit after N bars (e.g., 5–10) if neither target nor stop hit.
  - Trailing stop: once price moves 0.5× ATR in favor, trail by 0.5× ATR.

**Risk Management:**
- **Stop Loss:** Place hard stop at entry price − (1.5 × ATR). This caps downside if the liquidation cascade continues.
- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1–2% of equity per trade. Size = (Account Risk) / (Entry − Stop).
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** 1–2 to avoid correlated exposure during market-wide selloffs.
- **Regime Filter:** Only trade when ATR is above its 20-period average (high-volatility regime)—the edge depends on volatility, not chop.
- **Avoid:** Earnings events, macro releases (FOMC/CPI), or when price is in a confirmed bear trend (e.g., below 200-day SMA).

**Required Indicators:**
- ATR (14)
- Rolling 20-day High
- Rolling 10-day Low
- RSI (14) — optional confirmation
- Volume (for liquidation spike detection)
- 200-day SMA — optional regime filter

**Edge Rationale:**
Liquidation clusters create predictable, short-lived inefficiencies. By anchoring entries to volatility-scaled extremes from recent highs, the strategy avoids overpaying for dips while the tight exit near the 10-day low locks in the reversion before momentum fades.
