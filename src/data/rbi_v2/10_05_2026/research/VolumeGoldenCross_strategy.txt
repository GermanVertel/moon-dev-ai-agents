STRATEGY_NAME: VolumeGoldenCross

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines a classic EMA crossover trend signal with volume confirmation and momentum-based exit management. Below is the complete breakdown.

**1. Core Strategy Components**

- **Primary Trend Signal (Golden Cross):** The 50-period EMA crossing above the 200-period EMA signals a shift from bearish to bullish trend structure. This is a lagging but reliable trend confirmation.
- **Volume Confirmation:** The crossover must occur during a "high-volume spike" — defined as the current bar's volume exceeding a threshold (e.g., 1.5x–2x the 20-period average volume). This filters out weak, low-conviction crossovers that often fail.
- **Momentum Exit Trigger:** The RSI (14-period) crossing below 70 signals that bullish momentum is fading after potentially being overbought, prompting an exit.

**2. Entry Rules (Long Only)**

All conditions must be satisfied on the same bar (or within a tight window):
1. 50-EMA crosses above 200-EMA (crossover bar).
2. Volume on the crossover bar ≥ 1.5x the 20-period simple moving average of volume.
3. (Optional filter) Price is above the 200-EMA to confirm the broader uptrend.
4. Enter long at the close of the confirmation bar (or on the next bar's open).

**3. Exit Rules**

- **Primary Exit:** Close the long position when RSI(14) crosses below 70 from above. This locks in gains as momentum cools.
- **Stop Loss (Risk Management):** Place stop loss below the most recent swing low or at a fixed percentage/ATR-based distance (e.g., 1.5x ATR below entry).
- **Take Profit Target:** Set target at 2:1 risk-reward relative to the stop distance. For example, if stop is 100 points away, target is 200 points above entry.
- **Whichever comes first:** Exit on RSI cross below 70, stop loss hit, or take-profit hit.

**4. Risk Management**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade. Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop Loss).
- **2:1 R:R Enforcement:** Always calculate stop and target before entry. If the RSI exit is likely to trigger before the 2:1 target, consider tightening the stop or skipping the trade.
- **Volume Spike Requirement:** Never enter without confirmed volume — this is the strategy's edge against false crossovers.
- **Avoid Choppy Markets:** The 50/200 EMA crossover is prone to whipsaws in ranging markets; use an ADX filter (>20 or >25) to confirm trending conditions.

**5. Required Indicators**

| Indicator | Purpose | Settings |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| EMA 50 | Fast trend | 50-period |
| EMA 200 | Slow trend | 200-period |
| Volume SMA | Spike confirmation | 20-period |
| RSI | Momentum exit | 14-period, level 70 |
| ATR (optional) | Stop sizing | 14-period |
| ADX (optional) | Trend filter | 14-period, >20 |

**6. Trade Logic Summary**

- **Setup:** Wait for 50-EMA to cross above 200-EMA with a volume spike.
- **Entry:** Long on confirmation.
- **Stop:** Below swing low or 1.5x ATR.
- **Target:** 2x the stop distance.
- **Exit:** RSI drops below 70, stop hit, or target hit.

**7. Notes & Caveats**

- The 50/200 EMA crossover is slow — expect fewer signals but potentially higher-quality trends.
- The RSI-below-70 exit may conflict with the 2:1 R:R target; backtest which exit dominates in your market/timeframe.
- Works best on trending instruments (indices, large-cap stocks, crypto majors) on daily or 4H timeframes.
