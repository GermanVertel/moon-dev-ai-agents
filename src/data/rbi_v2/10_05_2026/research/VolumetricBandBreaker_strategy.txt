STRATEGY_NAME: VolumetricBandBreaker

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
This strategy combines a volatility-adjusted VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) with volume confirmation to identify high-probability breakout entries. It fades back to the mean (VWAP) for exits, creating an asymmetric risk/reward profile.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Volatility-Adjusted VWAP Bands**
   - Calculate standard VWAP (typically anchored to session or rolling N-period)
   - Compute rolling standard deviation of price deviation from VWAP
   - Upper Band = VWAP + (k × σ_deviation)
   - Lower Band = VWAP − (k × σ_deviation)
   - `k` is the volatility multiplier (commonly 1.5–2.5, tunable per asset)

2. **Volume Surge Filter**
   - Compare current bar volume to a rolling average (e.g., 20-period SMA of volume)
   - Volume Surge = Current Volume ≥ (Volume_Multiplier × Avg Volume), typically 1.5×–2×
   - Surge must occur on the breakout bar to confirm conviction

3. **Entry Rules**
   - **Long Entry:** Price closes above the Upper Band AND volume surge is present
   - **Short Entry:** Price closes below the Lower Band AND volume surge is present
   - Optional: Require VWAP slope agreement (rising VWAP for longs, falling for shorts)

4. **Exit Rules**
   - **Primary Exit:** Price reverts back to VWAP (touch or cross)
   - **Stop Loss:** Fixed % or ATR-based stop beyond the breakout bar's extreme
   - **Time Stop:** Exit if neither target nor stop hit within N bars (avoids dead trades)
   - **Trailing Option:** Trail stop along the band as price extends

**Risk Management:**
- Position size based on distance from entry to stop (ATR-normalized)
- Max risk per trade: 0.5%–1% of equity
- Avoid entries during low-liquidity sessions (thin volume distorts VWAP)
- Cap concurrent positions to prevent correlated exposure
- Skip trades when bands are unusually wide (high volatility regime) or extremely narrow (chop)

**Required Indicators:**
1. VWAP (anchored or rolling)
2. Standard deviation of price-to-VWAP deviation (rolling)
3. Volume SMA (20-period recommended)
4. ATR (for stop placement and position sizing)
5. Optional: VWAP slope / EMA of VWAP for trend filter

**Best Use Cases:**
- Intraday trading on liquid instruments (ES, NQ, SPY, BTC)
- Trending or expansion days where volume confirms directional moves
- Avoid ranging/low-volume sessions where false breakouts dominate

**Edge Rationale:**
VWAP acts as the institutional fair-value anchor. When price breaks a statistically significant deviation band with volume confirmation, it signals genuine order-flow imbalance. The reversion-to-VWAP exit captures the mean-reverting tendency while the volume filter reduces false breakouts.
