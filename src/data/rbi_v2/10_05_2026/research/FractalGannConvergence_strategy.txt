STRATEGY_NAME: FractalGannConvergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines price fractals with W.D. Gann's bullish and bearish signal systems to identify high-probability long and short entries when both methodologies agree on the same price point.

**Core Concept:**
The strategy waits for a confluence event — a price fractal (a swing high or swing low) that aligns with a W.D. Gann bullish or bearish signal. This dual confirmation filters out weak setups and only trades when two independent analytical frameworks point to the same directional bias.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Price Fractals:**
   - Bullish Fractal: A 5-bar pattern where the middle bar has the lowest low, with two higher lows on each side. Marks a potential swing low / reversal up.
   - Bearish Fractal: A 5-bar pattern where the middle bar has the highest high, with two lower highs on each side. Marks a potential swing high / reversal down.
   - The fractal bar's high or low becomes the reference price level.

2. **W.D. Gann Signals:**
   - **Bullish Gann Signal:** Occurs when price breaks above a prior swing high or Gann angle (e.g., 1x1, 1x2) with a confirmed higher low structure — signals upward momentum.
   - **Bearish Gann Signal:** Occurs when price breaks below a prior swing low or Gann angle with a confirmed lower high structure — signals downward momentum.
   - Gann swing charts (2-bar or 3-bar swings) can be used to define the trend direction.

3. **Convergence Condition (Entry Trigger):**
   - **Long Entry:** A bullish fractal forms AND a bullish Gann signal is triggered at or near the same price level (within a small tolerance, e.g., 1–3 ticks). Both must agree on the same bar or within 1–2 bars of each other.
   - **Short Entry:** A bearish fractal forms AND a bearish Gann signal is triggered at or near the same price level.

**Entry Rules:**
- Long: Enter at the close of the bar where the bullish fractal and bullish Gann signal converge, or on a stop-limit order at the fractal high + 1 tick.
- Short: Enter at the close of the bar where the bearish fractal and bearish Gann signal converge, or on a stop-limit order at the fractal low − 1 tick.

**Exit Rules:**
- **Stop Loss (Long):** Place below the bullish fractal low minus a buffer (e.g., 1 ATR or 2–5 ticks).
- **Stop Loss (Short):** Place above the bearish fractal high plus a buffer.
- **Take Profit Options:**
  - Fixed R:R (e.g., 2:1 or 3:1).
  - Next Gann resistance/support level or Gann angle projection.
  - Trailing stop using a 2-bar Gann swing or ATR-based trail.
- **Exit on Opposite Signal:** Close if an opposing fractal + Gann convergence forms.

**Risk Management:**
- Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
- Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry Price − Stop Price).
- Avoid trading during low-liquidity sessions or major news events.
- Require the convergence to occur at a meaningful Gann level (e.g., prior swing, 50% retracement, or Gann angle intersection) to improve reliability.
- Skip trades when volatility is extreme (e.g., ATR > 2x average) to avoid false fractals.

**Required Indicators / Tools:**
- Fractal indicator (Bill Williams fractals or custom 5-bar fractal).
- Gann swing chart or Gann angle overlay (1x1, 1x2, 2x1).
- ATR (for stop sizing and volatility filter).
- Optional: Pivot points, Fibonacci retracement (Gann/retracement confluence).
- Volume filter (optional, to confirm breakout strength).

**Timeframe & Market:**
- Works best on higher timeframes (1H, 4H, Daily) where fractals and Gann signals are more reliable.
- Suitable for trending markets (indices, forex majors, commodities) where Gann swing structures are clean.

**Summary:**
FractalGannConvergence is a confluence-based reversal/continuation strategy that only enters when a price fractal aligns with a W.D. Gann bullish or bearish signal. This dual confirmation reduces false signals and targets high-probability swing entries with defined risk.
