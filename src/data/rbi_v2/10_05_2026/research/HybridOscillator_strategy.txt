STRATEGY_NAME: HybridOscillator

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy combines multiple technical indicators in unconventional ways to identify high-probability trading opportunities. The approach focuses on cross-validation between different indicator families rather than relying on single-indicator signals.

**Core Strategy Components:**

1. **Momentum-Reversion Hybrid**
   - Uses RSI divergence combined with Bollinger Band squeeze patterns
   - Entry signals occur when RSI shows oversold/overbought while price remains within bands
   - Requires confirmation from volume-weighted average price (VWAP) position

2. **Multi-Timeframe Confluence**
   - Analyzes 3 timeframes simultaneously (15min, 1H, 4H)
   - Requires alignment between short-term momentum and longer-term trend structure
   - Uses EMA ribbons on higher timeframes for trend bias

**Entry Rules:**

*Long Entry:*
- RSI(14) < 30 on 15min chart
- Price touches lower Bollinger Band (20, 2)
- VWAP position: price above VWAP on 1H timeframe
- MACD histogram showing bullish divergence
- Volume spike > 1.5x 20-period average

*Short Entry:*
- RSI(14) > 70 on 15min chart
- Price touches upper Bollinger Band (20, 2)
- VWAP position: price below VWAP on 1H timeframe
- MACD histogram showing bearish divergence
- Volume spike > 1.5x 20-period average

**Exit Rules:**

- Take Profit: 1.5x ATR from entry
- Stop Loss: 0.75x ATR from entry
- Trailing stop activates after 1x ATR profit
- Exit if RSI crosses 50 level against position
- Time-based exit: 24 hours maximum hold

**Risk Management:**

- Position sizing: 1-2% risk per trade
- Maximum 3 concurrent positions
- Daily loss limit: 3% of account
- Avoid trading during major news events (30min buffer)
- Correlation filter: No more than 2 positions in correlated assets

**Required Indicators:**

1. RSI (14-period)
2. Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 std dev)
3. VWAP (session-based)
4. MACD (12, 26, 9)
5. ATR (14-period)
6. Volume MA (20-period)
7. EMA ribbons (8, 21, 55)

**Unusual Combinations:**

- **RSI + Bollinger Bands**: Instead of using RSI alone for overbought/oversold, combines with band touches for stronger signals
- **VWAP as trend filter**: Uses VWAP not just for execution but as multi-timeframe bias indicator
- **MACD divergence + Volume**: Requires volume confirmation for divergence signals
- **ATR-based dynamic exits**: Adapts profit targets to current volatility regime

**Optimization Parameters:**

- RSI periods: 10-20 range
- Bollinger Band width: 1.5-2.5 std dev
- ATR multipliers: 0.5-2.0 range
- Volume threshold: 1.2-2.0x average

**Market Conditions:**

- Best performance in ranging-to-mildly-trending markets
- Avoid strong trending markets (ADX > 40)
- Optimal during high-liquidity sessions
- Works across forex, crypto, and liquid equities

This hybrid approach reduces false signals by requiring multiple indicator agreement while maintaining sensitivity to market reversals through the momentum-reversion combination.
