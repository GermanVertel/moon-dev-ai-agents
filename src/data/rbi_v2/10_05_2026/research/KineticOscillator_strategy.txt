STRATEGY_NAME: KineticOscillator

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy combines the Stochastic Oscillator and Relative Strength Index (RSI) to identify high-frequency breakout opportunities. It focuses on capturing short-term price movements by detecting momentum shifts and overbought/oversold conditions.

**Key Strategy Components:**
1. **Stochastic Oscillator:** Used to identify overbought (>80) and oversold (<20) conditions, as well as bullish/bearish crossovers.
2. **Relative Strength Index (RSI):** Used to confirm momentum and identify divergence or breakout signals (RSI >70 overbought, <30 oversold).
3. **Breakout Confirmation:** A breakout is validated when both indicators align (e.g., Stochastic crosses above 20 while RSI crosses above 30 for a long entry).

**Entry Rules:**
- **Long Entry:** 
  - Stochastic %K crosses above %D from below 20 (oversold).
  - RSI crosses above 30 from below.
  - Price breaks above a short-term resistance level (e.g., previous 5-period high).
- **Short Entry:**
  - Stochastic %K crosses below %D from above 80 (overbought).
  - RSI crosses below 70 from above.
  - Price breaks below a short-term support level (e.g., previous 5-period low).

**Exit Rules:**
- **Long Exit:**
  - Stochastic %K crosses below %D from above 80.
  - RSI crosses below 70.
  - Or a fixed profit target/stop loss is hit.
- **Short Exit:**
  - Stochastic %K crosses above %D from below 20.
  - RSI crosses above 30.
  - Or a fixed profit target/stop loss is hit.

**Risk Management:**
- **Stop Loss:** Set at a recent swing low (for longs) or swing high (for shorts), or a fixed percentage (e.g., 0.5-1% for high-frequency trading).
- **Take Profit:** Use a risk-reward ratio of at least 1:1.5, or exit on indicator reversal.
- **Position Sizing:** Use small position sizes due to high-frequency nature; risk no more than 1-2% of capital per trade.
- **Time Filter:** Avoid trading during low-liquidity periods (e.g., major news events).

**Required Indicators:**
- Stochastic Oscillator (default: 14,3,3).
- Relative Strength Index (default: 14).
- Short-term support/resistance levels (e.g., 5-period high/low).

**Additional Notes:**
- This strategy is best suited for volatile markets with frequent price swings.
- Backtesting and optimization are recommended to adjust indicator parameters and thresholds.
- Consider using a trailing stop to lock in profits during strong trends.
