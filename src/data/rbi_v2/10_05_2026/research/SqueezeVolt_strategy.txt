STRATEGY_NAME: SqueezeVolt

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview**
SqueezeVolt is a volatility-transition strategy that captures explosive directional moves occurring when a low-volatility consolidation (detected via Keltner Channel contraction) resolves into a volatility expansion (confirmed via Bollinger Bandwidth). The dual confirmation filters out false breakouts that occur when only one volatility measure signals a squeeze.

**Core Concept**
The strategy exploits the classic "squeeze" setup: Bollinger Bands (which use standard deviation) contract inside the Keltner Channels (which use ATR). When price breaks out of this squeeze zone with a volume surge, it often signals the start of a sustained directional move.

---

**1. Required Indicators**

- **Keltner Channel (KC):**
  - EMA period: 20
  - ATR period: 20
  - ATR multiplier: 1.5 (upper/lower bands = EMA ± 1.5 × ATR)

- **Bollinger Bands (BB):**
  - SMA period: 20
  - Standard deviation multiplier: 2.0

- **Bollinger Bandwidth (BBW):**
  - BBW = (Upper BB − Lower BB) / Middle BB
  - Track a rolling percentile or a short SMA of BBW (e.g., 20-period) to identify expansion.

- **Volume:**
  - 20-period volume SMA for surge comparison.

- **ATR (for stops):**
  - 14-period ATR.

---

**2. Squeeze Detection (Pre-Condition)**

A valid squeeze is defined as:
- **Contraction:** KC width is in its lowest 20th percentile over the last 100 bars (Keltner Channel contraction).
- **Compression:** Bollinger Bands are inside (or nearly inside) the Keltner Channel — i.e., BB Upper < KC Upper AND BB Lower > KC Lower.
- **Bandwidth Low:** BBW is below its 20-period average (confirming low volatility regime).

Track a "squeeze active" state flag. The breakout must occur while squeeze was recently active (within the last 5 bars).

---

**3. Entry Rules**

**Long Entry (first close outside Keltner):**
1. Squeeze was active within the last 5 bars.
2. BBW begins expanding: current BBW > previous BBW AND BBW > 20-period BBW SMA.
3. Price closes ABOVE the upper Keltner Channel (first close outside KC).
4. Volume surge: current bar volume > 1.5 × 20-period volume SMA.
5. Optional filter: price also closes above upper Bollinger Band for confirmation.

**Short Entry (mirror):**
1. Squeeze active within last 5 bars.
2. BBW expanding.
3. Price closes BELOW the lower Keltner Channel.
4. Volume > 1.5 × volume SMA.

**Entry execution:** Enter at the close of the breakout bar or on the next bar's open.

---

**4. Exit Rules**

- **Initial Stop-Loss:** 
  - Long: entry − (1.5 × ATR(14)), or below the Keltner midline, whichever is tighter.
  - Short: entry + (1.5 × ATR(14)).

- **Profit Target 1 (partial):** 1.5R — scale out 50%.
- **Profit Target 2:** Trail using Keltner midline (EMA 20) or a 2× ATR chandelier stop.

- **Time Stop:** Exit if no favorable move within 10 bars and squeeze condition has re-formed.

- **Volatility Reversal Exit:** Close position if BBW contracts sharply (volatility dying) after a breakout, indicating a failed move.

---

**5. Risk Management**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk 1% of equity per trade; size = (0.01 × Equity) / (entry − stop).
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** 1–2 to avoid correlated volatility trades.
- **Avoid Choppy Regimes:** Skip signals when ADX < 20 (no trend) unless breakout is very strong.
- **Slippage Buffer:** Account for slippage on breakout bars since volume surges often cause gaps.
- **No Re-entry:** After a stop-out, wait for a fresh squeeze to form before re-entering the same direction.

---

**6. Confirmation Checklist (all must be TRUE for entry)**

| Condition | Long | Short |
|---|---|---|
| Squeeze active (last 5 bars) | ✔ | ✔ |
| BBW expanding | ✔ | ✔ |
| First close outside KC | Above upper | Below lower |
| Volume > 1.5× SMA | ✔ | ✔ |
| Trend filter (ADX > 20) | Optional | Optional |

---

**7. Why It Works**
The dual confirmation eliminates the biggest weakness of single-indicator squeeze systems: false breakouts. Keltner contraction confirms the *setup* (energy stored), while Bollinger Bandwidth expansion confirms the *trigger* (energy released). The volume surge validates real participation rather than a noise-driven spike, giving SqueezeVolt a higher win-rate-to-false-signal ratio than either indicator used alone.
