STRATEGY_NAME: LiquidityReversal

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept**
This strategy capitalizes on stop-hunt reversals by identifying high-liquidation zones where leveraged traders are likely to get liquidated, then entering counter-trend positions when price approaches these zones with confirming VWAP divergence, and exiting when OBV (On-Balance Volume) flips trend direction.

**Key Strategy Components**

1. **Liquidation Zone Mapping**
   - Aggregate cumulative liquidation levels from order book data (heatmap)
   - Identify clusters where large concentrations of stop-losses and liquidation orders sit
   - Mark these as "magnet zones" — areas where price is likely to spike into before reversing
   - Prioritize zones with the highest cumulative liquidation density (typically above/below recent swing highs/lows)

2. **VWAP Divergence Confirmation**
   - Calculate VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) on intraday basis
   - Look for divergence between price action and VWAP:
     - **Bullish setup**: Price makes a lower low into a liquidation zone below, but VWAP holds above prior VWAP low (positive divergence)
     - **Bearish setup**: Price makes a higher high into a liquidation zone above, but VWAP fails to make a new high (negative divergence)
   - Divergence signals that the move into the liquidation zone is exhausting

3. **OBV Trend Flip (Exit Signal)**
   - Track OBV with a short EMA (e.g., 9-period) for trend direction
   - Exit long when OBV EMA flips bearish
   - Exit short when OBV EMA flips bullish
   - This captures the reversal momentum as volume confirms the new direction

**Entry Rules**

*Long Entry (Stop-Hunt Below):*
- Price enters a high-liquidation zone located BELOW current price
- Bullish VWAP divergence confirmed (price lower low, VWAP higher low)
- Wait for a rejection candle (wick/hammer) or bullish engulfing at the zone
- Enter long on the close of the confirmation candle

*Short Entry (Stop-Hunt Above):*
- Price enters a high-liquidation zone located ABOVE current price
- Bearish VWAP divergence confirmed (price higher high, VWAP lower high)
- Wait for a rejection candle (shooting star) or bearish engulfing at the zone
- Enter short on the close of the confirmation candle

**Exit Rules**

- **Primary exit**: OBV EMA trend flip (9-period EMA on OBV)
- **Stop-loss**: Place just beyond the liquidation zone (e.g., 0.5–1 ATR past the zone edge) — if price breaks through, the stop-hunt thesis is invalidated
- **Take-profit (optional)**: Scale out at the opposite side of the range or at the next liquidation cluster in the reversal direction
- **Time stop**: Exit if no OBV flip within N bars (e.g., 20 bars) and price stalls

**Risk Management**

- Risk per trade: 1–2% of account equity
- Position sizing based on distance from entry to invalidation stop
- Avoid entering during high-impact news events (liquidation zones can be overrun)
- Require minimum liquidation density threshold (filter out thin zones)
- Max 2–3 concurrent positions to avoid correlated exposure
- Trail stop to breakeven once price moves 1R in favor

**Required Indicators & Data**

1. **Liquidation Heatmap Data** — cumulative liquidation levels from order book/derivatives feed (e.g., Coinglass, Hyblock, or exchange liquidation APIs)
2. **VWAP** — intraday anchored VWAP (session or rolling)
3. **OBV (On-Balance Volume)** — with 9-period EMA overlay
4. **ATR** — for stop placement and position sizing
5. **Volume** — to validate rejection candles

**Best Timeframes**
- Entry timeframe: 5m–15m (for precision at zones)
- Context timeframe: 1H–4H (to identify major liquidation clusters)
- Best suited for crypto perpetuals and highly liquid futures where liquidation data is transparent

**Edge Rationale**
Market makers and large players often push price into liquidation clusters to trigger cascading stop-outs, then reverse. By waiting for VWAP divergence (exhaustion proof) and entering only after rejection confirmation, the strategy avoids being the liquidity being hunted — instead, it rides the reversal that follows. OBV flip provides an objective momentum-based exit rather than arbitrary targets.
