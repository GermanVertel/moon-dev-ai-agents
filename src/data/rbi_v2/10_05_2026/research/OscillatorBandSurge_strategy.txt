STRATEGY_NAME: OscillatorBandSurge

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy merges RSI momentum with Bollinger Band volatility structure to identify high-conviction long entries. Unlike traditional RSI usage (overbought/oversold), this approach treats RSI as a momentum oscillator whose own Bollinger Bands define statistically significant momentum extremes.

**Core Concept:**
Apply Bollinger Bands to the RSI indicator itself (not just price), then require alignment with price-based Bollinger Bands. This dual-confirmation filters false momentum signals.

**Required Indicators:**
1. **Price Bollinger Bands** (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations)
   - Upper Band, Middle Band (SMA 20), Lower Band
2. **RSI** (14-period)
3. **RSI Bollinger Bands** (applied to RSI line, 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations)
   - RSI Upper Band, RSI Middle, RSI Lower Band

**Entry Rules (Long):**
Both conditions must be true on the same bar (or within 1-2 bars for flexibility):
1. **RSI Momentum Breakout:** RSI line crosses ABOVE its upper Bollinger Band (RSI > RSI_UpperBand after being below)
2. **Price Confirmation:** Close price breaks ABOVE the price Middle Bollinger Band (20-SMA)

Optional filter: RSI value should be > 50 at signal (ensures bullish momentum regime).

**Exit Rules:**
- **Primary Exit:** Close below the price Middle Bollinger Band
- **Momentum Exit:** RSI crosses back below its RSI Middle Band (momentum fading)
- **Stop Loss:** Below the most recent swing low OR below the price Lower Bollinger Band (whichever is tighter)
- **Take Profit:** At price Upper Bollinger Band, or trail with a 2×ATR stop

**Risk Management:**
- Position size: Risk no more than 1-2% of equity per trade
- ATR-based stop (2× ATR(14)) to adapt to volatility
- Avoid entries when price Upper Band and Lower Band are extremely narrow (squeeze) unless breakout confirms with volume
- Max 1 open position per instrument to avoid clustering

**Confirmation Enhancements:**
- Volume above 20-period average on breakout bar
- RSI upper band breakout should be a fresh cross, not a prolonged stay above (avoid chasing)
- Higher timeframe trend alignment (e.g., price above 200-EMA on daily)

**Best Suited For:**
Trending or momentum-driven markets (equities, crypto, indices) on 1H–Daily timeframes. Avoid ranging/choppy markets where RSI band breakouts frequently whipsaw.

**Key Insight:**
The RSI-Bollinger combination captures the moment when momentum itself becomes statistically extreme AND price confirms by reclaiming its own mean. This dual-band alignment significantly reduces the false signals typical of RSI-only or price-BB-only systems.
