STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilitySqueeze

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy exploits the convergence of volatility compression and volume expansion — a classic precursor to explosive directional moves. It uses ATR as a volatility proxy, Bollinger Bands to define statistically extreme low-volatility zones, and volume as the confirmation trigger.

**Core Concept:**
When market volatility (ATR) contracts below its own lower Bollinger Band, it signals a "squeeze" — the market is coiling. A simultaneous volume surge (200%+ of 10-day average) indicates that the squeeze is about to resolve with force. Direction is inferred from price action relative to the ATR/BB context and candle structure.

---

**Required Indicators:**
1. **ATR (14-period)** — primary volatility measure
2. **Bollinger Bands (20-period, 2 std dev)** applied to the ATR series itself (not price)
   - Upper Band, Middle (SMA20 of ATR), Lower Band
3. **Volume SMA (10-period)** — baseline for surge detection
4. Optional: Price EMA (20) or prior candle close for directional bias

---

**Entry Rules:**

*Long Entry:*
- ATR(14) < Lower Bollinger Band of ATR(20, 2σ)
- Current volume ≥ 200% of 10-day average volume
- Price closes above the prior candle's high (bullish resolution of squeeze)
- Optional confirmation: price above 20-period EMA

*Short Entry:*
- ATR(14) < Lower Bollinger Band of ATR(20, 2σ)
- Current volume ≥ 200% of 10-day average volume
- Price closes below the prior candle's low (bearish resolution of squeeze)
- Optional confirmation: price below 20-period EMA

Both conditions (squeeze + volume surge) must be true on the same bar.

---

**Exit Rules:**
- Close the position when ATR(14) crosses back above its 20-period moving average (the middle Bollinger Band of ATR)
- This signals volatility has normalized and the expansion phase is ending
- Exit on the bar close where ATR > SMA20(ATR)

---

**Risk Management:**
- **Stop Loss:** Place at 1.5× current ATR(14) from entry, or below/above the squeeze range's extreme (whichever is tighter)
- **Position Sizing:** Risk 1–2% of equity per trade; adjust size inversely to ATR (smaller size when ATR is elevated)
- **Time Stop:** If ATR fails to breach its 20-day MA within 15 bars, exit at market — the expansion thesis has failed
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** Limit to avoid correlated volatility trades across instruments
- **Avoid:** Low-liquidity sessions, news events that distort volume readings (earnings, CPI, FOMC)

---

**Key Edge & Caveats:**
- The edge comes from the statistical rarity of ATR trading below its lower BB *simultaneously* with a volume spike — this combination filters out most false squeezes
- Volume surge must be genuine (check for one-off block trades)
- Works best on liquid instruments with reliable ATR/volume data (futures, large-cap equities, major FX pairs)
- Backtest across regimes — the strategy will underperform in low-volatility trending markets where squeezes resolve weakly
