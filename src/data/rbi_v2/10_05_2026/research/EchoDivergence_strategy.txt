STRATEGY_NAME: EchoDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview**
EchoDivergence is a bearish trend-following short strategy that combines MACD bearish divergence, Bollinger Band breakdown, and volume confirmation to time entries, with RSI divergence and ATR-based breakeven as exit triggers. The strategy "echoes" momentum weakness across multiple indicators before committing to a short.

**Core Components**

1. **MACD Bearish Divergence**
   - Price makes a higher high while MACD line (or histogram) makes a lower high.
   - Identifies weakening bullish momentum despite rising price — a leading reversal signal.
   - Confirm divergence on the most recent two swing highs within a defined lookback (e.g., 20–50 bars).

2. **Bollinger Band Breakdown**
   - Price must close below the lower Bollinger Band (typically 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations).
   - Signals a volatility-driven downside expansion and confirms the divergence is being acted upon.

3. **Decreasing Volume**
   - Volume on the breakdown bar(s) must be lower than the prior rally's volume.
   - Falling volume on the upside rally (or on the breakdown) confirms exhaustion of buyers and lack of conviction behind the prior move.

4. **RSI Divergence Confirmation (Exit)**
   - Close the short when RSI shows bullish divergence (price makes lower low, RSI makes higher low), signaling downside momentum is fading.

5. **ATR Breakeven Stop**
   - Once the trade moves favorably by a defined ATR multiple (e.g., 1× ATR), move the stop to breakeven to protect capital.

**Entry Rules (Short)**
- MACD bearish divergence detected on recent swing highs.
- Price closes below the lower Bollinger Band.
- Volume on the breakdown is decreasing versus the prior up-leg.
- Optional filter: RSI < 50 or trending down to avoid counter-trend whipsaws.
- Enter short at the close of the confirmation bar (or on a retest of the lower band).

**Exit Rules**
- **Primary:** Bullish RSI divergence appears → close short.
- **Protective:** Stop-loss placed above the most recent swing high or at an ATR-based distance (e.g., 1.5× ATR).
- **Breakeven:** When profit reaches 1× ATR, move stop to entry price.
- **Optional target:** Lower Bollinger Band extension or a fixed risk-reward (e.g., 2R).

**Risk Management**
- Position size based on ATR: risk no more than 1–2% of equity per trade.
- Initial stop = entry + (1.5 × ATR).
- Trail stop at breakeven once 1× ATR profit is reached.
- Avoid trading during low-liquidity sessions or major news events that distort volume.

**Required Indicators**
- MACD (12, 26, 9) — for divergence detection.
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) — for breakdown confirmation.
- Volume — for decreasing-volume filter.
- RSI (14) — for exit divergence.
- ATR (14) — for stop placement and breakeven logic.

**Best Conditions**
- Works best in ranging-to-topping markets where momentum fades after an extended rally.
- Avoid strong trending bull markets where Bollinger breakdowns are quickly bought back.
- Ideal on liquid instruments (major FX pairs, large-cap equities, index futures) with reliable volume data.
