STRATEGY_NAME: ClusterBreakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
This strategy combines structural trend line analysis with volume/price cluster confirmation to identify high-probability breakout entries. Rather than entering on the initial trend line break (which often produces false signals), it waits for the price to decisively break through a consolidation cluster formed by 20 consecutive bars, providing confirmation that the breakout has genuine momentum behind it.

**1. Key Strategy Components:**

- **Ascending Trend Line Construction:** Draw a trend line connecting at least 2-3 higher lows over a defined lookback period. The line must have a positive slope and be validated by price respecting it (touching but not significantly closing below it).

- **20-Bar High-Low Cluster:** Identify a consolidation zone where the highest high and lowest low of the most recent 20 consecutive bars form a tight range. This cluster represents a "coiling" phase where the market is building energy before a breakout. The cluster width should ideally be less than 3-5% of price to qualify as a valid setup.

- **Breakout Confirmation Trigger:** Entry occurs only when BOTH conditions align:
  - Price breaks above the ascending trend line
  - Price closes above the highest high of the 20-bar cluster
  - This dual confirmation filters out weak trend line breaks

**2. Entry Rules (Long Only):**

- **Precondition:** An established ascending trend line with minimum 2 touchpoints
- **Cluster Formation:** 20 consecutive bars with a definable high-low range
- **Entry Trigger:** 
  - Candle closes above the cluster high AND above the trend line
  - Optional: Volume on breakout bar > 1.5x average volume of last 20 bars
- **Entry Price:** Market order at close of breakout candle, or stop-buy at cluster high + 1 tick

**3. Exit Rules:**

- **Primary Exit (Profit Target):** 1% retracement level from the highest high reached after entry. Once price advances, trail the exit at 1% below the peak.
- **Stop Loss Exit:** Place initial stop at the cluster's low or 1% below entry, whichever is tighter.
- **Time-Based Exit:** If neither target nor stop is hit within 15 bars, exit at market (optional filter to avoid stagnation).

**4. Risk Management:**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade
- **Risk-to-Reward:** Minimum 1.5:1 ratio; if cluster is too wide (stop too far), skip the trade
- **Trailing Stop:** Activate the 1% trailing retracement only after price moves 1% in favor
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** 1-2 to avoid correlated exposure
- **Volatility Filter:** Avoid trading when ATR is in the bottom 20th percentile (no momentum) or top 10th percentile (excessive whipsaw)

**5. Required Indicators:**

- **Trend Line Tool:** Manual or algorithmic slope detection (linear regression on swing lows)
- **Highest High / Lowest Low:** 20-period rolling max/min for cluster definition
- **ATR (14-period):** For volatility filtering and dynamic stop sizing
- **Volume MA (20-period):** For breakout confirmation
- **Trailing Peak Tracker:** Records the highest high since entry for the 1% retracement exit

**6. Trade Logic Flow:**

1. Detect ascending trend line (2+ higher lows)
2. Measure 20-bar high-low cluster width
3. Wait for close above trend line + cluster high
4. Enter long with stop at cluster low
5. Track peak price; exit on 1% retracement from peak
6. Log trade for performance analysis

**7. Optimization Parameters:**

- Cluster lookback: 15-30 bars (default 20)
- Retracement exit: 0.5%-2% (default 1%)
- Volume multiplier: 1.2x-2.0x (default 1.5x)
- Trend line minimum touches: 2-4

**Key Edge:** The dual confirmation (trend line + cluster) dramatically reduces false breakouts common in single-signal systems, while the tight 1% trailing exit locks in profits during momentum moves.
