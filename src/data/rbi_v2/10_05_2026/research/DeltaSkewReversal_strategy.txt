   - Price above upper Bollinger Band (20,2) or significantly above 20-period SMA.
   - Optionally, bearish divergence on RSI or MACD.

3. Select a put option with delta > 0.5 (e.g., delta 0.55–0.70). This is typically slightly ITM or ATM.
4. Select a call option with delta < -0. (likely -0.3 to -0.5, i.e., OTM call). Ensure the call strike is above current price.
5. Execute simultaneously: Buy the put, sell the call. Ensure the net debit/credit is acceptable (prefer net credit or small debit).

- **Time Exit:** Close the position 1-2 days before expiration to avoid gamma risk and assignment.

- **Overbought Condition Resolution:** If RSI drops below 50 or price returns to mean, consider exiting.
