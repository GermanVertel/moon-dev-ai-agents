STRATEGY_NAME: DivergentReversal

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a contrarian intraday setup that combines MACD momentum divergence with histogram confirmation and price-action support validation. Despite the wording "bearish divergence," the trade direction is LONG, meaning the setup is interpreted as a bullish reversal signal (classic positive/bullish divergence where price makes a lower low but MACD makes a higher low, or a momentum exhaustion signal at support). Below is the full specification.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Primary Signal: MACD divergence at an intraday support zone.
- Confirmation 1: MACD histogram turning from negative to positive (or shrinking negative bars), signaling momentum shift.
- Confirmation 2: Price action confirming intraday support (rejection wick, bullish engulfing, or higher-low structure).
- Timeframe: Intraday (5m, 15m, or 1H charts). Use a higher timeframe (1H/4H) for trend/context bias.
- Instrument: Liquid instruments (FX majors, index futures, large-cap equities, crypto majors).

2. INDICATORS REQUIRED
- MACD (standard 12, 26, 9) with both MACD line, Signal line, and Histogram.
- Support identification: recent swing lows, prior day high/low, VWAP, or session low.
- Optional: Volume or volume profile to validate support strength.
- Optional: ATR for dynamic stop sizing.

3. ENTRY RULES (LONG)
Step 1 — Locate Divergence:
- Price prints a lower low (or equal low) into a defined intraday support zone.
- MACD line prints a higher low vs. the prior swing low (bullish/positive divergence). If the setup is described as "bearish MACD divergence," it must be interpreted as bearish momentum divergence that is exhausting and reversing at support — i.e., the down-move is losing steam.

Step 2 — Histogram Confirmation:
- MACD histogram shows a bullish crossover (negative to positive) OR a sequence of shrinking negative bars followed by a positive bar.
- Histogram must confirm on the same or one candle after the divergence low.

Step 3 — Price Action Confirmation:
- Candle closes back above the intraday support level, OR
- Bullish rejection wick at support, OR
- Bullish engulfing / pin bar / inside-bar breakout above the prior candle high.

Entry Trigger:
- Enter long on the close of the confirmation candle, or
- Enter on a break above the high of the confirmation candle (stop-entry method for stronger confirmation).

4. EXIT RULES
- Take Profit 1: Nearest resistance / prior swing high / VWAP (scale out 50%).
- Take Profit 2: Next structural resistance or measured move equal to the divergence range.
- Trailing Stop: Trail behind swing lows or use MACD histogram flipping negative as an exit signal.
- Time Stop: Exit if the trade does not move in favor within N bars (e.g., 10–15 bars on 5m).

5. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Stop Loss: Place below the divergence low (the swing low that formed the divergence) minus a buffer (e.g., 0.5 × ATR or a few pips).
- Position Sizing: Risk no more than 0.5%–1% of account equity per trade.
- Risk/Reward: Minimum 1:2 R:R; skip setups where support is too close to resistance.
- Max Concurrent Trades: 1–2 to avoid correlated exposure.
- Avoid trading during major news events unless the setup is on a higher timeframe.

6. TRADE FILTERS / CONFLUENCE
- Only take longs when the higher timeframe trend is neutral-to-bullish, OR when price is at a major HTF support.
- Avoid divergence trades in strong, one-directional trending markets without a clear support reaction.
- Require the divergence to occur at a pre-identified support (not random mid-range).
- Volume expansion on the reversal candle increases probability.

7. COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID
- Divergence can persist in strong trends — always wait for histogram + price action confirmation.
- False support breaks: require a candle close above support, not just a wick.
- Overtrading: limit to high-quality setups at key levels.

SUMMARY:
This is a long-only, intraday mean-reversion/reversal strategy that fuses MACD divergence (momentum exhaustion), MACD histogram crossover (momentum shift), and price-action support confirmation (structure validation). The triple confirmation reduces false signals and targets a reversal bounce off intraday support with defined risk below the divergence low.
