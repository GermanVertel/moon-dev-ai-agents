STRATEGY_NAME: ContrarianAnalyst

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy exploits the divergence between short-term price underperformance and persistent bullish sell-side analyst coverage, combined with a technical trend filter and a profit-taking exit on extended winners.

**Core Concept**
The strategy bets that stocks which have lagged recently but still carry high analyst ratings are experiencing temporary weakness rather than fundamental deterioration. The 50-day moving average acts as a trend confirmation gate: only buy the dip when the longer-term trend is still intact. Simultaneously, it harvests profits from positions that have become overextended by hitting new all-time highs.

**Key Strategy Components**

1. **Universe & Screening**
   - Start with a broad equity universe (e.g., S&P 500 or Russell 1000) to ensure liquidity and analyst coverage.
   - Require a minimum number of covering analysts (e.g., 3+) for rating reliability.
   - Filter for stocks with a consensus sell-side rating of "Buy" or "Strong Buy" (or an average rating score above a threshold, e.g., ≤2.0 on a 1–5 scale where 1 = Strong Buy).

2. **Entry Criteria (Long Buys)**
   - **Underperformance:** Stock is a recent laggard — e.g., negative or below-median return over the trailing 1–3 months relative to its sector or the index.
   - **High Sell-Side Rating:** Consensus analyst rating remains bullish (Buy/Strong Buy).
   - **Trend Filter:** Price is trading ABOVE its 50-day moving average, confirming the intermediate uptrend is intact despite recent weakness.
   - All three conditions must be true simultaneously to trigger a buy.

3. **Exit Criteria (Profit-Taking / Selling Winners)**
   - **All-Time High Breach:** Sell any held position that has made a new all-time high since the start of the year (YTD high). This captures the "sell winners" momentum-exhaustion logic.
   - Optionally pair with a rating downgrade exit: exit if consensus rating falls below the buy threshold.

4. **Risk Management**
   - **Position Sizing:** Equal-weight or volatility-scaled positions; cap any single name (e.g., 2–5% of portfolio).
   - **Stop-Loss:** Hard stop below the 50-day MA (e.g., close below 50-DMA by a defined margin) to invalidate the trend thesis.
   - **Sector Caps:** Limit concentration (e.g., max 20–25% per sector) since laggards can cluster.
   - **Rebalancing Cadence:** Re-evaluate signals on a weekly or bi-weekly basis to avoid overtrading.
   - **Liquidity Constraint:** Only trade names with sufficient average daily volume.

5. **Required Indicators / Data**
   - 50-day simple moving average (SMA).
   - Trailing return metric (1-month and/or 3-month) for underperformance ranking.
   - Consensus sell-side analyst rating (mean rating and analyst count).
   - All-time-high and year-to-date high tracking for exit signals.
   - Optional: sector/index benchmark for relative underperformance calculation.

**Logic Summary**
Buy quality names the market has temporarily punished (laggards with intact bullish analyst sentiment trading above their 50-DMA), and rotate out of names that have already run to new all-time highs. It is a contrarian-entry, momentum-exit hybrid that leans on analyst conviction as a fundamental anchor and the 50-DMA as a trend guardrail.
