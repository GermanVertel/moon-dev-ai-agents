STRATEGY_NAME: ClusterVolumeBreakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy identifies high-conviction breakout opportunities by combining volume profile analysis with price action, specifically targeting breakouts from consolidation zones where institutional volume has accumulated.

**Core Concept:**
Traditional breakout strategies fail because they ignore where volume actually traded. This strategy identifies "volume clusters" — price levels where significant trading activity occurred — and only takes breakouts that clear these zones with confirming volume expansion.

**Key Components:**

1. **Volume Profile Construction**
   - Build a volume-at-price histogram over a lookback window (typically 20-50 bars)
   - Divide price range into bins (e.g., 30-50 bins)
   - Identify the Point of Control (POC) — the price level with highest volume
   - Identify High Volume Nodes (HVN) and Low Volume Nodes (LVN)
   - Mark the Value Area (typically 70% of total volume)

2. **Consolidation Detection**
   - Price must spend N bars (e.g., 10+) within a defined range
   - The range should contain a clear HVN or POC
   - Bollinger Band width or ATR should contract (squeeze condition)

3. **Breakout Trigger**
   - Price closes beyond the consolidation high/low
   - Volume on breakout bar must exceed 1.5x the average volume of the last 20 bars
   - Ideally, price breaks through a LVN (thin volume zone) — this signals low resistance ahead

**Entry Rules:**

- **Long Entry:** Price closes above consolidation high AND above the Value Area High (VAH), with breakout volume > 1.5x average. Enter on close or on retest of breakout level.
- **Short Entry:** Price closes below consolidation low AND below the Value Area Low (VAL), with breakout volume > 1.5x average.

**Exit Rules:**

- **Stop Loss:** For longs, place below the POC or the consolidation midpoint (whichever is tighter). For shorts, above POC/consolidation midpoint.
- **Take Profit 1:** Next HVN above entry (long) / below entry (short) — scale out 50%
- **Take Profit 2:** 2x the consolidation range projected from breakout point
- **Trailing Stop:** Trail below the most recent swing low (long) or above swing high (short) once TP1 is hit
- **Time Stop:** Exit if breakout fails to make progress within 5-10 bars and price re-enters consolidation

**Risk Management:**

- Risk per trade: 0.5-1% of account equity
- Position size = (Account Risk %) / (Entry - Stop Loss)
- Avoid trades where the nearest HVN is too close (poor R:R)
- Skip breakouts occurring against the higher-timeframe trend
- Maximum 2-3 concurrent positions to reduce correlated exposure
- No new entries in the 15 minutes before/after major news events

**Required Indicators:**

1. **Volume Profile** (VPVR or session-based) — core indicator
2. **Volume MA (20-period)** — for breakout confirmation
3. **ATR (14)** — for consolidation detection and stop sizing
4. **Bollinger Bands (20, 2)** — squeeze detection
5. **POC / VAH / VAL lines** — plotted from volume profile
6. **Higher timeframe trend filter** (e.g., 200 EMA on 4H)

**Best Timeframes:**
- Intraday: 5m-15m charts with session volume profile
- Swing: 1H-4H charts with 20-30 day volume profile

**Optimal Conditions:**
- Works best in liquid instruments (index futures, large-cap stocks, major FX pairs)
- Avoid low-volume instruments where profile is noisy
- Performs well after volatility compression regimes (post-squeeze)

**Common Pitfalls to Avoid:**
- False breakouts into HVNs (high resistance) — always check what's above/below
- Ignoring volume divergence (price breaks but volume doesn't confirm)
- Trading breakouts during low-liquidity sessions (Asian range for US instruments, etc.)
