STRATEGY_NAME: RelativeSqueeze

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy fuses Bollinger Band volatility structure with Relative Strength (RS) against a benchmark (e.g., SPY, BTC, or an index) to filter trades. The core idea: Bollinger Bands signal *when* volatility is compressing or expanding, while RS confirms *whether* the asset has the strength to sustain a directional move. This reduces false breakouts and improves timing on reversals.

---

## 1. Key Strategy Components

**A. Bollinger Bands (BB)**
- Period: 20 (SMA basis)
- Standard Deviation multiplier: 2.0
- Components:
  - Middle Band = 20-period SMA
  - Upper Band = SMA + (2 × StdDev)
  - Lower Band = SMA − (2 × StdDev)
  - Bandwidth = (Upper − Lower) / Middle
  - %B = (Price − Lower) / (Upper − Lower)

**B. Relative Strength (RS)**
- Definition: Ratio of the asset's price to a benchmark price.
  - RS Line = Asset Price / Benchmark Price
  - RS Momentum = RS Line today vs. RS Line N periods ago (e.g., 20)
- Alternative: RSI-style RS using a benchmark-adjusted return.
- Use RS *slope* and *position relative to its own moving average* to determine leadership or laggard status.

**C. Volatility Regime Detection**
- Squeeze condition: Bandwidth is in the lowest 20th percentile of its lookback (e.g., 100 bars).
- Expansion condition: Bandwidth rising after a squeeze.

---

## 2. Entry Rules

**Long Entry (Buy)**
1. Bollinger Squeeze detected (Bandwidth in lower percentile).
2. Price closes above the Middle Band (20 SMA).
3. RS Line is rising (positive slope over last 5–10 bars) AND RS Line > its 20-period MA.
4. Confirmation: %B crosses above 0.5 (or price closes above Upper Band on expansion).
5. Volume confirmation preferred (above 20-period average).

**Short Entry (Sell)**
1. Bollinger Squeeze detected.
2. Price closes below the Middle Band.
3. RS Line is falling (negative slope) AND RS Line < its 20-period MA.
4. Confirmation: %B crosses below 0.5 (or price closes below Lower Band on expansion).
5. Volume confirmation preferred.

**Optional Aggressive Entry**
- Enter on the breakout bar (close outside the band) only if RS slope agrees in direction.

---

## 3. Exit Rules

**Profit-Taking / Trend Exit**
- Long: Exit when price closes below the Middle Band AND RS Line slope turns negative.
- Short: Exit when price closes above the Middle Band AND RS Line slope turns positive.
- Alternative: Trail using the Lower Band (long) or Upper Band (short).

**Volatility Exit**
- Exit when Bandwidth reaches the upper 80th–90th percentile (volatility exhaustion).
- Exit if %B reverses from >1.0 (long) or <0.0 (short) back toward 0.5.

**RS Divergence Exit**
- Long: If price makes a new high but RS Line fails to make a new high → reduce/exit.
- Short: If price makes a new low but RS Line fails to make a new low → reduce/exit.

---

## 4. Risk Management

- **Position Sizing:** Risk 1–2% of equity per trade. Size based on distance from entry to stop.
- **Stop-Loss:**
  - Long: Below the Lower Band or below the recent swing low (whichever is tighter).
  - Short: Above the Upper Band or above the recent swing high.
- **ATR Filter:** Avoid entries when ATR is extremely low (dead squeeze with no catalyst) or extremely high (late-stage volatility).
- **Benchmark Alignment:** Skip longs if the benchmark itself is in a downtrend (RS rising but market falling = weak edge). Skip shorts if benchmark is in a strong uptrend.
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** 3–5 to limit correlated exposure.
- **Time Stop:** Exit if no expansion within 10–15 bars after entry.

---

## 5. Required Indicators / Data

| Indicator | Purpose | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Bollinger Bands | Volatility + trend reference | 20 SMA, 2 StdDev |
| Bandwidth | Squeeze detection | 100-bar percentile |
| %B | Momentum within bands | Derived from BB |
| RS Line | Relative performance vs benchmark | Asset / Benchmark |
| RS MA | RS trend filter | 20-period SMA on RS Line |
| Volume MA | Confirmation | 20-period |
| ATR | Volatility filter & sizing | 14-period |
| Benchmark data | RS calculation | SPY / BTC / Index |

---

## 6. Strategy Logic Summary

1. **Squeeze forms** → market is coiling.
2. **RS slope tells you which side has strength.**
3. **Breakout above/below Middle Band with RS agreement = entry.**
4. **Band expansion + RS continuation = hold.**
5. **RS divergence or volatility exhaustion = exit.**

This combination avoids the classic Bollinger Band trap of trading every breakout by requiring *relative strength confirmation*, making it especially effective in equity, crypto, and FX markets where a benchmark or index proxy is available.
