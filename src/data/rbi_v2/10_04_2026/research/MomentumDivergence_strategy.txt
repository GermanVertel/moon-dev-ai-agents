STRATEGY_NAME: MomentumDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines two momentum-based divergence signals to identify high-probability short entries, then uses volume-adaptive profit targets and volatility-based stops for exits.

**Core Components:**

1. **MACD Bearish Divergence Detection**
   - Price makes a higher high while MACD makes a lower high (classic bearish divergence)
   - Confirms weakening bullish momentum despite rising price
   - Use standard MACD (12, 26, 9) on the chosen timeframe

2. **RSI Bearish Divergence Detection**
   - Price makes a higher high while RSI makes a lower high
   - RSI period typically 14
   - Confirms overbought conditions losing strength

3. **Dual Confirmation Requirement**
   - Both MACD and RSI must show bearish divergence on the SAME price swing
   - This dual filter reduces false signals significantly
   - Only enter when both divergences align at the same pivot high

**Entry Rules (Short):**
- Identify a swing high where price prints a higher high
- Confirm MACD histogram/line prints a lower high at that same swing
- Confirm RSI prints a lower high at that same swing
- Enter short on the close of the reversal candle (bearish engulfing, shooting star, or break of prior candle low)
- Optional: wait for a lower low confirmation candle before entry

**Exit Rules:**

1. **Take Profit (Volume-Based):**
   - Calculate average daily volume percentage change (e.g., 20-period average of |(V_t - V_{t-1})/V_{t-1}| × 100)
   - Set TP at 2× that average percentage move from entry price
   - Example: if avg daily volume % change = 3%, TP = 6% below entry
   - This adapts the target to current market activity/liquidity

2. **Stop Loss (ATR-Based):**
   - Set SL at 1× ATR (14-period) above entry price
   - Provides volatility-adjusted risk control
   - Never move stop against position

**Risk Management:**
- Risk-to-reward must be at least 1:1.5 before entering (check TP distance vs ATR distance)
- Position size = (Account Risk %) / (Entry − Stop) distance
- Max 1-2% account risk per trade
- Avoid entries within 30 min of major news events
- Skip signals if ATR is unusually low (choppy) or unusually high (news spike)

**Required Indicators:**
- MACD (12, 26, 9)
- RSI (14)
- ATR (14)
- Volume with 20-period average % change calculation
- Swing high/low detection (pivot points, 3-5 bar lookback)

**Timeframe:** Works best on 1H–4H charts for cleaner divergences; avoid <15min due to noise.

**Invalidation:**
- If price breaks above the swing high that formed the divergence, the setup is invalid
- Exit early if MACD and RSI both re-align bullish before TP is hit
