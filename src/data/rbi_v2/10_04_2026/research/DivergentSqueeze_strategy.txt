STRATEGY_NAME: DivergentSqueeze

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines a classic long-term trend signal with a contrarian volatility setup, using volume and price action divergence as a filter to avoid false bearish continuation signals.

**Core Components:**

1. **Primary Trend Filter (Death Cross):**
   - 50-day SMA crossing below the 200-day SMA signals a bearish regime.
   - This defines the directional bias and the "bearish territory" context.

2. **Volume Confirmation (Lower-than-Average):**
   - The death cross must occur on volume below its 20-day average.
   - Low-volume breakdowns often indicate exhaustion rather than conviction, setting up a potential divergence/mean-reversion move.

3. **Price Action Divergence:**
   - While the MAs are bearish, watch for bullish divergence: price making lower lows while momentum (RSI or MACD histogram) makes higher lows.
   - This divergence signals weakening downside pressure and an impending volatility expansion.

4. **Volatility Breakout Trigger:**
   - Bollinger Bands must be in a "squeeze" (bandwidth at multi-week lows) after the divergence forms.
   - Entry occurs on a close above the upper Bollinger Band (or above the 50-day SMA) confirming the breakout from the squeeze.

**Entry Rules:**
- 50 SMA < 200 SMA (bearish regime established).
- Death cross volume < 20-day average volume.
- Bullish RSI/MACD divergence present over the last 10–20 bars.
- Bollinger Bandwidth in the bottom 20% of its 6-month range (squeeze).
- Enter long on a daily close above the upper Bollinger Band with above-average volume.

**Exit Rules:**
- Primary exit: price closes below the middle Bollinger Band (20 SMA).
- Secondary exit: price touches the lower Bollinger Band after entry (volatility reversal).
- Stop-loss: below the most recent swing low or 2× ATR from entry.

**Risk Management:**
- Position size based on 1% account risk per trade.
- ATR-based stop (2× ATR) to accommodate volatility expansion.
- No new entries if the broader market (e.g., SPY) is in a strong downtrend with rising volume.

**Required Indicators:**
- 50-day and 200-day SMA
- 20-day SMA with Bollinger Bands (2 std dev)
- Bollinger Bandwidth
- Volume with 20-day average
- RSI (14) or MACD (12,26,9)
- ATR (14)
