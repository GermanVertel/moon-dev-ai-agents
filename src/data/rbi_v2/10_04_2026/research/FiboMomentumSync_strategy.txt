STRATEGY_NAME: FiboMomentumSync

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Overview:**
FiboMomentumSync is a trend-following strategy that combines Fibonacci retracement zones with MACD oscillator divergence to identify high-probability continuation entries. The core premise is that pullbacks to key Fibonacci levels (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) within an established trend, confirmed by MACD momentum alignment, offer favorable risk-to-reward entries.

---

**1. Key Strategy Components**

- **Trend Identification:** Determine the prevailing trend using a higher timeframe (e.g., 4H or Daily) via a 50/200 EMA crossover or price relative to a 200-period EMA.
- **Fibonacci Retracement Mapping:** Automatically draw retracement levels from the most recent swing high to swing low (for uptrends) or swing low to swing high (for downtrends).
- **MACD Oscillator:** Standard MACD (12, 26, 9) used to confirm momentum direction and detect divergence.
- **Convergence Zone:** The area where price interacts with a Fibonacci level (38.2%–61.8%) AND MACD shows a bullish/bearish signal.

---

**2. Entry Rules**

**Long Entry (Uptrend):**
1. Price is above the 200 EMA and the 50 EMA > 200 EMA (trend confirmed up).
2. Price retraces into the 38.2%–61.8% Fibonacci zone of the most recent impulse leg.
3. MACD shows one of the following:
   - Bullish divergence (price makes lower low, MACD makes higher low).
   - MACD line crosses above the signal line within the retracement zone.
   - MACD histogram flips from negative to positive.
4. Entry trigger: A bullish candlestick confirmation (engulfing, pin bar, or close above the prior candle's high) inside the Fib zone.

**Short Entry (Downtrend):**
1. Price is below the 200 EMA and the 50 EMA < 200 EMA (trend confirmed down).
2. Price rallies into the 38.2%–61.8% Fibonacci zone of the most recent impulse leg down.
3. MACD shows:
   - Bearish divergence (price makes higher high, MACD makes lower high).
   - MACD line crosses below the signal line within the retracement zone.
   - MACD histogram flips from positive to negative.
4. Entry trigger: A bearish candlestick confirmation inside the Fib zone.

---

**3. Exit Rules**

- **Take Profit (TP1):** Previous swing high (long) or swing low (short) — typically the 0% Fibonacci extension.
- **Take Profit (TP2):** 127.2% or 161.8% Fibonacci extension of the retracement leg.
- **Stop Loss (SL):** Just beyond the 78.6% Fibonacci level (or below the swing low for longs / above swing high for shorts).
- **Trailing Exit:** Once TP1 is hit, move SL to breakeven; trail remaining position using the 50 EMA or ATR-based trailing stop (2× ATR).
- **Momentum Exit:** Close the position if MACD crosses back against the trade direction before TP1 is reached.

---

**4. Risk Management**

- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
- **Risk-to-Reward:** Minimum 1:2 R:R required; skip trades where SL-to-TP1 ratio is unfavorable.
- **Confluence Filter:** Only take trades where MACD signal AND Fibonacci level align — no single-signal entries.
- **Volatility Filter:** Avoid entries during low-volume sessions or major news events (e.g., NFP, FOMC).
- **Max Concurrent Positions:** Limit to 2–3 to avoid correlated exposure.
- **Time Stop:** If trade doesn't reach TP1 within a predefined number of bars (e.g., 20), close at market.

---

**5. Required Indicators**

| Indicator | Purpose | Settings |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Fibonacci Retracement | Identify pullback entry zones | Auto-drawn from swing high/low |
| MACD | Momentum confirmation & divergence | 12, 26, 9 |
| EMA (50 & 200) | Trend direction filter | 50-period, 200-period |
| ATR | Volatility-based stop/trail | 14-period |
| Volume | Confirmation filter | Optional, 20-period MA |

---

**6. Timeframes & Markets**

- **Best Timeframes:** 1H, 4H, Daily (swing trading).
- **Best Markets:** FX majors, indices (SPX, NASDAQ), liquid crypto (BTC, ETH), large-cap stocks.
- **Avoid:** Choppy/range-bound markets where Fibonacci levels are unreliable.

---

**7. Strategy Logic Summary**

FiboMomentumSync waits for a confirmed trend, then hunts for pullbacks into the golden Fibonacci zone. It only pulls the trigger when MACD momentum agrees — either through divergence or a crossover — ensuring entries are aligned with both structure (Fib) and momentum (MACD). This dual-confirmation approach filters out weak retracements and improves the probability of catching the next impulse leg in the trend direction.
