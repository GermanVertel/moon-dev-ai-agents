STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityCompressionMomentum

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a hybrid momentum/mean-reversion system that exploits the tendency of low-volatility consolidation phases to precede explosive directional moves. It uses Bollinger Band breakouts as the primary momentum trigger, ATR as a volatility filter, and an EMA-based mean-reversion adjustment to time exits.

=====================================================
1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
=====================================================

A) Bollinger Bands (4-6 week lookback)
- Period: 20-30 trading days (approximately 4-6 weeks)
- Standard deviation multiplier: 2.0
- Upper Band = SMA(period) + (2 × StdDev)
- Lower Band = SMA(period) − (2 × StdDev)
- Purpose: Identify statistically significant price extremes and breakout thresholds

B) Volatility Filter (ATR %)
- ATR Period: 14 days
- ATR% = ATR(14) / Current Close × 100
- Entry condition requires ATR% < 10% (low volatility regime)
- Purpose: Ensure entry only during compressed volatility, which historically precedes expansion moves

C) Mean-Reversion Adjustment (20-day EMA)
- EMA Period: 20 days
- Used as a dynamic anchor for exit timing
- Distance from EMA modulates how aggressively positions are closed

=====================================================
2. ENTRY RULES (LONG ONLY)
=====================================================

All conditions must be TRUE on the same bar (or within a 1-2 bar window):

1. Price closes ABOVE the upper Bollinger Band (breakout confirmation)
   - Close > UpperBB(period=20-30, mult=2.0)

2. ATR% < 10%
   - ATR(14) / Close × 100 < 10
   - Confirms low-volatility compression regime

3. Optional confirmation (recommended):
   - Close > EMA(20) to align with short-term trend
   - Volume expansion on breakout bar

Entry: Market/Limit order at next bar open after signal confirmation.

=====================================================
3. EXIT RULES
=====================================================

Primary Exit — Bollinger Band Reversion:
- Exit when price closes back BELOW the upper Bollinger Band
- This signals momentum exhaustion / reversion to the mean

Mean-Reversion Adjustment (EMA-based):
- Compute distance from EMA: D = (Close − EMA20) / EMA20
- If D exceeds a threshold (e.g., 5-8% above EMA), tighten stop / exit early
- If price crosses below EMA20, force exit regardless of BB position
- This prevents holding extended positions during reversal

Secondary Exits:
- Hard stop-loss: Entry price − (1.5 × ATR at entry)
- Time stop: Exit after N bars (e.g., 15-20 bars) if neither target nor stop hit
- Trailing stop: Activate once price moves 2× ATR in favor

=====================================================
4. RISK MANAGEMENT
=====================================================

- Position sizing: Risk 1-2% of equity per trade
  Position Size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop)
- Stop-loss: 1.5 × ATR(14) below entry
- Max concurrent positions: 3-5 (sector diversification)
- Volatility regime check: Skip entries if ATR% > 10% (avoid chasing extended moves)
- Re-entry: Allow re-entry only after a full BB reversion cycle completes
- Correlation filter: Avoid entering correlated assets simultaneously

=====================================================
5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
=====================================================

| Indicator          | Parameters                    | Purpose                          |
|--------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Bollinger Bands    | Period 20-30, StdDev 2.0      | Breakout + reversion trigger     |
| ATR                | Period 14                     | Volatility filter (ATR% < 10%)   |
| EMA                | Period 20                     | Mean-reversion anchor / exit     |
| Volume (optional)  | 20-day avg                    | Breakout confirmation            |

=====================================================
6. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
=====================================================

- Timeframe: Daily bars (best fit for 4-6 week BB lookback)
- Markets: Liquid equities, ETFs, futures, or large-cap crypto
- Backtest considerations:
  * Test BB periods of 20, 25, 30 to find optimal
  * Test ATR% thresholds of 8%, 10%, 12%
  * Optimize EMA distance exit threshold (5-8%)
- Watch for: False breakouts during choppy regimes — the ATR filter is critical
- Regime awareness: Strategy performs best after prolonged consolidation (squeeze)

=====================================================
SUMMARY
=====================================================
VolatilityCompressionMomentum combines the squeeze-breakout logic of Bollinger Bands with a strict volatility filter (ATR < 10%) and a dynamic EMA-based exit adjustment. It captures the "calm before the storm" pattern while protecting profits through mean-reversion-aware exits.
