STRATEGY_NAME: FractalVolatilitySync

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines three complementary technical indicators to create a high-conviction trend-following system. The core premise is that fractal patterns reveal self-similar price structures, RSI confirms momentum extremes, and ATR validates volatility conditions — entries only trigger when all three align.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Fractal Pattern Detection (Primary Signal)**
   - Use Bill Williams' Fractal indicator (5-bar pattern)
   - Bullish fractal: middle bar has the lowest low with two higher lows on each side
   - Bearish fractal: middle bar has the highest high with two lower highs on each side
   - Only confirmed fractals (bars fully closed) are valid entry triggers

2. **RSI Confirmation (Momentum Filter)**
   - RSI period: 14 (standard)
   - For long entries: RSI must be recovering from oversold (<30 recently crossed back above 30) OR in bullish momentum zone (50-70)
   - For short entries: RSI must be retreating from overbought (>70 recently crossed back below 70) OR in bearish momentum zone (30-50)
   - Reject entries when RSI is diverging against the fractal direction

3. **ATR Volatility Filter (Regime Validation)**
   - ATR period: 14
   - Require ATR to be above its 20-period moving average (expanding volatility)
   - Reject signals when ATR is contracting (low-volatility chop zones)
   - Use ATR to set dynamic stop-loss and position sizing

**Entry Rules:**
- **Long Entry:** Bullish fractal confirmed + RSI > 30 and rising + ATR expanding above its MA + price above 50 EMA (trend alignment)
- **Short Entry:** Bearish fractal confirmed + RSI < 70 and falling + ATR expanding above its MA + price below 50 EMA (trend alignment)
- All three conditions must be true on the same bar or within a 2-bar confirmation window

**Exit Rules:**
- **Stop-Loss:** 1.5 × ATR from entry price
- **Take-Profit:** 3 × ATR (2:1 reward-to-risk minimum)
- **Trailing Stop:** Activate after 1.5 × ATR profit; trail at 1 × ATR
- **Signal Exit:** Close on opposite fractal signal or RSI crossing 70/30 against position

**Risk Management:**
- Risk per trade: 1-2% of account equity
- Position size = (Account Risk $) / (1.5 × ATR)
- Maximum 3 concurrent positions
- Skip signals during major news events or when ATR is in the bottom 20th percentile of its 100-period range

**Required Indicators:**
- Fractals (Bill Williams, 5-bar)
- RSI (14)
- ATR (14) with 20-period MA overlay
- EMA (50) for trend bias
- Optional: Volume filter to confirm fractal breakouts

**Timeframe:** Best suited for 1H to 4H charts where fractals form reliably without excessive noise.
