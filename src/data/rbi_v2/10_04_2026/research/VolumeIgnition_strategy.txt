STRATEGY_NAME: VolumeIgnition

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
VolumeIgnition is a momentum-continuation strategy that combines Bollinger Band breakouts with volume confirmation. The premise is that a breach of the upper Bollinger Band on abnormally high volume signals genuine buying pressure (not a false breakout), while the lower band serves as a dynamic stop-loss exit zone within an ATR-bounded time window.

**Required Indicators:**
1. **Bollinger Bands** (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations) — Upper Band (UB), Middle Band (MB), Lower Band (LB)
2. **Volume Filter** — 50-period average volume; "high-volume day" = current volume ≥ 150% of the 50-period average (i.e., above the 50% percentile threshold)
3. **ATR** (14-period) — used to define the maximum holding window and dynamic exit tolerance

**Entry Rules (Long):**
- Price closes **above** the upper Bollinger Band (close > UB)
- Current bar's volume is **above the 50% threshold** relative to the rolling volume distribution (e.g., volume > median of last 50 bars, or > 150% of avg volume)
- Optional confirmation: close is in the upper 25% of the bar's range (bullish candle body)
- Enter at next bar's open or on close of the signal bar

**Exit Rules:**
1. **Primary Exit (Time-Bounded Band Reversion):** If price returns to the **lower Bollinger Band** (close ≤ LB) within a window of **2 × ATR days** (i.e., holding period ≤ 2 × ATR value in bars), exit the position.
2. **Time Stop:** If neither the lower band is touched nor a profit target is hit within 2 × ATR bars, force exit at market.
3. **Optional Profit Target:** Take partial profits at 1 × ATR above entry; trail remaining position using the middle band.

**Risk Management:**
- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1% of equity per trade; size = (Account × 0.01) / (Entry − Stop)
- **Initial Stop:** Place stop at the middle Bollinger Band or 1.5 × ATR below entry (whichever is tighter)
- **Trailing Stop:** Once price advances 1 × ATR, move stop to breakeven; thereafter trail by middle band
- **Max Holding Period:** 2 × ATR bars (hard time stop to avoid dead-money trades)
- **Avoid:** Low-liquidity sessions, earnings/news events within the holding window

**Logic Rationale:**
- Upper-band breach + high volume = institutional accumulation / breakout ignition
- Lower-band reversion = failure of the breakout thesis → exit
- ATR-based time window = adapts holding period to current volatility regime
- Combining volume confirmation with band mechanics reduces whipsaw from low-conviction breakouts

**Best Market Conditions:**
- Trending or breakout regimes with expanding volatility
- Liquid instruments (large-cap equities, index futures, major FX pairs)
- Avoid choppy, low-volume consolidation markets where band breaches are frequent but unsustainable
