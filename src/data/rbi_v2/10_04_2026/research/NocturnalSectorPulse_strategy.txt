STRATEGY_NAME: NocturnalSectorPulse

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines overnight session breakout trading with sector rotation analysis, creating a unique approach that captures momentum during low-liquidity hours while filtering trades through relative sector strength.

**Core Components:**

1. **Overnight Session Focus**: Trades exclusively during the overnight session (typically 6 PM - 9:30 AM ET for US equities or equivalent futures hours), capitalizing on reduced liquidity and potentially inefficient price discovery.

2. **60-Minute High Breakout**: Entry trigger occurs when price breaks above the rolling 60-minute high during overnight hours. This timeframe balances noise reduction with responsiveness to genuine momentum shifts.

3. **EROD Ratio Filter (Sector Rotation)**: 
   - EROD (Earnings-Relative Outperformance Differential) measures a stock's performance relative to its sector peers
   - Only take long entries when EROD is positive, indicating the stock is outperforming its sector
   - This filters out breakouts driven purely by broad sector moves vs. genuine relative strength

**Entry Rules:**
- Time window: Overnight session only
- Price must break above the 60-minute rolling high
- EROD ratio must be positive (stock outperforming sector)
- Optional: Require sector ETF itself to be in uptrend (above 50-period MA)

**Exit Rules:**
- Stop-loss: 5% below entry price, BUT dynamically adjusted using ATR
  - Formula: Stop = Entry - (min(5% of entry, 1.5 × ATR(14)))
  - This prevents stops from being too wide in low-volatility regimes
- Take-profit: Consider 2:1 or 3:1 R:R, or trail using 30-minute lows
- Time-based exit: Close position at session end if neither target nor stop hit

**Risk Management:**
- Position sizing: Risk no more than 1% of account per trade
- Max concurrent positions: 3-5 to avoid overnight gap risk concentration
- Avoid earnings dates (EROD becomes unreliable pre-earnings)
- Monitor overnight gap risk — consider reducing size before major macro events

**Required Indicators:**
- 60-minute rolling high (custom calculation on 1-min or 5-min data)
- ATR(14) on the entry timeframe
- EROD ratio (stock return / sector ETF return over rolling window, e.g., 5-day)
- Sector ETF identifier (e.g., XLK, XLF, XLE) mapped to each traded symbol
- 50-period MA on sector ETF (optional trend filter)

**Key Edge:**
The overnight session often exhibits momentum continuation into the open due to institutional order flow positioning. By requiring EROD confirmation, the strategy avoids false breakouts caused by sector-wide moves, isolating stocks with genuine idiosyncratic strength. The ATR-adjusted stop prevents premature exits during volatile overnight periods while capping maximum loss at 5%.
