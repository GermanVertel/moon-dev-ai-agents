STRATEGY_NAME: LiquiditySentinel

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines liquidity screening with institutional sentiment divergence captured through VIX futures term structure, creating a momentum-contrarian hybrid. The core premise is that high-liquidity contracts with institutional positioning against prevailing short-term sentiment offer asymmetric risk-reward opportunities.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Liquidity Filter (Primary Screen)**
   - Rank all tradable contracts by 20-day average dollar volume and open interest
   - Only trade instruments in the top liquidity quartile to minimize slippage and ensure clean execution
   - Reject any contract with bid-ask spread exceeding 0.05% of mid-price

2. **VIX Futures Spread Signal (Sentiment Gauge)**
   - Calculate the 20-day delta-neutral VIX futures spread: (Front-month VIX future - Second-month VIX future), normalized by their historical standard deviation
   - This spread captures institutional hedging demand and forward volatility expectations
   - Delta-neutral construction isolates pure term-structure signal from directional vol exposure
   - Positive/steepening spread = institutional fear/hedging → contrarian long signal on high-liquidity contracts
   - Negative/flattening spread = complacency → contrarian short signal

3. **Momentum-Contrarian Overlay**
   - Compute 10-day price momentum on each candidate contract
   - Long entries: when VIX spread signals institutional hedging (fear) AND the contract shows positive short-term momentum (institutions positioning for continuation despite retail fear)
   - Short entries: when VIX spread signals complacency AND the contract shows negative short-term momentum (institutions fading retail euphoria)

**Entry Rules:**
- LONG: Contract in top liquidity quartile + 20-day VIX spread z-score > +1.0 + 10-day momentum > +2% + price above 50-day SMA
- SHORT: Contract in top liquidity quartile + 20-day VIX spread z-score < -1.0 + 10-day momentum < -2% + price below 50-day SMA
- Stagger entries into 3 tranches over 2-3 sessions to reduce timing risk

**Exit Rules:**
- Profit target: 2.5x ATR(14) from entry
- Stop loss: 1.2x ATR(14) from entry
- Signal decay exit: close position if VIX spread z-score crosses back through zero
- Time stop: exit after 15 trading days if neither target nor stop hit
- Momentum reversal exit: close if 10-day momentum flips sign against position

**Risk Management:**
- Maximum 5 concurrent positions to limit correlation exposure
- Position sizing: 1% portfolio risk per trade based on ATR-based stop distance
- Portfolio-level VIX spread exposure cap: net delta-neutral spread exposure limited to ±2.0 z-score aggregate
- Daily correlation check: reduce size if any two positions exceed 0.7 correlation
- Circuit breaker: halt new entries if portfolio drawdown exceeds 6% in rolling 10-day window

**Required Indicators:**
- 20-day rolling average dollar volume and open interest (liquidity ranking)
- VIX front-month and second-month futures prices (spread calculation)
- 20-day rolling mean and standard deviation of VIX spread (z-score normalization)
- 10-day price momentum (rate of change)
- 50-day SMA (trend filter)
- ATR(14) for stops and targets
- Bid-ask spread monitor (real-time execution filter)

**Unique Edge:**
The strategy's novelty lies in using the VIX futures spread as a proxy for *institutional* sentiment rather than retail sentiment, then combining it with a liquidity screen that ensures only institutionally-traded instruments are selected. This avoids the common contrarian trap of fading retail noise in illiquid names—instead, it aligns with where smart money is positioning within liquid markets, using the spread as a fear/greed thermometer for the institutional cohort specifically.
