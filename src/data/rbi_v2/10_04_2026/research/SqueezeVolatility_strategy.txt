STRATEGY_NAME: SqueezeVolatility

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Bollinger Bands (BB) and the Relative Strength Index (RSI) to identify high-probability breakout opportunities that occur after periods of low volatility. The core premise is that a "squeeze" — a contraction in Bollinger Band width — often precedes a significant price move, and RSI confirms the momentum and direction of that breakout.

**Key Strategy Components:**
1. **Bollinger Band Squeeze Detection:** Identifies periods where the Bollinger Bands narrow significantly, indicating low volatility and an impending breakout.
2. **RSI Momentum Confirmation:** Uses RSI to confirm the strength and direction of the breakout, filtering out false signals.
3. **Breakout Confirmation:** Requires price to close outside the Bollinger Bands with a strong RSI reading to validate the breakout.

**Indicators Required:**
- **Bollinger Bands:** 20-period Simple Moving Average (SMA) with 2 standard deviations.
- **RSI:** 14-period RSI.
- **Bollinger Band Width (BBW):** Calculated as (Upper Band - Lower Band) / Middle Band. Used to detect the squeeze.
- **Optional:** Volume indicator to confirm breakout strength.

**Entry Rules:**
- **Long Entry (Buy):**
  1. BBW is in the lowest 20% of its range over the past 100 periods (squeeze condition).
  2. Price closes above the Upper Bollinger Band.
  3. RSI is above 60 (or crosses above 60), confirming bullish momentum.
  4. Optional: Volume is above its 20-period average.
- **Short Entry (Sell):**
  1. BBW is in the lowest 20% of its range over the past 100 periods (squeeze condition).
  2. Price closes below the Lower Bollinger Band.
  3. RSI is below 40 (or crosses below 40), confirming bearish momentum.
  4. Optional: Volume is above its 20-period average.

**Exit Rules:**
- **Long Exit:**
  1. Price closes below the Middle Bollinger Band (20-period SMA).
  2. RSI drops below 50.
  3. Take profit at a predetermined level (e.g., 2x the width of the Bollinger Bands at entry).
  4. Stop loss at the Lower Bollinger Band or a fixed percentage below entry.
- **Short Exit:**
  1. Price closes above the Middle Bollinger Band.
  2. RSI rises above 50.
  3. Take profit at a predetermined level (e.g., 2x the width of the Bollinger Bands at entry).
  4. Stop loss at the Upper Bollinger Band or a fixed percentage above entry.

**Risk Management:**
- **Position Sizing:** Risk no more than 1-2% of the trading capital per trade.
- **Stop Loss:** Place stop loss at the opposite Bollinger Band or a fixed percentage (e.g., 2-3%) from entry.
- **Take Profit:** Use a risk-reward ratio of at least 1:2. Alternatively, trail the stop loss using the Middle Bollinger Band or a moving average.
- **Avoid Trading in Choppy Markets:** Only take trades when the squeeze condition is met, and avoid trading during low-volume periods or major news events.

**Additional Notes:**
- This strategy works best in trending markets and may produce false signals in ranging markets. Always use additional confirmation (e.g., candlestick patterns, support/resistance levels).
- Backtest the strategy on historical data to optimize parameters (e.g., BB period, RSI thresholds) for the specific asset and timeframe.
- Consider using a higher timeframe (e.g., daily or 4-hour) for more reliable signals.
