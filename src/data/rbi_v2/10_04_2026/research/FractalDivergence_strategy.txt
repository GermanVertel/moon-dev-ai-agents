STRATEGY_NAME: FractalDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines fractal-based market structure analysis with RSI divergence to identify high-probability trend continuation and reversal points. It is designed for trending markets and works best on higher timeframes (1H, 4H, Daily).

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Fractal Patterns: Use Bill Williams-style fractals (5-bar pattern) to identify swing highs and swing lows. An up-fractal forms when a candle's high is greater than the highs of the two candles on each side. A down-fractal forms when a candle's low is lower than the lows of the two candles on each side.
- RSI Divergence: Use RSI (14-period) to detect divergence between price and momentum. Bullish divergence occurs when price makes a lower low while RSI makes a higher low. Bearish divergence occurs when price makes a higher high while RSI makes a lower high.
- Trend Filter: Use a 50/200 EMA to determine overall trend direction. Only take long trades when price is above the 200 EMA, and short trades when price is below the 200 EMA.
- Confirmation: A valid signal requires a fractal to form in the direction of the trend, followed by RSI divergence at that fractal point.

2. ENTRY RULES
Long Entry:
- Price is above the 200 EMA (uptrend).
- A down-fractal forms (swing low).
- RSI shows bullish divergence at that down-fractal (price lower low, RSI higher low).
- Enter long on the close of the candle that breaks above the high of the fractal candle.
- Optional: Require a bullish engulfing or pin bar for additional confirmation.

Short Entry:
- Price is below the 200 EMA (downtrend).
- An up-fractal forms (swing high).
- RSI shows bearish divergence at that up-fractal (price higher high, RSI lower high).
- Enter short on the close of the candle that breaks below the low of the fractal candle.
- Optional: Require a bearish engulfing or shooting star for confirmation.

3. EXIT RULES
- Take Profit: Place TP at the next opposing fractal level, or use a 2:1 risk-reward ratio.
- Stop Loss: Place SL just beyond the fractal low (for longs) or fractal high (for shorts), plus a small buffer (e.g., 0.5x ATR).
- Trailing Stop: Once price moves 1R in profit, move SL to breakeven. Trail using the most recent fractal in the trend direction.
- Exit on Divergence Failure: If RSI divergence is invalidated (RSI makes a new low with price), exit immediately.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Risk no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade.
- Position size = (Account Risk) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price).
- Avoid trading during major news events or low-liquidity sessions.
- Maximum 3 concurrent trades to limit exposure.
- Skip signals if RSI is already overbought (>70) for longs or oversold (<30) for shorts.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Fractals (Bill Williams-style, 5-bar pattern) — built-in on most platforms.
- RSI (14-period) — for divergence detection.
- EMA 50 and EMA 200 — for trend filter.
- ATR (14-period) — for stop-loss buffer and volatility sizing.
- Optional: Volume indicator to confirm breakout strength.

6. TIMEFRAMES AND MARKETS
- Best on 1H, 4H, and Daily charts.
- Works well on Forex majors, indices, and large-cap crypto (BTC, ETH).
- Avoid ranging/choppy markets — confirm with ADX > 20 for trending conditions.

7. COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID
- Do not trade divergence alone — always require fractal confirmation.
- Divergence can persist for multiple swings; wait for the fractal break to trigger.
- Avoid counter-trend trades against the 200 EMA unless on a higher timeframe reversal setup.
- Backtest across at least 2 years of data before live deployment.
