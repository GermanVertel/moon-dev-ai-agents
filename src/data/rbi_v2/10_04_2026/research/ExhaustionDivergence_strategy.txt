STRATEGY_NAME: ExhaustionDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy identifies bearish RSI divergence during an uptrend as a signal to enter long positions, exploiting the phenomenon where momentum weakens even as price makes new highs. The setup requires three simultaneous conditions: price confirms a Higher High, RSI fails to confirm with a Lower High (bearish divergence), and volume declines on the new price high, signaling weakening participation.

CORE LOGIC:
- Bearish RSI divergence within an established uptrend often precedes a short-term pullback rather than a full reversal.
- When volume contracts on the Higher High, it suggests the rally is running out of fuel, creating a favorable entry for a long trade anticipating a bounce off the pullback or continuation after a shakeout.
- The strategy treats divergence as a warning of exhaustion, not a reversal signal, so it trades with the trend (long) on the dip rather than fading the trend.

ENTRY RULES (LONG):
1. Trend filter: Price must be above a rising 50-period EMA (or equivalent) to confirm an uptrend.
2. Identify a Higher High in price over the last N bars (e.g., 20-40 bars).
3. Confirm bearish RSI divergence: RSI (14-period) makes a Lower High while price makes a Higher High.
4. Volume filter: Volume on the Higher High bar is lower than volume on the prior swing high.
5. Entry trigger: Enter long on the first bullish confirmation candle after the divergence (e.g., close above prior candle high, or bullish engulfing/pin bar) OR on a pullback to a short-term support (e.g., 20-EMA or VWAP).

EXIT RULES:
1. ATR-based profit targets: Set take-profit at 1.5x, 2.5x, and 4x ATR from entry, scaling out at each level.
2. ATR-based stop-loss: Initial stop at 1.5x ATR below entry (or below the divergence swing low).
3. RSI convergence exit: Close remaining position when RSI converges with price (RSI makes a Higher High alongside price, or RSI crosses back above 70 without divergence).
4. Trailing stop: After first ATR target hits, trail stop using 1x ATR or a rising 20-EMA.

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Risk per trade: 1% of account equity, position size = (Account Risk) / (Entry − Stop).
- Max 2 concurrent positions to avoid correlated exposure.
- No new entries if RSI > 75 (overbought extreme) to avoid chasing.
- Invalidate setup if volume expands sharply on the Higher High (contradicts exhaustion thesis).
- Time stop: Exit if neither target nor stop is hit within 15 bars.

REQUIRED INDICATORS:
- RSI (14-period) for divergence detection.
- Volume (raw or relative volume vs 20-period average).
- ATR (14-period) for stops and targets.
- EMA 50 (trend filter) and EMA 20 (trailing/support).
- Swing high/low detection (fractal or pivot-based, e.g., 5-bar pivots).

OPTIMAL CONDITIONS:
- Best in trending markets with clear swing structure.
- Avoid ranging/choppy markets where divergences produce false signals.
- Higher timeframes (4H, Daily) produce more reliable divergence signals than 1H or lower.

NOTES:
This is a counterintuitive long strategy — it buys weakness signaled by divergence, betting that the exhaustion leads to a shallow pullback followed by trend continuation rather than a full reversal. The volume filter is critical to distinguish genuine exhaustion from healthy consolidation.
