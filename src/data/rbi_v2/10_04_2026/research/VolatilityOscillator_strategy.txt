STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityOscillator

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Bollinger Bands (BB) with the Stochastic Oscillator to identify high-probability reversal points. Bollinger Bands measure volatility and price extremes, while the Stochastic Oscillator measures momentum and overbought/oversold conditions. Together, they filter out false signals and confirm reversals when price touches a band AND the oscillator reaches an extreme.

**Key Strategy Components:**
1. **Bollinger Bands (20, 2):** Middle band = 20-period SMA. Upper/lower bands = ±2 standard deviations.
2. **Stochastic Oscillator (14, 3, 3):** %K = 14-period, smoothed by 3; %D = 3-period SMA of %K.
3. **Reversal Confirmation:** A reversal signal requires BOTH price at/near a Bollinger Band AND Stochastic in overbought (>80) or oversold (<20) territory.

**Entry Rules:**

*Long Entry (Buy at potential bottom):*
- Price touches or pierces the LOWER Bollinger Band.
- Stochastic %K crosses ABOVE %D while both are below 20 (oversold).
- Optional confirmation: Bullish candlestick (hammer, bullish engulfing) at the lower band.
- Enter long on the close of the confirmation candle.

*Short Entry (Sell at potential top):*
- Price touches or pierces the UPPER Bollinger Band.
- Stochastic %K crosses BELOW %D while both are above 80 (overbought).
- Optional confirmation: Bearish candlestick (shooting star, bearish engulfing) at the upper band.
- Enter short on the close of the confirmation candle.

**Exit Rules:**
- **Take Profit (Long):** Exit when price reaches the middle Bollinger Band (20 SMA) or when Stochastic crosses back above 80.
- **Take Profit (Short):** Exit when price reaches the middle Bollinger Band or when Stochastic crosses back below 20.
- **Stop Loss (Long):** Place below the most recent swing low or 1.5 × ATR below entry.
- **Stop Loss (Short):** Place above the most recent swing high or 1.5 × ATR above entry.
- **Time Exit:** If no target/stop hit within 10–15 bars, exit to avoid stagnation.

**Risk Management:**
1. Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
2. Position size = (Account Risk $) ÷ (Entry Price − Stop Loss Price).
3. Avoid trading during major news events or low-liquidity sessions.
4. Require both indicators to align; skip trades where only one condition is met.
5. In strong trends, price can "walk the band"—avoid counter-trend shorts at upper band during uptrends and longs at lower band during downtrends. Use a higher-timeframe trend filter (e.g., 200 SMA) to only take reversals in the direction of the larger trend, or switch to a breakout strategy in trending regimes.
6. Consider a maximum of 2–3 consecutive losing trades before pausing to reassess market conditions.

**Required Indicators:**
- Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations)
- Stochastic Oscillator (14, 3, 3)
- Optional: ATR (14) for dynamic stops
- Optional: 200-period SMA for trend filter

**Best Timeframes:**
- 15-minute to 4-hour charts for intraday/swing reversals.
- Works best in ranging or mean-reverting markets; less effective in strong trending markets.

**Signal Quality Notes:**
- The strongest signals occur when Bollinger Bands are relatively narrow (low volatility) and then expand with the reversal.
- Divergence between price (new low) and Stochastic (higher low) at the lower band significantly boosts long signal reliability (and vice versa for shorts).
