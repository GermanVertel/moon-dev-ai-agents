STRATEGY_NAME: FiboIchimoku

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Fibonacci retracement levels with the Ichimoku Cloud to create a trend-following system that identifies high-probability pullback entries within established trends. The Ichimoku Cloud confirms trend direction and momentum, while Fibonacci levels pinpoint optimal retracement zones for entries.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Trend Confirmation (Ichimoku Cloud):**
   - Price must be above the Kumo Cloud for bullish trend, below for bearish trend.
   - Tenkan-sen (Conversion Line, 9-period) above Kijun-sen (Base Line, 26-period) confirms bullish bias; opposite for bearish.
   - Chikou Span (Lagging Line, 26-period shifted back) must be above price for bullish, below for bearish.
   - Cloud color and thickness indicate trend strength and future support/resistance.

2. **Fibonacci Retracement Setup:**
   - Identify the most recent significant swing high and swing low in the direction of the trend.
   - Draw Fibonacci retracement from swing low to swing high (uptrend) or swing high to swing low (downtrend).
   - Key retracement zones: 38.2%, 50%, 61.8% (Golden Pocket), and 78.6%.
   - The 50%-61.8% zone is the primary entry area.

3. **Entry Rules (Long Example):**
   - Ichimoku confirms bullish trend (price above cloud, Tenkan > Kijun, Chikou above price).
   - Price retraces into the 50%-61.8% Fibonacci zone.
   - Bullish candlestick confirmation (engulfing, pin bar, or hammer) at the Fibonacci level.
   - Price remains above the Kijun-sen or the top of the Kumo Cloud.
   - Enter long on the close of the confirmation candle.

4. **Entry Rules (Short Example):**
   - Ichimoku confirms bearish trend (price below cloud, Tenkan < Kijun, Chikou below price).
   - Price rallies into the 50%-61.8% Fibonacci zone.
   - Bearish candlestick confirmation (shooting star, bearish engulfing) at the Fibonacci level.
   - Price remains below the Kijun-sen or bottom of the Kumo Cloud.
   - Enter short on the close of the confirmation candle.

5. **Exit Rules:**
   - **Take Profit:** Target the recent swing high (long) or swing low (short), or use Fibonacci extensions (127.2%, 161.8%) for extended targets.
   - **Stop Loss:** Place below the 78.6% Fibonacci level or below the Kijun-sen/cloud for longs; above for shorts.
   - **Trailing Stop:** Trail using the Kijun-sen or the Tenkan-sen as the trend progresses.
   - **Trend Invalidation Exit:** Exit if price closes through the Kumo Cloud against the position, or if Tenkan/Kijun cross reverses.

6. **Risk Management:**
   - Risk no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade.
   - Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price).
   - Minimum risk-reward ratio of 1:2; ideally 1:3 or better.
   - Avoid trading during major news events or when the cloud is extremely thin/flat (indecision).
   - Skip setups where the retracement exceeds 78.6% (trend weakness signal).

7. **Required Indicators:**
   - Ichimoku Cloud (Tenkan-sen 9, Kijun-sen 26, Senkou Span B 52, Chikou Span 26).
   - Fibonacci Retracement tool (auto or manual swing detection).
   - Candlestick pattern recognition (for entry confirmation).
   - Optional: ATR for dynamic stop-loss adjustment and volatility filtering.

8. **Timeframe & Market Suitability:**
   - Best on higher timeframes (4H, Daily) for cleaner trends and more reliable Fibonacci levels.
   - Works well in trending markets (forex majors, indices, crypto in strong trends).
   - Avoid ranging/choppy markets where Ichimoku signals whipsaw and Fibonacci levels fail.

**Summary:** FiboIchimoku waits for Ichimoku to establish a clear trend, then uses Fibonacci retracements to enter on pullbacks at discounted prices, maximizing risk-reward while filtering false signals through multi-indicator confluence.
