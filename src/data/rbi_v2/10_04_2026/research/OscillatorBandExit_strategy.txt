STRATEGY_NAME: OscillatorBandExit

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is specifically designed as a LONG EXIT mechanism (not an entry strategy). It combines MACD momentum confirmation with Bollinger Band overbought detection to identify optimal exit points for existing long positions, then waits for price confirmation before finalizing the exit.

═══════════════════════════════════════════
1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════

A) MACD Bullish Crossover Trigger
   - MACD Line (typically 12-period EMA minus 26-period EMA)
   - Signal Line (9-period EMA of MACD Line)
   - Trigger condition: MACD Line crosses ABOVE Signal Line (bullish crossover)
   - Note: This may seem counterintuitive for an exit, but the logic is that a fresh bullish crossover often marks the FINAL thrust of an uptrend — a momentum exhaustion signal that frequently precedes a pullback.

B) Bollinger Band Touch Condition
   - Upper Band = SMA(20) + (2 × Standard Deviation of 20 periods)
   - Trigger condition: Price (high or close) touches or exceeds the Upper Bollinger Band
   - This signals the asset is statistically overextended (typically 2 standard deviations above mean)

C) Coincidence Requirement (Dual Confirmation)
   - Both conditions must occur simultaneously or within a small tolerance window (e.g., same bar or within 1-3 bars)
   - This dual confirmation filters out false signals from either indicator alone

D) Subsequent Pullback Exit
   - The strategy does NOT exit immediately on the signal
   - Instead, it ARMS an exit trigger and waits for a pullback
   - Pullback definition: price closes below the previous bar's low, or price crosses back below a short-term EMA (e.g., 5-period), or a bearish candle confirmation

═══════════════════════════════════════════
2. ENTRY / EXIT RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════

ENTRY (assumed prerequisite):
   - A long position must already be open (this strategy only manages exits)

EXIT SEQUENCE:
   Step 1 — Arm the exit signal:
      IF MACD Line crosses above Signal Line
      AND Price touches/exceeds Upper Bollinger Band
      THEN set exit_armed = TRUE (do not exit yet)

   Step 2 — Wait for pullback confirmation:
      IF exit_armed = TRUE
      AND Price closes below previous bar's low (or below 5-EMA)
      THEN EXIT long position at market

   Step 3 — Invalidation:
      IF exit_armed = TRUE
      AND price makes a new high AND MACD begins to roll over (MACD histogram declining for 2+ bars)
      THEN exit immediately (momentum exhaustion confirmed)
      OR reset exit_armed = FALSE if conditions no longer valid after N bars (e.g., 5 bars)

═══════════════════════════════════════════
3. RISK MANAGEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════

- Trailing Stop: Maintain an ATR-based trailing stop (e.g., 2× ATR) to protect profits if the pullback never materializes
- Time Stop: If exit_armed remains TRUE for more than 10 bars without pullback confirmation, exit at market to avoid giving back gains
- Partial Exit Option: Consider scaling out 50% on the initial signal and 50% on pullback confirmation
- Invalidation Stop: If price breaks significantly above the upper band with strong momentum (large bullish candle + rising MACD histogram), cancel the exit and let the trade run with a tightened trailing stop

═══════════════════════════════════════════
4. REQUIRED INDICATORS
═══════════════════════════════════════════

- MACD (12, 26, 9) — both MACD Line and Signal Line
- Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations)
- Optional: 5-period EMA for pullback confirmation
- Optional: ATR (14) for trailing stop placement
- Optional: MACD Histogram for momentum exhaustion detection

═══════════════════════════════════════════
5. LOGIC RATIONALE
═══════════════════════════════════════════

The strategy exploits the phenomenon where a bullish MACD crossover occurring AT the upper Bollinger Band often represents the climax of a rally — momentum is still bullish (hence the crossover) but price is statistically overextended. Rather than exiting at the peak (which is risky to time), the strategy waits for the first sign of weakness (pullback) to confirm the top is in, capturing near-optimal exit prices while avoiding premature exits during strong trends.
