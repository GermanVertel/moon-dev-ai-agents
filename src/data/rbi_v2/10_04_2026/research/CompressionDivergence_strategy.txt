STRATEGY_NAME: CompressionDivergence

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
A volatility-contraction breakout system that fuses dual moving-average trend filtering, Bollinger/Keltner squeeze detection, and RSI divergence confirmation to trade high-probability expansions out of consolidation ranges.

---

1. CORE CONCEPT

Markets cycle between expansion (trending) and contraction (consolidation). This strategy waits for volatility to compress into a tight range, then uses RSI divergence as an early warning that the compression is resolving in a specific direction. The breakout is only taken when price escapes the range with momentum confirmation, filtering out false breaks.

The edge comes from three stacked conditions:
- Volatility is compressed (low-probability of chop, high-probability of expansion)
- Momentum is diverging (smart money is positioning before the break)
- Trend filter agrees (we only trade in the direction of the higher-timeframe bias)

---

2. REQUIRED INDICATORS

- EMA 20 (fast trend)
- EMA 50 (slow trend / bias)
- EMA 200 (macro bias, optional higher TF)
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0) — for range envelope
- Keltner Channels (20, 1.5 ATR) — for squeeze detection
- ATR (14) — for volatility measurement and stops
- RSI (14) — for divergence detection
- Volume (20-period average) — for breakout confirmation

---

3. CONSOLIDATION / SQUEEZE DETECTION

A "compression regime" is active when ALL of the following are true:
- Bollinger Bands sit INSIDE the Keltner Channels (classic TTM Squeeze condition)
- ATR(14) is below its 20-period average (volatility is contracting)
- EMA 20 and EMA 50 are within 0.5 × ATR of each other (trend is flat)
- Price has made at least 5 consecutive closes inside the Bollinger envelope without a close beyond the outer band

Mark the compression window and track its duration. Longer squeezes (10+ bars) tend to produce more explosive breakouts.

---

4. RSI DIVERGENCE CONFIRMATION

While the compression is active, scan for divergence on the RSI(14):

Bullish Divergence (bias = long breakout):
- Price prints a lower low inside the range
- RSI prints a higher low at the same swing
- Divergence must form within the last 15 bars of the squeeze

Bearish Divergence (bias = short breakout):
- Price prints a higher high inside the range
- RSI prints a lower high at the same swing

Only trade in the direction the divergence points. This is the "pre-positioning" signal that separates real breaks from fakeouts.

---

5. TREND FILTER

Directional alignment rule:
- For long breakouts: EMA 20 > EMA 50 OR price above EMA 200 on the higher timeframe
- For short breakouts: EMA 20 < EMA 50 OR price below EMA 200 on the higher timeframe

If divergence and trend filter conflict, skip the trade.

---

6. ENTRY RULES

Long Entry:
1. Compression regime is active
2. Bullish RSI divergence detected
3. Trend filter agrees (bullish bias)
4. Price closes ABOVE the upper Bollinger Band
5. Breakout candle volume > 1.5 × 20-period average volume
6. Enter on the close of the breakout candle (or on the retest of the upper band)

Short Entry:
Mirror of the above with bearish divergence, bearish bias, and a close below the lower Bollinger Band.

Optional refinement: enter on the first pullback to the broken band or EMA 20 rather than the breakout candle itself — this reduces slippage and improves R:R.

---

7. EXIT RULES

Initial Stop Loss:
- Long: below the lower Bollinger Band OR entry − 1.5 × ATR(14), whichever is tighter
- Short: above the upper Bollinger Band OR entry + 1.5 × ATR(14)

Profit Targets:
- Target 1 (scale 50%): 2 × ATR from entry
- Target 2 (runner): trail using EMA 20 on the closing basis, or 3 × ATR, whichever is hit first

Trailing Logic:
- Once price moves 1 × ATR in favor, move stop to breakeven
- Once price moves 2 × ATR in favor, trail stop with EMA 20 (long) or EMA 20 (short)

Time Stop:
- If the trade does not reach Target 1 within 15 bars, exit at market. Compression breakouts should resolve quickly.

---

8. RISK MANAGEMENT

- Risk per trade: 0.5% – 1% of account equity
- Position size = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Entry − Stop)
- Max concurrent positions: 2
- Max daily loss: 2R — stop trading for the session
- Avoid trading during major news events (FOMC, NFP, CPI) unless the breakout occurs after the release
- Do not take counter-trend breakouts even if divergence is strong — the trend filter is non-negotiable

---

9. HIGH-PROBABILITY FILTERS (CONFLUENCE SCORING)

Score each setup 0–5 and only trade if score ≥ 4:
- +1 Squeeze duration ≥ 10 bars
- +1 RSI divergence forms within 5 bars of the breakout
- +1 Volume expansion ≥ 2× average
- +1 EMA 20/50 alignment with breakout direction
- +1 Breakout candle closes in top/bottom 25% of its range (strong close)

---

10. WHEN TO STAND ASIDE

- Compression is active but no divergence has formed
- Divergence exists but price is still mid-range (no breakout close)
- Volume on the breakout is below average
- ATR is expanding without a clean range (chop, not compression)
- Higher-timeframe trend is directly opposing the divergence direction

---

SUMMARY: CompressionDivergence is a volatility-regime strategy — it only fires when the market has coiled, momentum has diverged, and price escapes the range with volume. This combination targets the highest-probability breakout windows while systematically avoiding the low-volatility chop that kills most breakout traders.
