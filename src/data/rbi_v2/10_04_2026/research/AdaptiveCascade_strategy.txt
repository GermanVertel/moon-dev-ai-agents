STRATEGY_NAME: AdaptiveCascade

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is an adaptive, multi-timeframe cascade system that layers trend confirmation, momentum acceleration, and volatility-adjusted risk controls. It is designed to avoid whipsaws by requiring alignment across fast, medium, and slow structural signals before entering, then scaling in only when the market proves continuation.

1. Key strategy components
- Multi-timeframe trend alignment: Use a fast EMA, a medium EMA, and a slow EMA on the primary chart. A valid long bias requires fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA; a valid short bias requires fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA.
- Momentum confirmation: Use RSI or MACD histogram to confirm that momentum is expanding in the direction of the trend, not merely positive or negative.
- Volatility filter: Use ATR to define whether the market is in a tradeable expansion phase. Avoid entries when ATR is below its moving average, signaling compression and false-break risk.
- Cascade entry logic: Enter only after a pullback or consolidation resolves in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend, then add to the position if price makes a new structure high/low while momentum and volatility remain supportive.
- Market regime filter: Optional higher-timeframe trend filter, such as a 200 EMA or daily bias, to avoid fighting the dominant trend.

2. Entry rules
Long entry:
- Fast EMA crosses above medium EMA, and medium EMA is above slow EMA.
- RSI is above 50 and rising, or MACD histogram is positive and increasing.
- ATR is above its 20-period average, confirming expansion.
- Price closes above the recent swing high or breaks a short consolidation range.
- Enter on the breakout candle close or on the first retest of the breakout level.

Short entry:
- Fast EMA crosses below medium EMA, and medium EMA is below slow EMA.
- RSI is below 50 and falling, or MACD histogram is negative and decreasing.
- ATR is above its 20-period average.
- Price closes below the recent swing low or breaks a short consolidation range.
- Enter on the breakdown candle close or on the first retest of the breakdown level.

3. Exit rules
- Initial stop loss: Place stop beyond the most recent swing point or at 1.5x to 2.0x ATR from entry, whichever is wider.
- Trailing stop: After price moves 1R in favor, trail by the fast EMA or by 1.5x ATR.
- Profit target: Use a partial exit at 2R, then let the remainder run with the trailing stop.
- Trend invalidation exit: Exit if fast EMA crosses back through medium EMA against the position, or if momentum diverges sharply from price.
- Time stop: If price does not move at least 1R in favor within a predefined number of bars, reduce or close the position.

4. Risk management
- Risk per trade: 0.5% to 1.0% of account equity.
- Position sizing: Calculate size from the distance between entry and stop loss using ATR and account risk.
- Maximum exposure: Limit total open risk to 3% to 5% of equity across all positions.
- Correlation control: Avoid multiple simultaneous entries in highly correlated markets.
- Cascade scaling: Add only when the trade is already in profit and the trend structure remains intact; never average down.
- Volatility adjustment: Reduce size when ATR is unusually high, and avoid trading during major news events unless the setup is exceptionally strong.

5. Required indicators
- Fast EMA, medium EMA, and slow EMA for trend alignment.
- RSI or MACD for momentum confirmation.
- ATR and ATR moving average for volatility filtering and stop placement.
- Higher-timeframe EMA for regime bias.
- Swing high/low detection for breakout and retest entries.

This AdaptiveCascade approach is unique because it combines layered trend alignment, momentum acceleration, volatility expansion, and staged position scaling into a single adaptive framework. It is not a simple trend follower; it is a regime-aware cascade system that only engages when multiple independent signals confirm continuation.
