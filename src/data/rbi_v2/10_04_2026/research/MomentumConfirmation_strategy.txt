STRATEGY_NAME: MomentumConfirmation

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines RSI momentum signals with ADX trend-strength confirmation to enter long positions during strong uptrends, using divergence and ADX weakening as exit triggers.

**Key Strategy Components:**
1. **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** — Used as the primary momentum/entry trigger. Notably, this strategy treats RSI overbought readings (typically >70) as a *bullish continuation signal* rather than a reversal warning, which aligns with momentum-trading philosophy (strength begets strength).
2. **ADX (Average Directional Index)** — Used as the trend-strength filter and confidence gauge. Only enter when ADX confirms a strong uptrend (typically ADX > 25, and +DI > -DI).
3. **Divergence Detection** — Bearish RSI divergence (price making higher highs while RSI makes lower highs) serves as an exit signal.
4. **ADX Decline** — A falling ADX indicates waning trend strength and triggers exit.

**Entry Rules (Long):**
- RSI crosses above the overbought threshold (e.g., 70) OR remains sustained above it, signaling strong bullish momentum.
- ADX is above 25 and rising, confirming a strong, strengthening uptrend.
- +DI is above -DI (directional confirmation of bullish bias).
- Optional: Enter on the RSI cross above 70 while ADX > 25, rather than chasing extended overbought readings.

**Exit Rules:**
- Exit when bearish RSI divergence is detected (price higher high, RSI lower high).
- Exit when ADX begins to decline (e.g., ADX turns down or falls below its recent peak / below 25), indicating weakening trend confidence.
- Optional hard stop: Exit if RSI drops back below 50 or a fixed ATR/percentage stop is hit.

**Risk Management:**
- Use an ATR-based or percentage stop-loss below the entry or recent swing low.
- Consider trailing stops tied to ADX decline to lock in profits as momentum fades.
- Position sizing based on volatility (ATR) to normalize risk across trades.
- Avoid entries when ADX is flat or below 25, even if RSI is overbought (avoids choppy, range-bound false signals).

**Required Indicators:**
- RSI (period 14 recommended)
- ADX with +DI and -DI (period 14 recommended)
- Optional: ATR for stops and position sizing; price action for divergence confirmation

**Notes & Caveats:**
- This is a momentum-continuation strategy, so it performs best in trending markets and poorly in ranging/choppy conditions.
- The interpretation of "overbought RSI" as a long entry is counter to classic mean-reversion logic — it relies on ADX to filter out the cases where overbought RSI precedes a reversal.
- Divergence signals can be subjective; define them programmatically (e.g., using pivot highs on price and RSI) to avoid ambiguity.
