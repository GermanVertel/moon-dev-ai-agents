STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityContraction

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
This strategy exploits the tension between momentum (MACD) and volatility structure (Bollinger Bands). It targets long entries when bullish momentum emerges during a volatility expansion phase within a broader downtrend, betting on a sharp mean-reversion rally. The exit is mechanical and volatility-based, using the lower band as a trailing stop.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **MACD (Momentum Filter)**
   - Standard settings: 12, 26, 9
   - "Bullish" condition: MACD line crosses above the signal line, OR MACD histogram turns positive
   - This confirms short-term upward momentum is building

2. **Bollinger Bands (Volatility & Trend Context)**
   - Standard settings: 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations
   - **Downtrend phase:** Middle band (20 SMA) is sloping downward
   - **Expanding width:** Band width (upper - lower) is increasing compared to prior bars — signals rising volatility
   - This combination identifies a falling market entering a high-volatility regime

3. **Entry Logic (Long)**
   - All must be true simultaneously:
     - MACD bullish crossover or histogram flipped positive
     - Bollinger middle band sloping down
     - Bollinger band width expanding for 2+ consecutive bars
   - Enter long at next bar open after signal confirmation

4. **Exit Logic**
   - Exit long when price closes below the lower Bollinger Band
   - This acts as a volatility-adjusted trailing stop — as bands expand downward, the stop widens; as price recovers, the lower band trails up

**Entry/Exit Rules Summary:**
- **Entry:** MACD bullish + BB downtrend + BB width expanding → Buy
- **Exit:** Close below lower Bollinger Band → Sell
- **No short entries** in this variant (long-only mean-reversion)

**Risk Management:**
- Position sizing based on ATR or fixed % of equity (1–2% risk per trade)
- Hard stop-loss optional: place at 1.5× ATR below entry as a safety net in case the lower band is far away
- Avoid entries during low-liquidity sessions or major news events that can cause gap-throughs
- Max concurrent positions: 1–2 to avoid correlated exposure
- Re-entry filter: wait for a new MACD bullish cross before re-entering after a stop-out

**Required Indicators:**
- MACD (12, 26, 9) — line, signal, histogram
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) — upper, middle, lower, width
- Optional: ATR(14) for stop sizing and volatility confirmation

**Best Market Conditions:**
- Choppy or sharply declining markets where volatility is expanding
- Works best on liquid instruments (index futures, large-cap equities, major FX pairs)
- Timeframes: 15m to 4H for intraday/swing; avoid very low timeframes due to noise

**Potential Weaknesses:**
- "Catching a falling knife" — MACD can flip bullish briefly during strong downtrends
- Expanding bands can continue expanding, causing whipsaws
- Requires strict discipline on the lower-band exit to avoid holding through further downside
