STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityZone

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines volatility regime detection with dynamic support/resistance zone mapping to trade reversals at zone boundaries and confirmed breaks through zones. The core idea is that volatility expansion/contraction determines whether price is likely to respect a zone (reversal) or slice through it (breakout).

---

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS

A. Volatility Regime Detection
- Use ATR (Average True Range, 14-period) as the primary volatility gauge.
- Compute a volatility ratio: ATR(14) / SMA(ATR(14), 50).
- Regimes:
  - Compression: ratio < 0.85 → zones are strong, expect reversals.
  - Normal: 0.85 ≤ ratio ≤ 1.15 → trade both reversals and breaks with normal size.
  - Expansion: ratio > 1.15 → zones are weak, favor breakouts and trend continuation.
- Optionally confirm with Bollinger Band Width (BBW = (Upper − Lower) / Middle) to validate squeeze/expansion phases.

B. Dynamic Support & Resistance Zone Construction
- Identify swing highs/lows using a fractal lookback (e.g., 5 bars left/right).
- Cluster nearby swing points within an ATR-based tolerance (e.g., 0.5 × ATR) into a single zone.
- Each zone has:
  - Center price (average of clustered swings)
  - Zone width (max − min of clustered pivots, capped at 1.0 × ATR)
  - Strength score = number of touches + recency weight + volume at touches
- Keep only the top 3–5 zones by strength on each side of current price.

C. Trend Context Filter
- Use EMA(50) vs EMA(200) for macro trend bias.
- Use EMA(20) slope for short-term momentum.
- This determines whether we prefer long or short setups at each zone.

---

2. ENTRY RULES

A. Reversal Entries (Compression / Normal Regime)
Long reversal:
- Price enters a support zone from above.
- Rejection candle forms: bullish engulfing, hammer, or pin bar with wick piercing the zone.
- Volatility ratio < 1.15 (not in strong expansion).
- RSI(14) < 35 or bullish divergence vs. prior swing low.
- Enter on close of the rejection candle or on break of its high.

Short reversal:
- Price enters a resistance zone from below.
- Bearish rejection candle (shooting star, bearish engulfing, pin bar).
- Volatility ratio < 1.15.
- RSI(14) > 65 or bearish divergence.
- Enter on close of the rejection candle or on break of its low.

B. Breakout Entries (Expansion / Normal Regime)
Long breakout:
- Price closes decisively above a resistance zone (close > zone high + 0.25 × ATR).
- Volatility ratio > 1.15 OR BBW expanding from a squeeze.
- Volume > 1.5 × 20-period average volume.
- Enter on the breakout close or on the first pullback that holds the zone as new support.

Short breakout:
- Price closes below a support zone (close < zone low − 0.25 × ATR).
- Volatility ratio > 1.15 OR BBW expanding.
- Volume confirmation.
- Enter on breakout close or retest that fails to reclaim the zone.

---

3. EXIT RULES

A. Reversal Trades
- Take Profit 1: next opposing zone or 1.5 × ATR, whichever comes first.
- Take Profit 2: 2.5 × ATR or prior swing extreme.
- Stop Loss: just beyond the zone (zone extreme ± 0.5 × ATR).
- Move to breakeven once price reaches 1 × ATR in profit.

B. Breakout Trades
- Take Profit 1: measured move = zone width projected from breakout point, or 2 × ATR.
- Take Profit 2: next major zone in the breakout direction.
- Stop Loss: back inside the broken zone (zone midpoint or zone far edge).
- Trail stop using 1.5 × ATR once trade is 2 × ATR in profit.

C. Universal Exit Triggers
- Volatility regime flip: if expansion turns to compression against the trade, tighten stops.
- Time stop: exit if trade hasn't reached TP1 within 20 bars.
- Zone invalidation: if price re-enters and closes beyond the zone against the position, exit immediately.

---

4. RISK MANAGEMENT

- Risk per trade: 0.5%–1.0% of account equity.
- Position size = (Account × Risk%) / (Entry − Stop distance).
- Maximum concurrent positions: 3, with no more than 2 in the same direction.
- Correlation cap: avoid multiple positions in correlated instruments simultaneously.
- Daily loss limit: stop trading after −2% daily drawdown.
- Weekly loss limit: reduce size by 50% after −4% weekly drawdown.
- No new entries within 15 minutes of major scheduled news (CPI, FOMC, NFP) when in compression regime.

---

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS

- ATR(14) and SMA(ATR(14), 50) → volatility regime ratio
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) → BBW for squeeze/expansion confirmation
- EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200) → trend and momentum context
- RSI(14) → reversal confirmation and divergence detection
- Volume SMA(20) → breakout validation
- Fractal/Swing pivot detector (5-bar lookback) → zone construction
- Optional: VWAP for intraday zone weighting

---

6. EXECUTION NOTES

- Timeframes: 1H and 4H for swing trading; 15m for intraday with same logic.
- Best instruments: liquid FX majors, index futures, large-cap crypto (BTC/ETH).
- Backtest across all three volatility regimes separately to validate edge.
- Track win rate by regime: reversals should dominate in compression, breakouts in expansion.
- Continuously re-rank zones after each new swing forms; drop zones older than 200 bars unless untested and strong.

This framework ensures entries are always aligned with the prevailing volatility state, using support/resistance zones as the decision points for either fading or following price action.
