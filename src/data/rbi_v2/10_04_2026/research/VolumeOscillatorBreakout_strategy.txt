STRATEGY_NAME: Volume Oscillator Breakout

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines volume-based oversold detection with a price breakout trigger to catch reversal moves after capitulation-style selling. The core idea is that extreme volume spikes paired with oversold oscillator readings often mark exhaustion points, and the subsequent breakout above a short-term resistance confirms the reversal.

1. Key Strategy Components
- Volume Oversold Oscillator: A composite indicator that flags when volume surges to an extreme (e.g., volume > 2x its 20-period average) while a momentum oscillator (RSI or stochastic) is in oversold territory (e.g., RSI < 30). This dual condition identifies capitulation.
- Confirmation Breakout: After the oversold-volume signal, wait for price to break above a short-term resistance level (e.g., the high of the signal bar or a 5-period high).
- Trend Filter: Optional longer-term moving average (e.g., 200-period EMA) to avoid counter-trend trades in strong downtrends.

2. Entry Rules
- Long Entry: 
  a) Volume > 2x 20-period average volume AND RSI(14) < 30 (or stochastic < 20).
  b) Price closes above the high of the signal bar or above a 5-period high within the next 3 bars.
  c) Optional: Price above 200-period EMA for trend alignment.
- Short Entry (mirror):
  a) Volume > 2x average AND RSI(14) > 70 (or stochastic > 80).
  b) Price closes below the low of the signal bar or below a 5-period low.

3. Exit Rules
- Profit Target: 1.5x to 2x ATR(14) from entry, or a fixed risk-reward ratio of 2:1.
- Stop Loss: Below the signal bar low (for longs) or above the signal bar high (for shorts), or 1x ATR.
- Trailing Stop: Optional trailing stop using ATR or a moving average (e.g., 10-period EMA) to lock in profits.
- Time Exit: Close position if no target/stop hit within 10 bars.

4. Risk Management
- Position Sizing: Risk no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade.
- Max Concurrent Trades: Limit to 2-3 open positions to avoid overexposure.
- Avoid News Events: Skip signals around major earnings or economic releases to reduce gap risk.
- Volatility Filter: Avoid trades when ATR is extremely low (choppy markets) or extremely high (unstable).

5. Required Indicators
- Volume (with 20-period simple moving average)
- RSI(14) or Stochastic(14,3,3)
- ATR(14) for stops and targets
- 200-period EMA (optional trend filter)
- 5-period high/low for breakout confirmation

This strategy is best suited for liquid markets (stocks, forex majors, crypto) on intraday to daily timeframes, where volume data is reliable and oversold reversals tend to follow through.
