STRATEGY_NAME: DivergentOscillator

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines a momentum filter with a mean-reversion entry trigger, using RSI divergence against a longer-term moving average, and exits based on CCI directional bias.

**Core Components:**

1. **Entry Signal (Long Only):**
   - Short-term RSI (default 14-period) must be below 30 (oversold).
   - Simultaneously, price must be positioned such that the RSI is diverging from the 50-period MA in the opposite direction.
   - Interpretation: While RSI is deeply oversold, the 50-period MA slope/position suggests underlying bullish momentum (e.g., price above the 50-MA, or MA rising). This creates a bullish divergence between the oscillator (weak) and the trend filter (strong).
   - Enter long when both conditions align.

2. **Exit Signal:**
   - Monitor the CCI (Commodity Channel Index, default 20-period).
   - Exit the long position when CCI's directional bias flips bearish, i.e., CCI crosses below zero or below a defined threshold (e.g., -100), indicating momentum exhaustion.

**Entry Rules (Long):**
- RSI(14) < 30
- Price > 50-period MA (or 50-MA slope > 0) — divergence condition
- Optional confirmation: RSI turning upward (RSI[t] > RSI[t-1]) to avoid catching a falling knife.
- Enter at next bar open or market.

**Exit Rules:**
- CCI(20) crosses below 0 (or below -100 for stricter exit).
- Alternatively, exit when CCI directional bias turns negative (CCI slope negative for N bars).
- Optional stop-loss: fixed % or ATR-based (e.g., 1.5x ATR below entry).

**Risk Management:**
- Position sizing based on fixed fractional risk (e.g., 1-2% of equity per trade).
- Stop-loss placed below recent swing low or 1.5x ATR.
- Take-profit optional but can be tied to CCI reaching overbought (+100) or RSI > 70.
- Avoid trading during low-liquidity sessions.

**Required Indicators:**
- RSI (period 14)
- 50-period Simple or Exponential Moving Average
- CCI (period 20)
- Optional: ATR (period 14) for stops, volume filter

**Logic Rationale:**
The strategy seeks to exploit temporary oversold conditions (RSI < 30) that occur within an otherwise bullish trend (price above 50-MA). This divergence suggests a pullback within an uptrend, offering a high-probability long entry. The CCI exit ensures the position is closed when momentum shifts against the trade, locking in gains or cutting losses before a full reversal.
