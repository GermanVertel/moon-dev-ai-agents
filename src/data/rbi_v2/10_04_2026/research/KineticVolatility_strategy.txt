STRATEGY_NAME: KineticVolatility

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy is a volatility-adaptive trading system that adjusts position sizing, entry thresholds, and stop placement based on real-time changes in market volatility. It is designed for liquid instruments (e.g., futures, FX majors, large-cap equities) and works best on intraday to swing timeframes (5m–4h).

1. Key Strategy Components
- Volatility Regime Detection:
  - Use ATR(14) and Bollinger Band Width (BBW = (Upper−Lower)/Middle, 20-period, 2σ) to classify volatility into Low, Normal, High, and Extreme regimes.
  - Optional: realized volatility (standard deviation of log returns over 20–50 bars) for confirmation.
- Adaptive Band Construction:
  - Build dynamic bands around a short EMA (e.g., EMA(20)) using a multiple of ATR that scales with the regime.
  - In Low volatility: tighter bands (e.g., 1.0×ATR) to catch early breakouts.
  - In High volatility: wider bands (e.g., 2.0–2.5×ATR) to avoid noise.
- Momentum Confirmation:
  - Use RSI(14) or MACD histogram to confirm direction and avoid false breakouts.
  - Require slope alignment: EMA(20) slope positive for longs, negative for shorts.
- Volume/Spread Filter:
  - Require volume > 1.2× 20-period average and spread within normal bounds to avoid illiquid conditions.

2. Entry Rules
- Long Entry:
  - Price closes above upper adaptive band.
  - RSI(14) > 55 and rising, or MACD histogram > 0 and increasing.
  - EMA(20) slope > 0.
  - Volatility regime is Normal or High (avoid Extreme unless using reduced size).
- Short Entry:
  - Price closes below lower adaptive band.
  - RSI(14) < 45 and falling, or MACD histogram < 0 and decreasing.
  - EMA(20) slope < 0.
  - Volatility regime is Normal or High.
- Optional Mean-Reversion Variant (Low volatility only):
  - Enter counter-trend when price touches outer band and RSI shows divergence, targeting the middle band.

3. Exit Rules
- Profit Targets:
  - Target 1: 1.0×ATR from entry (scale out 50%).
  - Target 2: 2.0×ATR from entry or opposite adaptive band, whichever comes first.
- Trailing Stop:
  - Trail using 1.5×ATR from the highest high (longs) or lowest low (shorts) after Target 1 is hit.
- Time Stop:
  - Exit if trade does not reach Target 1 within N bars (e.g., 20 bars) and momentum stalls.
- Volatility Exit:
  - If volatility regime shifts to Extreme, tighten stops to 0.75×ATR and reduce size.

4. Risk Management
- Position Sizing:
  - Risk per trade = 0.5%–1.0% of equity.
  - Size = (Equity × Risk%) / (Stop Distance in price × Point Value).
  - Reduce size by 50% in High volatility and by 75% in Extreme volatility.
- Stop Loss:
  - Initial stop = entry ∓ 1.5×ATR (longs: entry − 1.5×ATR).
  - Never widen a stop; only tighten.
- Max Exposure:
  - Limit concurrent positions to 2–3 in correlated instruments.
  - Daily loss limit: 3% of equity; weekly: 6%.
- Slippage/Commission Buffer:
  - Add 0.1×ATR to stop distance for high-volatility instruments.

5. Required Indicators
- ATR(14) — volatility measurement and stop/target sizing.
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2) — band width and regime detection.
- EMA(20) — trend filter and adaptive band center.
- RSI(14) or MACD(12,26,9) — momentum confirmation.
- Volume MA(20) — liquidity filter.
- Optional: Realized Volatility (20–50 bars) and VIX/IV rank for regime context.

6. Execution Notes
- Best sessions: London/New York overlap for FX; first 2 hours for equities.
- Avoid major news releases unless trading the post-news volatility expansion with reduced size.
- Backtest across Low/Normal/High regimes separately; optimize ATR multipliers per regime, not globally.
- Walk-forward test to ensure regime classification remains stable out-of-sample.

This KineticVolatility approach is unique because it treats volatility not as a static parameter but as a dynamic state variable that simultaneously governs entry sensitivity, stop distance, target placement, and position size.
