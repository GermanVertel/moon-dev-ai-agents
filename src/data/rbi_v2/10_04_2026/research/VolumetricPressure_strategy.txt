STRATEGY_NAME: VolumetricPressure

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
The VolumetricPressure strategy is a momentum-based breakout system that identifies high-probability trade entries by confirming price breakouts with significant volume expansion. The core premise is that genuine breakouts—moves that sustain and lead to extended trends—are almost always accompanied by a surge in trading volume, which signals strong conviction from institutional or large market participants. Conversely, price breakouts on low volume are often false signals that quickly reverse.

This strategy combines price action, volume analysis, and volatility measurement to filter out noise and capture explosive moves. It is suitable for liquid markets (stocks, forex, crypto, futures) on intraday or swing timeframes.

KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS:

1. **Price Breakout Detection**: The strategy identifies when price closes above a recent resistance level (for long entries) or below a recent support level (for short entries). A common approach is to use the highest high or lowest low of the last N bars (e.g., 20 bars) or a Donchian Channel.

2. **Volume Confirmation**: The breakout must be accompanied by a volume surge. This is measured by comparing the current bar's volume to a moving average of volume (e.g., 20-period SMA of volume). A typical threshold is that the breakout bar's volume must be at least 1.5x to 2x the average volume. This ensures the move has genuine participation.

3. **Volatility Filter**: To avoid breakouts during choppy, low-volatility consolidation, the strategy may require the Average True Range (ATR) to be above a certain level, or it may use a volatility contraction pattern (e.g., Bollinger Band squeeze) as a precursor to the breakout.

4. **Trend Alignment (Optional)**: For higher probability, the breakout should align with the broader trend. For example, only take long breakouts when price is above a 200-period EMA, and short breakouts when below.

ENTRY RULES:

**Long Entry**:
- Price closes above the highest high of the last 20 bars (or upper Donchian Channel).
- Volume on the breakout bar is > 1.5x the 20-period average volume.
- (Optional) Price is above the 200-period EMA.
- Enter at the close of the breakout bar or on a stop order above the breakout level.

**Short Entry**:
- Price closes below the lowest low of the last 20 bars (or lower Donchian Channel).
- Volume on the breakout bar is > 1.5x the 20-period average volume.
- (Optional) Price is below the 200-period EMA.
- Enter at the close of the breakout bar or on a stop order below the breakout level.

EXIT RULES:

**Profit Target**: 
- Use a trailing stop based on ATR (e.g., 2x ATR trailing stop) to let winners run.
- Alternatively, exit at a fixed risk-reward ratio (e.g., 2:1 or 3:1) or at a measured move (e.g., the height of the prior consolidation projected from the breakout point).

**Stop Loss**:
- Initial stop loss placed at the opposite side of the breakout range (e.g., for a long breakout, place stop just below the breakout level or the low of the breakout bar).
- A common method is to use 1.5x ATR below the entry for longs, or above for shorts.

**Time-Based Exit**: 
- If the trade does not move in favor within a certain number of bars (e.g., 10 bars), consider exiting to avoid dead trades.

RISK MANAGEMENT:

- **Position Sizing**: Risk no more than 1-2% of account equity per trade. Calculate position size based on the distance between entry and stop loss.
- **Maximum Daily Loss**: Limit daily losses to 3-5% of equity to prevent overtrading or revenge trading.
- **Correlation Risk**: Avoid taking multiple breakouts in highly correlated instruments simultaneously.
- **Slippage & Liquidity**: Ensure the market has sufficient liquidity to avoid slippage on entry/exit, especially during high-volume breakouts.
- **False Breakout Protection**: Consider requiring a close beyond the breakout level (not just an intrabar spike) and volume confirmation to reduce false signals.

REQUIRED INDICATORS:

1. **Volume Moving Average**: 20-period SMA of volume (to measure volume surge).
2. **Donchian Channel** or **Highest High/Lowest Low**: 20-period (to identify breakout levels).
3. **Average True Range (ATR)**: 14-period (for stop loss and trailing stop).
4. **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**: 200-period (optional trend filter).
5. **Bollinger Bands** (optional): To identify volatility squeeze before breakout.

ADDITIONAL NOTES:

- The strategy performs best in trending markets and can suffer in ranging markets. Consider adding a market regime filter (e.g., ADX > 25 for trending).
- Backtesting should include realistic transaction costs and slippage, as breakout strategies are sensitive to execution.
- Optimize parameters (lookback period, volume multiplier, ATR multiplier) on a walk-forward basis to avoid curve-fitting.
- The VolumetricPressure strategy can be automated using platforms like TradingView (Pine Script), MetaTrader (MQL), or Python (Backtrader, Zipline).
