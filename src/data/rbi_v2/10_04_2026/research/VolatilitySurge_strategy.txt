STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilitySurge

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
This strategy exploits moments when Bollinger Band width (a proxy for statistical volatility) expands beyond the market's realized range volatility (ATR). When band spread exceeds ATR, it signals a statistically significant volatility expansion — often coinciding with the onset of a directional momentum move. The strategy captures that momentum by going long, then exits when RSI signals overbought exhaustion or momentum reversal.

---

**1. Key Strategy Components**

- **Bollinger Bands (BB):** Period = 20, Standard Deviations = 2 (default). Upper Band (UB), Lower Band (LB), Basis (SMA 20).
- **Band Spread / Width:** `BB_Width = UB - LB` (absolute price difference between bands, not normalized).
- **Average True Range (ATR):** Period = 14 (default).
- **RSI:** Period = 50 bars (unusually long, smoothing out noise to capture macro momentum shifts).
- **Long-only bias** per the strategy description.

---

**2. Entry Rules (Long)**

Enter a long position when **all** of the following are true:

1. `BB_Width > ATR` — the band spread exceeds the average true range, indicating a volatility surge / expansion regime.
2. Price is trading **above the Bollinger Basis (SMA 20)** — confirms bullish directional bias within the expansion.
3. (Recommended filter) Price closed **above the Upper Band** on the signal bar — confirms breakout momentum, not just a wide band.
4. Optional confirmation: `RSI(50) > 50` — ensures the long-term momentum is bullish before entry.

**Entry Execution:** Market order at the close of the signal bar (or next bar open for realism).

---

**3. Exit Rules**

Exit the long position when **either** condition triggers:

1. **RSI(50) Crossover Down:** `RSI(50)` crosses below its prior value / below the 50 midline (or a defined threshold such as 70 → 50 crossover). The 50-bar RSI is slow, so this exit avoids premature exits but catches sustained momentum reversals.
2. **RSI(50) Crossover Up into overbought then back down** — if using a threshold model, exit on the first bearish crossover after an overbought reading.
3. **Stop-loss:** ATR-based stop (e.g., `Entry − 1.5 × ATR`) to cap downside if volatility collapses.
4. **Take-profit (optional):** Fixed R multiple (e.g., 2R) or when BB_Width contracts back below ATR.

---

**4. Risk Management**

- **Position sizing:** Risk a fixed % of equity per trade (e.g., 1–2%). Size = `(Equity × Risk%) / (Entry − Stop)`.
- **Stop-loss:** 1.5× ATR below entry (dynamic, volatility-adjusted).
- **Max exposure:** Limit to one open long at a time; no pyramiding unless specified.
- **Regime filter:** Only trade when `BB_Width > ATR` — avoid chop when bands are narrow.
- **Time stop:** Exit if trade hasn't reached target within N bars (e.g., 20) and momentum stalls.
- **Slippage/commission:** Model realistic costs, especially during volatility surges.

---

**5. Required Indicators**

| Indicator | Parameters | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Bollinger Bands | 20 period, 2 SD | Volatility band width |
| ATR | 14 period | Realized range benchmark |
| RSI | 50 period | Momentum exit signal |
| SMA | 20 period | Trend/bias filter (BB basis) |

---

**6. Practical Notes**

- The **50-bar RSI** is intentionally slow — it acts as a trend-following exit rather than a fast oscillator, reducing whipsaws.
- The **BB_Width > ATR** condition is the strategy's signature: it distinguishes genuine volatility expansion from quiet consolidation.
- Backtest across trending vs. ranging regimes; the strategy should underperform in choppy markets and excel in breakout trends.
- Consider adding a **volume confirmation** (e.g., volume > 1.5× average) to validate the volatility surge.

---

**Summary:** VolatilitySurge is a long-only, volatility-expansion momentum strategy that enters when Bollinger Band width exceeds ATR and exits via slow RSI(50) crossovers, with ATR-based stops for disciplined risk control.
