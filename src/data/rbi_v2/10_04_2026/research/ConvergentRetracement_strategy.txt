STRATEGY_NAME: ConvergentRetracement

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Fibonacci retracement levels, Bollinger Band compression, and support/resistance confirmation to identify high-probability reversal entries after a trend has paused. The core idea is that when price retraces into a key Fibonacci level while volatility contracts (Bollinger Bands narrowing), a breakout or reversal is imminent — and confirmation at a support/resistance zone provides the trigger.

─────────────────────────────
1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
─────────────────────────────

A. Trend Identification
- Establish the prevailing trend using a higher timeframe (e.g., 4H or Daily).
- Use a 50-period EMA and 200-period EMA:
  • Uptrend: Price above both EMAs, 50 EMA > 200 EMA.
  • Downtrend: Price below both EMAs, 50 EMA < 200 EMA.
- Only take long setups in uptrends and short setups in downtrends (or allow counter-trend with reduced size).

B. Fibonacci Retracement Mapping
- Identify the most recent significant swing high and swing low in the direction of the trend.
- Draw Fibonacci retracement from swing low → swing high (uptrend) or swing high → swing low (downtrend).
- Key zones to watch: 0.382, 0.500, 0.618, and 0.786.
- The 0.618–0.786 "golden pocket" is the highest-probability reversal zone.

C. Bollinger Band Narrowing (Squeeze)
- Bollinger Bands: 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations.
- Measure Band Width = (Upper Band − Lower Band) / Middle Band.
- "Narrowing range" condition: current Band Width is in the lowest 20–30% of its range over the last 50–100 bars.
- Optional: Use Bollinger Band Width Percentile (BBWP) < 25 to confirm squeeze.
- A squeeze signals energy compression — often precedes an explosive move.

D. Support/Resistance Confirmation
- Mark horizontal S/R levels from prior swing highs/lows, round numbers, or high-volume nodes.
- A valid setup requires the Fibonacci level to CONVERGE with a known S/R zone (within 0.5–1.0 ATR).
- Confirmation candles: bullish/bearish engulfing, pin bar, hammer/shooting star, or a close back above/below the S/R level.

─────────────────────────────
2. ENTRY RULES
─────────────────────────────

LONG ENTRY (in uptrend):
1. Price retraces into 0.382–0.786 Fibonacci zone.
2. Fibonacci level aligns with a prior support level (confluence).
3. Bollinger Bands are in a squeeze (BBWP < 25 or Band Width in bottom 25%).
4. Confirmation: bullish reversal candle closes above the S/R + Fib level.
5. Enter on the close of the confirmation candle or on a break of its high.

SHORT ENTRY (in downtrend):
1. Price retraces up into 0.382–0.786 Fibonacci zone.
2. Fibonacci level aligns with a prior resistance level.
3. Bollinger Bands are in a squeeze.
4. Confirmation: bearish reversal candle closes below the S/R + Fib level.
5. Enter on the close of the confirmation candle or on a break of its low.

Optional enhancement: Wait for a Bollinger Band expansion (price closes outside the band) AFTER the squeeze to confirm the direction of the breakout.

─────────────────────────────
3. EXIT RULES
─────────────────────────────

Take Profit:
- TP1: Previous swing high (longs) / swing low (shorts) — scale out 50%.
- TP2: 1.272 or 1.618 Fibonacci extension of the retracement leg.
- Trailing stop: Use 1.5× ATR or trail below the 20-period EMA.

Stop Loss:
- Long: Below the 0.786 Fib level or below the most recent swing low, whichever is tighter, plus a 0.5× ATR buffer.
- Short: Above the 0.786 Fib level or above the most recent swing high, plus a 0.5× ATR buffer.

Time-based exit:
- If price does not move in favor within 10–15 bars after entry, close the position (squeeze setups can fail if momentum doesn't materialize).

─────────────────────────────
4. RISK MANAGEMENT
─────────────────────────────

- Risk per trade: 0.5%–1% of account equity.
- Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop Loss distance).
- Minimum R:R of 1:2 required; skip setups offering less.
- Max concurrent positions: 3.
- Avoid trading during major news events (FOMC, NFP, CPI) unless already in profit.
- Reduce size by 50% if the setup is counter-trend.
- Daily loss limit: 3% of equity → stop trading for the day.

─────────────────────────────
5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
─────────────────────────────

- Exponential Moving Averages: 50 EMA, 200 EMA (trend filter).
- Bollinger Bands: 20-period, 2 SD (squeeze detection).
- Bollinger Band Width / BBWP (volatility compression measurement).
- Fibonacci Retracement tool (0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786; extensions 1.272, 1.618).
- ATR (14) for stop placement and position sizing.
- Volume (optional but recommended) — look for declining volume during the squeeze and rising volume on breakout.
- Support/Resistance zones (manually marked or via pivot points).

─────────────────────────────
6. MARKET CONDITIONS & FILTERS
─────────────────────────────

- Best on liquid instruments: major FX pairs, index futures (ES, NQ), large-cap stocks, BTC/ETH.
- Works best on 1H–4H timeframes for swing entries.
- Avoid ranging markets with no clear swing structure — the Fib anchor becomes ambiguous.
- Skip if Bollinger Bands are already wide (no squeeze) — the edge comes from compression.

─────────────────────────────
7. SUMMARY LOGIC
─────────────────────────────

Trend (EMA) + Retracement (Fibonacci) + Compression (Bollinger squeeze) + Confirmation (S/R candle) = High-probability entry with tight risk.

The convergence of three independent signals (Fib level, S/R zone, and volatility squeeze) drastically reduces false signals compared to using any one indicator alone, while the confirmation candle ensures you enter only after the market agrees with your level.
