STRATEGY_NAME: ChikouVolatility

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines the Chikou Span (Lagging Span) from the Ichimoku Kinko Hyo system with Bollinger Bands to create a trend-following system that anticipates reversals through lagging price comparison and volatility expansion/contraction.

CORE COMPONENTS:

1. **Chikou Span (Lagging Span)**
   - Plot current close price shifted back 26 periods
   - Compare Chikou Span against historical price action 26 periods ago
   - Bullish signal: Chikou Span breaks above price from 26 periods ago
   - Bearish signal: Chikou Span breaks below price from 26 periods ago
   - This confirms trend direction by comparing current momentum to past structure

2. **Bollinger Bands (20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations)**
   - Upper Band, Middle Band (SMA20), Lower Band
   - Bandwidth = (Upper - Lower) / Middle — measures volatility
   - Squeeze: bandwidth at multi-period lows (consolidation)
   - Expansion: bandwidth rising sharply (breakout confirmation)

ENTRY RULES:

**Long Entry:**
- Chikou Span crosses above the price candle from 26 periods ago
- Price closes above the Middle Band (SMA20)
- Bollinger Bandwidth is expanding from a recent squeeze
- Confirmation: Price closes above Upper Band on breakout candle

**Short Entry:**
- Chikou Span crosses below the price candle from 26 periods ago
- Price closes below the Middle Band (SMA20)
- Bollinger Bandwidth is expanding from a recent squeeze
- Confirmation: Price closes below Lower Band on breakdown candle

EXIT RULES:

**Long Exit:**
- Price closes back below Middle Band, OR
- Chikou Span crosses back below historical price, OR
- Price touches Lower Band (trailing stop hit)

**Short Exit:**
- Price closes back above Middle Band, OR
- Chikou Span crosses back above historical price, OR
- Price touches Upper Band (trailing stop hit)

RISK MANAGEMENT:

- **Position Sizing:** Risk 1-2% of account per trade
- **Stop Loss (Long):** Below the Lower Band or recent swing low, whichever is tighter
- **Stop Loss (Short):** Above the Upper Band or recent swing high, whichever is tighter
- **Take Profit:** Trail using Middle Band as dynamic stop; partial profit at 2x ATR
- **Avoid trading during Bollinger Squeeze** — wait for expansion confirmation
- **Avoid counter-trend trades** when Chikou Span is strongly displaced (>2x ATR from price)
- **Maximum 2 concurrent positions** to limit correlated exposure

REQUIRED INDICATORS:

1. Chikou Span (Close shifted -26)
2. Bollinger Bands (Length: 20, StdDev: 2.0)
3. Bollinger Bandwidth (for squeeze detection)
4. ATR (14-period) for stop placement and volatility filter
5. Optional: 200-period EMA for higher timeframe trend bias

TIMEFRAMES:
- Best on 4H and Daily charts for reliable Chikou signals
- Avoid timeframes below 1H (noise distorts 26-period lag)
- Multi-timeframe confirmation: align with Daily trend direction

MARKET CONDITIONS:
- Thrives in trending markets with periodic volatility expansion
- Poor performance in tight ranges where Chikou gives false signals
- Filter out low-bandwidth periods (squeeze) until breakout confirms

The synergy: Chikou Span validates trend direction using historical price memory, while Bollinger Bands provide the volatility trigger and dynamic support/resistance for entries, exits, and stops.
