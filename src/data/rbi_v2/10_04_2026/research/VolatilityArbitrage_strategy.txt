STRATEGY_NAME: VolatilityArbitrage

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

This strategy exploits temporary price discrepancies between implied volatility (IV) and realized volatility (RV) across correlated instruments, using volume metrics as a confirmation filter. The core premise: when IV diverges significantly from RV while volume surges, a mean-reversion arbitrage opportunity exists between volatility-based instruments (options, VIX futures, volatility ETFs) and their underlying assets.

---

**1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS**

- **Volatility Spread Signal**: Compute the spread between Implied Volatility (IV) and Realized Volatility (RV) for each instrument.  
  - IV: Derived from options pricing (e.g., 30-day ATM implied vol) or VIX/VIX-equivalent.  
  - RV: 20-period annualized standard deviation of log returns.  
  - Spread = IV − RV. Extreme positive spreads suggest overpriced options (sell vol); extreme negative spreads suggest underpriced options (buy vol).

- **Volume Confirmation**: Use relative volume (current volume / 20-period average volume) to validate the signal.  
  - High relative volume (>1.5x) on a divergence indicates institutional participation and increases arbitrage reliability.  
  - Low volume divergences are ignored to avoid false signals.

- **Cross-Instrument Arbitrage Legs**:  
  - **Leg A (Volatility Instrument)**: Options straddle/strangle, VIX futures, or volatility ETFs (e.g., VXX, UVXY).  
  - **Leg B (Underlying/Correlated Asset)**: The underlying equity, index, or a correlated asset.  
  - Trade the spread between the two when volatility measures diverge.

- **Regime Filter**: Only trade when the volatility term structure is in contango or backwardation consistently — avoid chaotic regimes (e.g., VIX spikes >2 standard deviations).

---

**2. ENTRY RULES**

**Long Volatility Arbitrage (Buy Vol, Sell Underlying):**
- IV − RV < −0.05 (IV significantly below RV, options underpriced).
- Relative volume > 1.5x on the volatility instrument.
- VIX term structure in backwardation (near-term > long-term).
- Enter: Buy ATM straddle (or VIX futures long), short the underlying (or use delta hedge).

**Short Volatility Arbitrage (Sell Vol, Buy Underlying):**
- IV − RV > +0.05 (IV significantly above RV, options overpriced).
- Relative volume > 1.5x on the volatility instrument.
- VIX term structure in contango (near-term < long-term).
- Enter: Sell ATM straddle (or VIX futures short), long the underlying (or delta hedge).

**Volume Confirmation Rule**: Only enter if the volume spike occurs within the same bar or the prior bar of the signal.

---

**3. EXIT RULES**

- **Profit Target**: Close when IV − RV spread reverts to within ±0.01 of zero (mean reversion complete).
- **Stop Loss**: Exit if the spread widens by an additional 0.03 against the position (e.g., for short vol, if IV − RV > 0.08).
- **Time Stop**: Exit after 10 trading days if no reversion occurs (avoid theta decay and regime shifts).
- **Volume Exit**: Exit if relative volume drops below 0.5x (liquidity drying up).
- **Delta Neutrality**: Rebalance delta hedge daily if the underlying moves >1.5% from entry.

---

**4. REQUIRED INDICATORS**

- **Implied Volatility (IV)**: 30-day ATM implied vol from options chain or VIX.
- **Realized Volatility (RV)**: 20-period annualized standard deviation of log returns.
- **Relative Volume**: Current volume / 20-period simple moving average of volume.
- **VIX Term Structure**: VIX (near-term) vs. VIX3M (3-month) for contango/backwardation.
- **ATR (Average True Range)**: 14-period ATR for position sizing and stop placement.
- **Delta**: Options delta for hedging the underlying leg.

---

**5. RISK MANAGEMENT**

- **Position Sizing**: Risk no more than 1% of portfolio equity per arbitrage trade.  
  - Size = (0.01 × Equity) / (ATR × Contract Multiplier).
- **Max Exposure**: Limit total volatility arbitrage exposure to 5% of portfolio.
- **Correlation Check**: Only trade pairs with historical correlation > 0.7 (e.g., SPY vs. VIX, QQQ vs. VXN).
- **Event Filter**: Avoid earnings, FOMC, or CPI releases unless the trade is explicitly event-driven.
- **Hedge Ratio**: Maintain delta-neutral or beta-neutral exposure using the underlying leg.
- **Liquidity Filter**: Only trade instruments with average daily volume > 500,000 shares/contracts.
- **Drawdown Limit**: Stop trading the strategy for 5 days if drawdown exceeds 3% of allocated capital.

---

**6. EXECUTION NOTES**

- Use limit orders to avoid slippage on the volatility instrument leg.
- Monitor the spread in real-time; volatility arbitrage windows are often short-lived (minutes to hours).
- Backtest across multiple regimes (2017 low-vol, 2020 COVID spike, 2022 rate hikes) to validate robustness.
- Consider transaction costs (options spreads, futures roll costs) — arbitrage edges are thin.

---

This strategy capitalizes on the statistical tendency of IV and RV to converge, using volume as a confirmation signal to filter out noise and low-conviction setups. The dual-leg structure (volatility instrument + underlying) ensures market-neutral exposure while isolating the volatility mispricing.
