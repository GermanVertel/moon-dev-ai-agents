STRATEGY_NAME: FibonacciMomentum

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines Fibonacci retracement levels with momentum confirmation to identify high-probability trend continuation entries. The core idea is that Fibonacci levels alone can produce false signals, so momentum must validate the retracement completion before entering.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Identify a clear directional impulse move (swing high to swing low for downtrend, or swing low to swing high for uptrend).
- Plot Fibonacci retracement levels on that impulse move: 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, and 78.6%.
- Wait for price to pull back into a key Fibonacci zone (typically 38.2%–61.8%, the "golden pocket").
- Confirm that momentum is rising (for longs) or falling (for shorts) as price approaches or touches the retracement level.
- Enter only when both conditions align: retracement level reached AND momentum confirmation present.

2. ENTRY RULES
Long Entry:
- Prior impulse move is bullish (swing low → swing high).
- Price retraces down into 38.2%–61.8% zone.
- Momentum indicator (e.g., RSI, MACD histogram, or Stochastic) is rising or turning up from oversold/neutral.
- Enter on bullish candlestick confirmation (engulfing, pin bar, or close above prior candle high) at or near the Fibonacci level.

Short Entry:
- Prior impulse move is bearish (swing high → swing low).
- Price retraces up into 38.2%–61.8% zone.
- Momentum indicator is falling or turning down from overbought/neutral.
- Enter on bearish candlestick confirmation at or near the Fibonacci level.

3. EXIT RULES
- Take Profit 1: Prior swing high (for longs) or swing low (for shorts) — the 0% retracement level.
- Take Profit 2: Fibonacci extension levels (127.2%, 161.8%) for extended moves.
- Stop Loss: Below the 78.6% retracement level (for longs) or above it (for shorts), or below the most recent swing low/high.
- Optional trailing stop using momentum reversal or a moving average (e.g., 20 EMA).

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
- Position size = (Account Risk) / (Entry Price − Stop Loss Price).
- Only take trades where reward-to-risk ratio is at least 1:2.
- Avoid trading during major news events or low-liquidity sessions.
- Skip setups where momentum and price action conflict (e.g., price at Fibonacci level but momentum still strongly trending against the trade).

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Fibonacci retracement tool (manual or automated swing detection).
- Momentum oscillator: RSI (14), MACD (12,26,9), or Stochastic (14,3,3).
- Candlestick pattern recognition for entry confirmation.
- Optional: 20 EMA or 50 EMA for trend bias and trailing stops.
- Volume indicator to confirm momentum strength (rising volume on impulse, falling volume on retracement).

6. TIMEFRAME AND MARKET CONTEXT
- Best suited for trending markets (stocks, forex, crypto, futures).
- Recommended timeframes: 1H, 4H, and Daily for reliability.
- Confirm higher timeframe trend direction before taking lower timeframe entries (multi-timeframe alignment).

This strategy reduces false Fibonacci signals by requiring momentum confirmation, improving entry timing and overall win rate.
