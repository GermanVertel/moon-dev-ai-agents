STRATEGY_NAME: BollingerBandMomentum

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy combines trend-following momentum with Bollinger Band structure to trade both mean-reversion touches and momentum breakouts. It uses the Bollinger Bands (typically 20-period SMA with 2 standard deviations) as dynamic support/resistance zones, entering long on upper-band touches and shorting on lower-band breaks.

CORE COMPONENTS:
1. Bollinger Bands indicator (period 20, std dev 2.0)
   - Upper Band = SMA(20) + 2 * StdDev(20)
   - Lower Band = SMA(20) - 2 * StdDev(20)
   - Middle Band (basis) = SMA(20)
2. Momentum confirmation filter (e.g., RSI or MACD histogram)
3. Trend filter (e.g., 200-period EMA) to align direction

ENTRY RULES:

LONG ENTRY (trend-following touch):
- Price action touches or pierces the UPPER Bollinger Band
- Momentum indicator confirms bullish bias (RSI > 50 or MACD histogram > 0)
- Overall trend filter is bullish (price above 200 EMA)
- Enter long on close of the candle that touches the upper band, or on the next candle's breakout above the touch candle's high

SHORT ENTRY (momentum break):
- Price breaks ABOVE the LOWER Bollinger Band's moving average midpoint (i.e., price crosses above the middle band from below in a downtrend context) — interpreted as a failed rally / continuation short
- Momentum indicator confirms bearish bias (RSI < 50 or MACD histogram < 0)
- Overall trend filter is bearish (price below 200 EMA)
- Enter short on close of the break candle or on the next candle's break below the prior candle's low

EXIT RULES:
- Long exits: Close below the middle band (SMA 20) OR momentum flips bearish OR stop-loss hit
- Short exits: Close above the middle band OR momentum flips bullish OR stop-loss hit
- Optional trailing exit: ATR-based trailing stop (2x ATR) to lock in trend gains

RISK MANAGEMENT:
1. Position sizing: Risk no more than 1-2% of equity per trade
2. Stop-loss placement:
   - Long: Below the lower Bollinger Band or 1.5x ATR below entry
   - Short: Above the upper Bollinger Band or 1.5x ATR above entry
3. Take-profit: 2:1 or 3:1 reward-to-risk, or trail with middle band
4. Avoid trading during low-volatility squeeze unless a breakout is confirmed
5. Maximum concurrent positions and daily loss limits enforced

REQUIRED INDICATORS:
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0)
- 200-period EMA (trend filter)
- RSI (14) or MACD (12, 26, 9) for momentum confirmation
- ATR (14) for stop-loss and volatility sizing

TIMEFRAME & MARKET NOTES:
- Best suited for trending markets (equities, FX majors, crypto in directional phases)
- Recommended timeframes: 1H, 4H, or Daily to reduce noise
- Avoid ranging/choppy markets where band touches produce false signals

CAVEATS:
The described logic mixes two distinct concepts — band-touch mean reversion (long on upper band) and momentum breakout shorts (break above lower band midpoint). This is internally inconsistent with classic Bollinger theory (upper band touch usually signals overbought/mean-reversion short, not long). Traders should backtest carefully and clarify whether the intent is:
(a) trend-continuation entries where upper band touch confirms strength, or
(b) reversal entries where upper band touch signals exhaustion.
Validate with historical data before deploying capital.
