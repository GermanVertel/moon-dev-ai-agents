STRATEGY_NAME: FractalVolumeArb

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy exploits the divergence between price fractal geometry and volume-momentum confirmation to identify arbitrage windows where price structure and participation flow are temporarily misaligned. The core premise is that fractal patterns (self-similar price swings) are only valid when corroborated by volume-momentum; when they diverge, a high-probability reversion/continuation trade emerges.

1. KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS
- Fractal Detection: Identify Williams-style fractals (5-bar swing highs/lows) on the primary timeframe. A bullish fractal = middle bar has the lowest low with two higher lows on each side; bearish fractal = middle bar has the highest high with two lower highs on each side.
- Volume-Momentum Engine: Compute a volume-weighted momentum oscillator (e.g., Volume-Weighted RSI or a normalized OBV slope over N bars) to measure the strength of participation behind each fractal.
- Divergence Arbitrage: Compare fractal direction against volume-momentum direction. 
  - Bullish fractal + falling volume-momentum = "hidden bullish arbitrage" (accumulation).
  - Bearish fractal + rising volume-momentum = "hidden bearish arbitrage" (distribution).
- Multi-Timeframe Confirmation: Validate fractals on a higher timeframe (e.g., 4H) while executing on a lower timeframe (e.g., 15m) to filter noise.

2. ENTRY RULES
- Long Entry: A confirmed bullish fractal forms, price closes above the fractal high, AND volume-momentum shows positive divergence (momentum higher while price made a lower low). Enter on the close of the confirmation candle.
- Short Entry: A confirmed bearish fractal forms, price closes below the fractal low, AND volume-momentum shows negative divergence (momentum lower while price made a higher high). Enter on the close of the confirmation candle.
- Optional Confluence: Require price to be at a Fibonacci retracement zone (0.5–0.618) of the prior impulse for higher win rate.

3. EXIT RULES
- Profit Target: Next opposing fractal level, or 1.5×–2× the ATR(14) measured from entry.
- Stop Loss: Placed just beyond the fractal's extreme (below the bullish fractal low for longs, above the bearish fractal high for shorts) plus a small ATR buffer (0.25× ATR).
- Trailing Exit: Trail stop using the most recent opposite fractal once price moves 1× ATR in favor.
- Time Exit: Close position if volume-momentum flips against the trade for two consecutive bars.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
- Position Sizing: Risk 0.5–1% of equity per trade, calculated from the distance between entry and stop.
- Max Concurrent Positions: 2–3 to avoid correlated exposure.
- Volatility Filter: Skip trades when ATR is below the 20th percentile (dead market) or above the 95th percentile (news spike).
- Correlation Check: Avoid stacking longs/shorts in highly correlated instruments.
- Daily Loss Limit: Stop trading after 3 consecutive losses or -2% daily drawdown.

5. REQUIRED INDICATORS
- Williams Fractals (5-bar) — primary pattern detector.
- Volume-Weighted RSI (VW-RSI) or OBV Slope — momentum confirmation.
- ATR(14) — volatility and stop sizing.
- Volume MA(20) — filter for above-average participation.
- Fibonacci Retracement tool — optional confluence zone.
- Higher-timeframe fractal overlay — trend/context filter.

6. EXECUTION NOTES
- Best suited for liquid instruments (FX majors, index futures, large-cap crypto) where volume data is reliable.
- Avoid during low-liquidity sessions (Asian lunch, holidays).
- Backtest across at least 500 trades to validate the divergence edge; expect win rate ~45–55% with reward:risk ≥ 1.8.
- Combine with a regime filter (e.g., ADX > 20) to trade only in trending or expanding-volatility environments.
