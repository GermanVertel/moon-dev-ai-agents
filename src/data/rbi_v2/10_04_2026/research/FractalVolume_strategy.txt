STRATEGY_NAME: FractalVolume

STRATEGY_DETAILS:

**Core Concept:**
This strategy identifies high-probability breakouts by combining fractal geometry with volume confirmation. Fractal patterns (Bill Williams-style 5-bar fractals) mark local swing highs/lows, and breakouts above/below these fractal levels are only valid when accompanied by a significant volume surge. This filters out low-conviction breakouts that often fail.

**Key Strategy Components:**

1. **Fractal Identification**
   - Bullish Fractal: A 5-bar pattern where the middle bar has the lowest low, flanked by two higher lows on each side.
   - Bearish Fractal: A 5-bar pattern where the middle bar has the highest high, flanked by two lower highs on each side.
   - Fractals are confirmed only after the 5th bar closes (2 bars of lag).

2. **Volume Baseline**
   - Calculate a rolling Volume Moving Average (e.g., 20-period VMA).
   - Define a "Volume Spike" as current volume > 1.5x to 2.0x the VMA.
   - Optionally use Volume Profile to identify high-liquidity zones.

3. **Breakout Trigger**
   - **Long Entry:** Price closes above the most recent confirmed bearish fractal high AND volume spike condition is met.
   - **Short Entry:** Price closes below the most recent confirmed bullish fractal low AND volume spike condition is met.

**Entry Rules:**
- Wait for a confirmed fractal (5 bars complete).
- Monitor price action for a close beyond the fractal level.
- Confirm with volume spike (current bar volume ≥ 1.5 × 20-period VMA).
- Enter on the next bar open or on the breakout bar close.

**Exit Rules:**
- **Stop Loss:** Place below the most recent bullish fractal low (for longs) or above the most recent bearish fractal high (for shorts).
- **Take Profit Options:**
  - Fixed R:R (e.g., 2:1 or 3:1).
  - Trailing stop using ATR (e.g., 2× ATR).
  - Exit on opposite fractal formation or volume exhaustion.

**Risk Management:**
- Risk no more than 1–2% of account equity per trade.
- Position size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry − Stop Loss).
- Avoid trading during low-volume sessions (e.g., Asian session for US equities).
- Skip trades when fractals cluster tightly (choppy market) — require minimum fractal spacing (e.g., 10 bars apart).

**Required Indicators:**
- Fractal indicator (5-bar Williams Fractal).
- Volume bars + Volume Moving Average (20-period).
- ATR (14-period) for dynamic stops/targets.
- Optional: Volume Profile / VWAP for confluence.

**Best Timeframes:**
- Intraday: 15m–1H for momentum breakouts.
- Swing: 4H–Daily for higher reliability.

**Strengths:**
- Volume confirmation significantly reduces false breakouts.
- Fractals provide objective, rule-based structure levels.
- Works across trending and breakout regimes.

**Weaknesses:**
- Fractals lag by 2 bars — entries may miss the earliest move.
- Requires liquid markets with reliable volume data.
- Choppy markets generate frequent invalid signals.
