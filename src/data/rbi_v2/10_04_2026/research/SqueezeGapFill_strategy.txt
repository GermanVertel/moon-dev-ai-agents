STRATEGY_NAME: SqueezeGapFill

STRATEGY_DETAILS:
This strategy exploits short-squeeze dynamics combined with options-flow sentiment to capture upside gap-fill moves. It targets heavily shorted names with bullish options positioning that are trading at discounted price levels, betting on forced covering and mean reversion toward prior gaps.

KEY STRATEGY COMPONENTS:

1. Universe Screening (Short Interest Coverage)
- Screen for stocks with Days-to-Cover (DTC) ratio ≥ 5 days (high short interest relative to average daily volume)
- Short interest as % of float ≥ 15% (heavily shorted)
- Focus on names that have recently gapped DOWN and are trading near lower price levels (below 20-day or 50-day moving average)
- Market cap filter: $500M–$10B (liquid enough to trade, small enough for squeezes)

2. Options Sentiment Confirmation (Put-Call Ratio)
- Put-Call Ratio (PCR) on volume: between 0.5 and 0.8 (bullish tilt — more call buying than puts)
- Put-Call Ratio on open interest: declining trend over last 5 sessions (shorts covering hedges)
- Look for elevated call volume relative to 20-day average (≥ 1.5x) as confirmation of bullish positioning
- Optional: rising implied volatility skew toward calls

3. Price Level / Gap-Down Setup
- Identify a recent unfilled gap-down (price gapped below prior close by ≥ 3% within last 10 trading days)
- Stock currently trading in lower 25% of its 52-week range
- RSI(14) between 25–40 (oversold but not dead-cat territory)
- Price stabilizing above a recent swing low (higher low formation)

ENTRY RULES:
- Enter long when ALL of the following align:
  a) DTC ≥ 5 and short float ≥ 15%
  b) PCR (volume) ≤ 0.8 and declining
  c) Price closes above the prior day's high (confirmation of reversal)
  d) Volume on entry day ≥ 1.5x 20-day average volume
- Stagger entries: 50% initial position on signal, add 50% on breakout above the gap-fill midpoint

EXIT RULES:
- Primary target: Gap-fill level (prior gap-down origin) — typically 10–20% upside
- Scale out: 50% at +10%, remaining 50% at gap-fill or +20%
- Trailing stop: Activate after +8% gain, trail by 5% (ATR-based)
- Time stop: Exit if no progress toward target within 15 trading days

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Hard stop-loss: -7% from entry (or below the reversal-day low, whichever is tighter)
- Position sizing: Risk no more than 1% of portfolio per trade
- Max 3 concurrent positions (squeeze trades are volatile and correlated)
- Avoid earnings dates within 5 days of entry unless catalyst is intentional
- Reduce size by 50% if VIX > 30 (broad market stress)
- Monitor borrow rates: if short borrow fee spikes > 50%, expect squeeze acceleration (favorable) but also higher volatility

REQUIRED INDICATORS:
- Days-to-Cover (DTC) and Short % of Float
- Put-Call Ratio (volume and open interest, 5-day and 20-day)
- Relative Volume (current vs 20-day average)
- RSI(14)
- ATR(14) for trailing stops and volatility sizing
- 20/50-day SMAs for trend/price-level context
- 52-week high/low for range positioning
- Gap detection (prior close vs current open)

MARKET CONTEXT FILTERS:
- Best performance in neutral-to-bullish broad market (SPY above 50-day SMA)
- Avoid during high-correlation risk-off events (credit spreads widening, VIX > 35)
- Sector preference: small-cap growth, biotech, and high-beta tech where squeezes are most common

EXPECTED PERFORMANCE PROFILE:
- Win rate: ~45–55% (asymmetric payoff driven)
- Average winner: +12–18%
- Average loser: -6–7%
- Reward-to-risk: ~2:1 or better
- Holding period: 3–15 trading days

KEY RISKS:
- Squeezes can reverse violently; strict stops are essential
- Low-float names may have poor liquidity and wide spreads
- False PCR signals during earnings or macro events
- Gap-downs sometimes reflect fundamental deterioration (avoid "value traps")
